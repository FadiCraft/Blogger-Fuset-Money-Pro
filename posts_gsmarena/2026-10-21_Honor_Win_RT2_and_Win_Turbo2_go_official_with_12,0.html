Honor has unveiled its Win2 series in China, consisting of the Win2, Win RT2, and Win Turbo2. In this post, we’ll focus on the Win RT2 and Win Turbo2, both of which pack massive 12,070mAh batteries.

The Honor Win RT2 is powered by the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 chipset, paired with LPDDR5X Ultra RAM and UFS 4.1 storage. It also gets in-house display and communication chipsets.

The Win RT2 also comes with an in-built active cooling fan that sits within the rear camera island. According to Honor, the fan can reduce temperatures by up to 6 degrees Celsius under load. Meanwhile, the software also offers 4 levels of fan speed adjustment.

At the front, the phone features a 6.89-inch AMOLED display that offers a 2K resolution, a 185Hz refresh rate, and a peak brightness of 10,000 nits.

For photography, the Win RT2 gets a 200MP main rear camera with CIPA 6.0 stabilization and a 12MP ultrawide unit. Selfies and video calls are handled by a 50MP front camera.

The 12,070mAh battery supports 90W wired and 27W wired reverse charging. The phone runs Android 17-based MagicOS 11 out-of-the-box.

The Honor Win Turbo2 also gets the same display and battery features, but is equipped with the less powerful Snapdragon 8s Gen 4 chipset.

It also doesn’t get an active cooling fan and has a 50MP Sony main sensor at the back, coupled with a 12MP ultrawide camera. For selfies, the phone offers a 50MP unit.

Both smartphones offer an ultrasonic under-display fingerprint scanner, IP69K rating, 5G connectivity, Wi-Fi 7, Bluetooth 6.0, NFC, and GPS.

The Honor Win RT2 and Win Turbo2 are available in three color options and are currently on sale in China.

As for pricing, the Win RT2 starts at CNY 4,199 ($625) for the base 12GB/256GB option with the top-end 16GB/512GB variant carrying a price tag of CNY 5,199 ($775).

Meanwhile, the Win Turbo2 is priced at CNY 3,199 ($480) for the 8GB/256GB model, whereas the 16GB/512GB option is sold at CNY 4,499 ($670).
