A few days ago, tipster Smart Pikachu suggested that the Magic10 series might mark the return of variable-aperture cameras on Honor smartphones. Now, a new Weibo post by the tipster suggests the Magic10 series might feature a primary camera with a large 200MP sensor and LOFIC tech.

In a separate post, the tipster said Honor is evaluating tablets and smartphones with dual-layer OLED. This post included #HonorMagic10, meaning the Magic10 series could sport a dual-layer OLED display.

However, while responding to a comment on this post, the tipster ruled out the possibility of all flagship smartphones coming with a dual-layer OLED display in 2027.

Honor Magic9 Pro Max

If Honor really launches a smartphone with a 200MP primary camera having LOFIC tech, it could be the Magic10 Pro Max, successor to the Magic9 Pro Max unveiled in September. Its key features include the Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6 SoC, a 200MP primary camera, a 200MP 3.5x periscope telephoto camera, and a 50MP ultrawide camera. All three rear cameras benefit from ARRI image science and feature ARRI color profiles and cinematic video filters.

The Magic9 Pro Max's selfie camera uses a 55MP sensor, joined by a ToF 3D sensor. Our Honor Magic9 Pro Max review is in progress. In the meantime, check out some pictures we took with it in LA.
