Ahead of their August 18 launch in China, iQOO has revealed the chipsets powering the Neo11 Ultra and Z11s. The company had previously shared the design and battery specifications of both smartphones.

The iQOO Neo11 Ultra will be powered by a custom Dimensity 9500M chipset, which features an all-big-core CPU architecture. Built on a 3nm process, iQOO says that the chipset was developed specifically for the upcoming smartphone.

iQOO claims the custom chipset will deliver “top-tier performance and power efficiency” thanks to the Monster Hyper-Core engine.

In another Weibo post, the company also revealed that the iQOO Z11s will be equipped with the MediaTek Dimensity 7500 chipset.

iQOO has also confirmed several other features of the Neo11 Ultra. The handset will run OriginOS 6 and offer IP68 and IP69 ratings. It will also feature an AMOLED display using F2 luminescent material, which is claimed to deliver up to 2,000 nits of full-screen peak brightness.

The Neo11 Ultra will pack a 9,100mAh battery, while the iQOO Z11s will come with a larger 10,000mAh cell.
