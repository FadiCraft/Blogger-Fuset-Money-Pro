Samsung has introduced No Cost EMI financing for buyers of its latest foldables in India. Eligible customers can spread the cost over up to 18 months without paying interest or making a down payment, as Samsung covers the financing charges.

The Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra starts at INR 11,111 per month for 18 months (INR 199,999 for the 12/256GB model). It's available in Violet Shadow, Graphite, Cream, and the online-exclusive Green Shadow, with 12/512GB and 16GB/1TB versions also on offer.

The Galaxy Z Fold8 starts at INR 9,999.94 per month (INR 179,999 for the 12/256GB model) and comes in Lavender, Graphite, Cream, and the online-exclusive Pistachio. Storage options include 12/512GB and 16GB/1TB.

The Galaxy Z Flip8 starts at INR 6,944.39 per month for 18 months (INR 124,999 for the 12/256GB model). Buyers can also opt for a 12/512GB version, while color choices include Pink, Graphite, Cream, and the online-exclusive Mint.

Unlike some other markets, Samsung isn't bundling free storage upgrades with the new foldables in India. Considering all three models cost more than their predecessors, prospective buyers may have hoped for a similar launch incentive.
