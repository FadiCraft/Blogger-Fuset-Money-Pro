Last week, we reported that Oppo would unveil the Reno17 series today, but it turns out that it was incorrect. The company has now officially confirmed the lineup's launch date in China.

Oppo’s Reno17 series will go official in China on November 16, the company confirmed in a Weibo post. The brand will also unveil the Oppo Watch 5 and the Oppo Pad Air 6.

Alongside revealing the launch date, Oppo also shared the design and color options for the upcoming Reno17 and Reno17 Pro. Both phones are now also available for pre-order in the country, after blind reservations went live last week.

The Oppo Reno17 and Reno17 Pro retain a design similar to their predecessors, the Reno16 and Reno16 Pro. Both phones feature a square rear camera module and a flat display with slim, uniform bezels.

The phones will be available in Adventure Cat, Cocoa Brown, First Snow White, and Ice Moon White color options.

Oppo has yet to reveal the specifications of the phones, but recent leaks suggest that the Reno17 will feature a 6.5-inch display, a Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 chipset, and an 8,000mAh battery.

The Reno17 Pro, meanwhile, is tipped to feature a smaller 6.3-inch display, a 7,200mAh battery, and a Dimensity 8550 chipset.

As mentioned earlier, Oppo will also launch the Watch 5 and Pad Air 6 alongside the Reno17 series next week.

The Oppo Watch 5 is confirmed to measure 9.2mm in thickness and weigh just 25g. It is claimed to offer up to 7 days of battery life and support features such as sleep and menstrual tracking.

The smartwatch will be available in multiple color options and is already up for pre-order online.

The Oppo Pad Air 6 will feature a 12.1-inch LCD with a 2.8K resolution and a 120Hz refresh rate. It will pack a 10,050mAh battery and feature 8MP cameras on both the front and back. The tablet is powered by the Dimensity 7360 Super SoC and runs ColorOS 17.

It will be available in two colors, with buyers also getting the choice of a matte display. The tablet will be offered with up to 12GB of RAM and 256GB of storage, in both Wi-Fi and LTE variants.
