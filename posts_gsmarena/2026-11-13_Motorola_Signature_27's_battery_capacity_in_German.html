Motorola introduced the Signature 27 in September and fully unveiled it last week. The smartphone packs a 7,000 mAh battery. However, Motorola initially said it would come with a 5,500 mAh battery in Germany. Thankfully, that's not the case.

That confirmation comes straight from Motorola Germany, which has updated the Signature 27's product page on its website to confirm the smartphone will pack a 7,000 mAh battery in Germany, just like other regions.

The rest of the specs remain the same, meaning customers in Germany will also get the Motorola Signature 27 with the Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6 SoC, Android 17, a 6.78" 120Hz "Extreme AMOLED Duo" display with 2,772x1,272-pixel resolution, IP68 and IP69 ratings, MIL-STD-810H certification, and audio tuned by B&O.

The Signature 27 supports 90W wired, 50W wireless, 5W reverse wireless, and 5W reverse wired charging. It sports a 50MP selfie camera on the front, and a triple-camera setup on the back comprising a 50MP primary, a 200MP 3x periscope telephoto, and a 50MP ultrawide camera. These are joined by a dedicated Event Vision Sensor (EVS).

EVS is an action sensor that detects pixel-level changes in light intensity and works with the Action Shot feature to freeze fast-moving subjects.


Motorola Signature 27's color options

The Motorola Signature 27 is priced at €1,899 in Germany and has two color options - Pantone Coal Smoke and Pantone Capulet Olive. While it comes with up to 16GB RAM and 512GB storage, customers in Germany have only one memory option: 12GB/512GB.
