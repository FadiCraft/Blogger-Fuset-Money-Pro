Apple switched from titanium to aluminum for the frame of the iPhone 17 Pro and 17 Pro Max, and stuck with that material for the iPhone 18 Pro and 18 Pro Max this year. However, it looks like aluminum might not live much longer in its top-end lineup.

Today, tipster Fixed Focus Digital took to Weibo to claim that the iPhone 20 Pro will have a curved design, and that early-stage engineering work on a titanium frame for it is already underway. According to the tipster, the frame is somewhat like the one on the iPhone Air, but much more rounded.

iPhone 18 Pro with its aluminum chassis

This isn't the first time the Air has been brought up in connection with Apple's 20th-anniversary iPhones. Back in August, the same tipster said that the iPhone 20 series would get a frame similar to the Air's, and the thin handset just so happens to be built around a titanium one. Meanwhile, as we reported earlier today, another tipster claims that Apple is developing four models for next year, two of which will have quad-curved glass.

If this pans out, the 17 Pro and 18 Pro generations will end up being the only aluminum Pro iPhones since titanium arrived with the iPhone 15 Pro. The post doesn't mention the Pro Max specifically, but past rumors said that the redesign will be applied to both Pro models. Anyway, the iPhone 20 Pro lineup is expected to launch in September 2027, so there's plenty of time for more leaks to arrive in the meantime.
