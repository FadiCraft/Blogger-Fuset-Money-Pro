iQOO will launch the Z11 in India on August 20, and on the same day, the brand will also unveil the iQOO Buds TWS earphones.

The teaser posted by iQOO's Indian branch on X reveals the iQOO Buds' design. These are in-ear type and have silicone ear tips, which should offer passive noise cancellation. However, it is unclear whether they will have active noise cancellation.

The iQOO Buds' charging case has an LED indicator on the front, above which is the brand logo. At the bottom is the USB-C port.

iQOO hasn't revealed the features of the iQOO Buds yet, but we might see the brand reveal a few features ahead of the August 20 unveiling to build hype.
