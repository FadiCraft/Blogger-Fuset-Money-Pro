Last week, Honor opened its early access program for the upcoming MagicOS 11 based on Android 17 for the Magic V6, and today the brand has officially announced the new software's unveiling date.

Honor is holding a "Pioneer Event" in China on August 6, which is when it will officially showcase MagicOS 11 and all of its new features. As you can see, a glass-like UI is coming, because of course it is - Apple's influence over Chinese Android makers is undeniable.

Honor says MagicOS 11 will deliver a "vibrant, transparent, and silky-smooth" user experience, thanks to its significant upgrades that make it "a whole new evolution of the Android experience" (all quotes are machine translated from Chinese).
