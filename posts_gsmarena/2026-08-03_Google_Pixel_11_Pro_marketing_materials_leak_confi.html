After showing us marketing materials for the Pixel 11 and Pixel 11 Pro Fold last week, Evan Blass aka @evleaks is back today with the Pixel 11 Pro.

As you can see, the design is similar to that of the Pixel 11, but the Pro has the Pixel Glow round RGB LED light on the back, which will be used for notifications and such.


Google Pixel 11 Pro leaked images

Like the rest of the family, the Pixel 11 Pro's camera bump is now fully glass-clad, there's no more metal bit on the right side. The camera island also seems to protrude ever so slightly more than in the Pixel 10 Pro, but we can't be sure.


More Google Pixel 11 Pro leaked images

Gemini Intelligence is once again advertised in these marketing materials, and it seems like it will be a big unique selling point for the entire Pixel 11 series. A couple of these images also reveal some specs: first, that the Pixel 11 Pro's telephoto camera will support 120x digital zoom, and second, that the Pixel 11 Pro is powered by the Tensor G6 SoC, just as expected.

The Pixel 11 devices will be made official on August 12.
