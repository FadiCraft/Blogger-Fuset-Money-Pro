We’ve heard plenty about the Oppo Find X10 Pro Max in recent weeks. The flagship is said to launch in China this month with a triple 200MP rear camera setup. Ahead of any official announcement, leaked renders of the phone have now surfaced.

The leaked renders, shared by tipster Fixed Focus Digital, show a redesigned rear camera module that now stretches across the width of the device.

It’s somewhat similar to the iPhone 17 Pro’s camera plateau. However, the Find X10 Pro Max features a second square module positioned within the larger rectangular camera island.

The inner module houses three camera sensors, while an LED flash and an additional sensor are placed outside it in separate cutouts. Also visible is the Lumo and Hasselblad branding on the camera island.


The phone is shown in white and blue colorways. The white variant of the Oppo Find X10 Pro Max appears to draw design inspiration from the Find X9 Ultra’s Tundra Umber option.

The white colorway is also said to feature a unique texture, possibly similar to the faux-leather finish found on the Find X9 Ultra.

According to recent rumors, the Oppo Find X10 Pro Max could come with a MediaTek Dimensity 9600 Pro chipset and an 8,000 mAh battery.
