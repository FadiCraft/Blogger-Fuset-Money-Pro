Oppo teased the K14 Plus last week, and today it announced that the smartphone will be unveiled in India on September 29 at 12 PM local time.

The Oppo K14 Plus will be available through Flipkart as a Flipkart Unique product, with Oppo promising "exciting offers during the Big Billion Days" to customers.

A promo page set up on Flipkart reveals the Oppo K14 Plus will come with a MediaTek 7360 Max SoC, a 6.77" 1.5K AMOLED screen with a 144Hz refresh rate and 3,600 nits peak brightness, ColorOS 16, 8GB RAM, and 256GB storage.

The Oppo K14 Plus will feature three cameras - one on the front and two on the rear. Fueling the smartphone will be an 8,000 mAh battery with 45W charging support. Other features revealed by Flipkart include dual stereo speakers, 5,000 mm2 VC cooling system, an in-display fingerprint scanner, and an IP69K rating.

The Oppo K14 Plus will come in two colors, but their marketing names are yet to be revealed.
