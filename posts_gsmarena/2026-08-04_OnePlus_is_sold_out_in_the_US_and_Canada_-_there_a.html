Good night, sweet prince – OnePlus is officially out of the US market. On July 16, the company made the initial announcement that it will be exiting the North American and European markets. And now the last OnePlus phone has been sold via oneplus.com.

The OnePlus 15 and OnePlus 15R are both out of stock on the official site. The site is now pretty barren – there are no more tablets, no smartwatches either. You can still find some headphones and a few other accessories, but that’s about it. The OnePlus Canada site has even less – there, even the OnePlus Buds are out of stock. There are no “Buy” buttons on OnePlus Mexico either.


OnePlus.com has sold its last phone in the US and Canada

If you are in the US and want an official US-bound phone, you might still find a few units at third-party retailers like Amazon, but these are the last dregs. And if you were planning on importing future models, keep in mind that you are at the mercy of cellphone carriers and their whitelists.

Speaking of future models, we might see the OnePlus 16 in October, but that will launch in China and, perhaps, India and nowhere else. Realme will continue operating outside of China, but that brand never launched in North America. Oppo has no presence in the US or Canada either. Oppo Mexico does sell current flagships like the Find N6 and there is Realme Mexico too.
