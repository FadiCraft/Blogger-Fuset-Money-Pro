Earlier this month word got out that Honor's upcoming 700 Pro will have a rear screen just like Xiaomi's 18 Pro and 18 Pro Max (and the 17 Pro and 17 Pro Max before that). Today, the prolific leaker who goes by Digital Chat Station on Weibo has shared some more details about it and Honor's other planned releases for the end of this year.

The Honor 700 Pro's rear screen is once again 'confirmed' (as much as a rumor can confirm anything), as is its 200MP main camera, which we've heard about before too.

Honor will also apparently launch a new X-series phone with a 7" screen and a "super-large" battery. This could be the 12,000 mAh battery that we heard about in August. Honor is allegedly working on a wide foldable device as well, and even a wider normal phone akin to Huawei's Pura X View.

All of these devices are currently scheduled to launch at the end of the year, so it's definitely going to be a very busy period for Honor going forward. Undoubtedly, we'll be hearing a lot more about all of them in the coming weeks, so stick around for that.
