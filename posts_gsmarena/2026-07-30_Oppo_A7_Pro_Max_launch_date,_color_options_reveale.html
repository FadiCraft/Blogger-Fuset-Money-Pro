After recently confirming the arrival of the Oppo A7 Pro Max with a massive 10,000mAh battery, the company has now revealed the smartphone's launch date along with a few additional details.

The Oppo A7 Pro Max will be unveiled in China on August 4 and is already available for pre-order through the company's official online store.

Oppo has also confirmed that the phone will come with a dual-rear camera setup with a 50MP primary rear camera. And for selfies, the phone will feature a 50MP camera.

Meanwhile, the official listing reveals that the A7 Pro Max will be offered in black, light blue, and orange color options. The blue and orange variants feature a distinctive patterned finish on the rear panel.


The handset will be available in 8GB/128GB, 12GB/256GB, and 12GB/512GB memory configurations.

As per recent rumors and a Geekbench listing, the Oppo A7 Pro Max could come with a 6.78-inch AMOLED display and a Snapdragon 4 Gen 5 chipset.
