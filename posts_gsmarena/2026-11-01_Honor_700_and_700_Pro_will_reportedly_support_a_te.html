Honor's 700 series is expected to launch in China next month, and two well-known tipsters on Weibo have shared a few more details about it. According to Digital Chat Station, both the Honor 700 and the Honor 700 Pro will support a 200mm teleconverter. The tipster also says the series has a mid-size screen with ultra-narrow bezels, a large battery, a built-in rear screen, a Snapdragon chipset, and a periscope telephoto camera, plus an A-list celebrity as its brand ambassador.

Smart Pikachu, on the other hand, says reservations for the Honor 700 series are about to open, and lists 100MP-class imaging, ultra-long battery life, a screen with ultra-narrow bezels, a "creative" rear screen, teleconverter support, a trendy design, and Live Photos from both the front and rear cameras. That tipster also mentioned a new brand ambassador and tagged the post with the name of Chinese actress Ju Jingyi, hinting that she might be the "A-list celebrity" DCS was also talking about.

Honor 600 Pro

In a post from last week, DCS said there are three Honor 700 models, and that the engineering prototype of the 700 Pro has a 6.57-inch 1.5K screen with ultra-narrow bezels on all four sides, a 1.72-inch rear screen, a 200MP main camera, a 50MP periscope telephoto camera, and a battery of around 9,000 mAh (the biggest in the series). The phones in the Honor 700 family should come in colors that roughly translate to Cool White, Clear Sky, Blues, and Pink Mist.
