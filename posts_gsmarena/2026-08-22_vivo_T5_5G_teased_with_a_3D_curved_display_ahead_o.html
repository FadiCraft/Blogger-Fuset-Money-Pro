vivo's T5 series has five smartphones, including the T5 4G, which was unveiled in May. The brand will soon unveil the T5 5G as the next member of the lineup, as it has started teasing the smartphone in India.

vivo says the T5 5G will have "Faster AI with GenAI Ready NPU" and a 3D curved display. The vivo T5 5G is also advertised to have an "Ultra Slim Body," but its exact slimness isn't known.

A video posted by vivo India on its official X account suggests the T5 5G will feature a horizontally aligned camera island on its rear, like the vivo S2, but there's no confirmation on how many usable cameras it will include.
