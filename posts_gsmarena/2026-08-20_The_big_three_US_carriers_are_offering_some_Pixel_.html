The Google Pixel 11 series is now available in stores and from carriers in select regions, following a week of pre-orders. Here’s a look at what the big three US carriers have to offer.

T-Mobile is offering the three non-folding models for free – or as the carrier puts it “get the Pixel 11 Pro XL on Us”. This means that it’s $1,300 off, making the base model free. You need to add a new line or trade in an old device (in any condition) and sign up for Experience Beyond 2.0 and Go5G Next. The actual discount will come in the form of credits to your monthly bill (up to 36 months).

Similarly, the Pixel 11 and 11 Pro are available with Essentials 2.0 and Experience More 2.0/Go5G Plus, respectively. The Google Pixel Watch 5 is $300 off, so a 41mm model will cost you $200 and a 45mm model is $30 more. You’ll need to add a new line on the Watch Plus Plan for that.

T-Mobile also has a 25% discount on an accessory bundle – a case, a screen protector and a charger. Business users are also looking at free devices (“on Us”), check the press release for more details. Note that some plans give you access to T-Satellite, which is a T-Mobile/SpaceX collaboration that adds cell coverage via Starlink satellites.

AT&T offers the Google Pixel 11 Pro for free if you trade in an old Pixel – it can be any old Pixel phone in any condition. The Pro XL model has a similar deal – up to $1,350 off with a trade-in. The vanilla Pixel 11, meanwhile, is available for $11/month with no trade-in required. The Pixel Watch 5 is 50% off when purchased online.

The Pixel 11 series is FirstNet Ready (this is a government-run network for first responders). The phones are available for AT&T Business customers too, more details here.

Existing Verizon customers can get up to $900 off on an eligible myPlan by trading in an old phone. This makes the vanilla Pixel 11 free. If you want the Pixel 11 Pro (256GB), you can get it for $45 a month for 48 months – this means a $15 device payment plus a $30 plan rate per month. $15 x 48 months = $720, so this includes $380 in promotional credits.

If you don’t want to get locked into using the Pro for four years, you can get it for free on the Simplicity plan for $80 a month – this plan allows you to upgrade your phone every year. Verizon Business users can get up to $900 off on the Pixel 11 series – more on that in the press release.

The Google Pixel 11 Pro Fold is available on T-Mobile and Verizon (but not on AT&T), but you won’t be getting any special deals and discounts for it.
