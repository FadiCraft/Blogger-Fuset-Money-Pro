Late last month, a tipster shared some details about the vivo X500s, which is expected to be the fourth model in the company’s X500 lineup. Now, the same tipster has revealed more specifications of the upcoming phone.

According to tipster Digital Chat Station, an upcoming S-series flagship phone, expected to be the vivo X500s, will be powered by a Dimensity 9-series chipset.

vivo X500

The phone is also said to pack a larger battery than its predecessor, the vivo X300s, which features a 7,100mAh cell. Additionally, the tipster claims that the X500s will feature a 64MP periscope telephoto camera.

DCS previously revealed that the upcoming phone will sport a 6.85-inch LTPO display with 2K resolution and a 144Hz refresh rate.
