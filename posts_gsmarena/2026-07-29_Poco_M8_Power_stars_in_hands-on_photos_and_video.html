The Poco M8 Power is going to be launched in India on August 4, and it's expected to be a rebranded Redmi Note 17. Ahead of its debut, the phone has been showcased in hands-on photos in three colors, which you can see below.


Poco M8 Power hands-on images

Naturally, the orange colorway is the most striking (and Apple-inspired). Thus, it's the star of the quick hands-on video you can see below.

From the same source as these photos and videos comes the knowledge that the phone's 8,000 mAh battery will charge at 45W, and will also support 22.5W reverse wired charging.


More Poco M8 Power hands-on images

If this is a rebranded Redmi Note 17, then expect it to sport a 7-inch 1080x2396 OLED display with a 120Hz refresh rate and 1,800-nit peak brightness, the Snapdragon 4 Gen 4 chipset, 6/8GB of RAM, 128/256GB of storage, a 50MP main camera, and an 8MP selfie camera.
