Last year, Realme teamed up with Warner Bros. Discovery Global Consumer Products for the Realme 15 Pro Game of Thrones Limited Edition. Today, the company announced another partnership with Warner Bros. for new limited-edition products, this time inspired by the Harry Potter universe. The Realme products getting the Harry Potter treatment are the Realme 16 Pro and the Realme Buds T500 Pro TWS earphones.

The Realme 16 Pro Harry Potter Edition and the Realme Buds T500 Pro Harry Potter Edition are expected to have the same hardware as the standard models, but come with very different designs and much richer retail packages.

The Realme 16 Pro Harry Potter Edition appears to be a customized Master Gold version with a color-changing back panel featuring the logos of the four houses of Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry - Gryffindor, Slytherin, Ravenclaw, and Hufflepuff. It also features the Hogwarts School's official Latin motto, "Draco Dormiens Nunquam Titillandus," which translates to "Never tickle a sleeping dragon."

Furthermore, the smartphone's right frame has "Welcome to Hogwarts" written on it.

The Realme Buds T500 Pro Harry Potter Edition also comes in the brown-gold color theme. The buds have the letter 'H' printed on them, while their charging case features the Hogwarts School's logo. A closer look at the case also suggests it has a vegan leather finish.

Realme hasn't revealed pricing or availability details for these Harry Potter edition products, but we know they will launch in India.
