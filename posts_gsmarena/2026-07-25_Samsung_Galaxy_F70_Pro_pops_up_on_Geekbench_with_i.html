Samsung unveiled the Galaxy F70e in February, and we might see it launch a Pro model in the coming months, as the smartphone was spotted on the GSMA database a few months ago and has now popped up on Geekbench.

The benchmark database reveals that the Samsung Galaxy F70 Pro, bearing model code SM-E476B, is powered by Qualcomm's SM6475 chip, which corresponds to the Snapdragon 6 Gen 3 SoC. It consists of two quad-core clusters - one having a maximum clock speed of 1.8GHz and the other having a clock speed of up to 2.4GHz. GPU is likely Adreno 710.


Samsung Galaxy F70e

The Galaxy F70 Pro unit that ran the Geekbench test had Android 16 and 8GB RAM onboard. Although there could be more memory options that are yet to be confirmed. Regarding performance, the F70 Pro scored 998 and 3,090 points in Geekbench's single and multi-core tests, respectively. That's in line with what we've seen from some smartphones powered by the same chip in our tests.


Samsung Galaxy F70 Pro on Geekbench

There's no word from Samsung about the Galaxy F70 Pro, but expect to hear more about it as we inch closer to its launch.
