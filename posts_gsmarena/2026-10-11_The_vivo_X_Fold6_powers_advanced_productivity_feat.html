As folding technology matures, the focus has shifted from perfecting the hinge to slimming things down and now battery life has emerged as a key differentiating factor for foldables - the tablet-like multitasking, media consumption and gaming experience enabled by the larger screen put extra strain on the battery.

The vivo X Fold6 is a productivity powerhouse with its large 8.02” main display, advanced multitasking, cross-platform features - plus India’s biggest battery in the foldable category, so that users can get an uncompromised smartphone experience.

It has a 6,900mAh battery capacity and uses 5th gen Silicon Anode Technology for increased energy density and improved cycle life. This ensures good battery endurance when you get the phone and also after years of using it. vivo's Semi-Solid Electrolyte 2.0 design allows the battery to run at temperatures below freezing – down to -20°C and even -30°C - so you can take it on your next ski trip without worries.

The X Fold6 supports 80W FlashCharge and 40W Wireless FlashCharge. The Predictive Charging Time feature shows an estimated time remaining to hit 80% state of charge on the lock screen – the Battery settings screen shows that plus a prediction for the time remaining for a full charge.


vivo X Fold6

Normally, a bigger screen means a bigger battery, so heavy multitaskers naturally gravitate towards bigger phones. The geometry of foldable phones complicates this - for one, they have a larger screen than similarly sized slab phones, but also the internals are more tightly packed. Battery technology needed time to catch up to the expectations of a large-screened flagship, but it's finally here.

Speaking of the large screen, the vivo X Fold6 brings an 8.02” LTPO 8T panel with 2,504 x 2,312px resolution (424ppi). It has an adaptive refresh rate of 1-120Hz. Its brightness can go all the way up to 5,000 nits (peak local brightness) and all the way down to 1 nit.

To make the most use of the large working area, Origin OS 7 brings an improved Origin Workbench that allows you to run up to 5 apps on the screen simultaneously – four apps split the screen horizontally and vertically and you can add a fifth app in a floating window.

Information tends to bounce between document editors, email, chat apps and various other software - completing a single task can easily require multiple app switches. Split screen simplifies things by reducing the need to switch between apps, copy and paste information manually or interrupt ongoing tasks.

And you don't always get information in a convenient form. The Snap and Go feature on the Origin Island allows you to, say, scan a plane ticket and add it to your calendar. The similar Snap Note feature can take a photo of a whiteboard, handwritten notes, slides or a mind map and use AI to turn the recognized text and structure into an editable note. It can even stitch multiple shots in order. If you travel or work with teams across borders, the phone can translate between different languages. All these feature allow you to complete tasks on the go, saving you time throughout your day.
