Indian handset maker Lava is set to make its UK debut soon with the Agni 4N, which it has been teasing for over a week now by taking constant digs at Samsung. The brand has posted one more teaser, which is yet another dig at the South Korean company.

The teaser video posted by Lava UK on its X account gives us a glimpse of the Lava Agni 4N while also confirming the metal build. The smartphone has a horizontally aligned camera island on the rear like the Agni 4, with the right side frame featuring the volume rocker and power button.

Lava Agni 4

The teaser was posted with the caption "Dear SAM, Watch and Learn," which is yet another dig by Lava at Samsung for launching some phones with a plastic build.

Aside from that, the Indian handset maker took multiple digs at Samsung for launching smartphones that look identical to the previous models and have pre-loaded apps, a plastic build, slow processing, and slow charging speeds. As if that's not enough, Lava UK's X bio says, "Escape the SAMe cycle."
