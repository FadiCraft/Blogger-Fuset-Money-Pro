Samsung released Wear OS 7-based One UI 9 Watch beta for the Galaxy Watch8 and Watch8 Classic last month, and a few days ago, it rolled out the beta for the Galaxy Watch7 and Galaxy Watch Ultra. Now, Samsung has expanded the One UI 9 Watch beta rollout to the Galaxy Watch6 and Galaxy Watch6 Classic.

The One UI 9 Watch beta update for the Galaxy Watch6's 40mm and 44mm models has firmware versions R93xXXU2ZZI1 and R94xXXU2ZZI1, respectively, while the Galaxy Watch6 Classic's 43mm and 47mm models are receiving the update with firmware versions R95xXXU2ZZI1 and R96xXXU2ZZI1.

The One UI 9 Watch beta for the Galaxy Watch6 series is rolling out in South Korea, and you can try it by enrolling in the One UI Watch 9 beta program through the Samsung Members app.

Samsung Galaxy Watch6 Classic

In addition to expanding the One UI 9 Watch beta to the Galaxy Watch6 series, Samsung rolled out the second One UI 9 beta update for the Galaxy S25 series, which received its first beta about a week ago.

Samsung Galaxy S25 Ultra

The One UI 9 Beta 2 update for the Galaxy S25, Galaxy S25+, and Galaxy S25 Ultra is currently rolling out in India with firmware version ending in ZZI4/DZI4. The update weighs around 660MB and includes bug fixes and new AI features.

Samsung Galaxy S25

While the update's changelog doesn't name these "new AI features," multiple reports confirm they are the Galaxy Z Fold8 series features Samsung rolled out for the Galaxy S26 series with its seventh One UI 9 beta. We are talking about Now Nudge, My FanCam, Call Brief, and Custom Cards in Now Brief.
