The Honor Watch 5 Pro never got the wide release that it deserved – oh well, all eyes are on its successor now. Initially announced at the end of September for China, this watch is launching in Europe now.

The Honor Watch 6 Pro is going on pre-sale today – Honor is offering it at promotional prices and with cool early bird perks. Note that the euro prices below apply to France, Germany, Italy and Spain. Also, the price depends on whether you want the sporty fluoroelastomer strap or the more stylish steel strap. The promo prices are valid until December 31.

Honor Watch 6 Pro RRP Promo price
Black Fluoroelastomer Strap €379 / £329 €279 / £229
Silver Steel Strap €429 / £379 €329 / £279

Before you decide, you should know that Honor will include a free 22mm universal leather strap during the initial sales period, so you will have a stylish option, even if you go for the cheaper option. This might boil down the choice to whether you like the Black case or the Silver Steel case better.


Honor Watch 6 Pro: Silver body with Silver Steel Strap

The extra strap isn’t the only freebie either – Honor will include a free pair of clip-on headphones. Which ones depends on where you are. The default is the Honor Clip buds, but they are out of stock in Germany and Spain, so those regions get the Honor Clip 2 Pro instead.

Finally, watches bought during the initial sales period come with a 12-month warranty extension, liquid damage protection, screen protection and replacement service. Note that the discounted price for the watch, the free accessories and the other promotional perks are subject to availability.

We’ve already seen the Honor Watch 6 Pro specs, but let’s do a quick recap – starting from the outside and going in. The watch measures 46.3mm across and is 11.6mm thick with a weight of 50.9g. It has a diamond-cut octagonal crown and a side button.

Honor Watch 6 Pro

The 1.5” display is a Tandem OLED panel that can reach an impressive 5,500 nits of brightness and is surrounded by a ceramic bezel. Inside is a 515mAh battery that can last up to 18 days in Ultra-Long Battery Mode, up to 10 days of regular usage and up to 7 days with Always-On Display active.

The watch is rated 5ATM, IP68, IP69 and IP69K so that it can brave the outdoors. It remains functional in the rain thanks to Wet-Screen Touch. The triple-antenna design offers accurate positioning in the urban jungle or out in the forest. There are 120+ sports modes, including a Professional Running Coach, Outdoor Trail Running (with barometric pressure tracking and a Route Back option), Professional Football Mode and Professional Badminton Mode. There is Strava integration so that you can share your adventures.


Honor Watch 6 Pro: Black body with Black Fluoroelastomer Strap

The new Intelligent Sense System on the watch provides 24/7 health tracking. Its optical heart rate sensor has multi-wavelength and multi-optical path so that it can reach the deeper layers of skin. It offers a Morning Report when you wake up and tracks Body Energy, Vitality, All-day HRV, health trends and Daily Wellness Indicators throughout the day.

The watch supports both Android and iOS – literally, the Dual-Phone Message Notification allows it to show you messages and manage calls from an Honor phone and an iPhone connected simultaneously. Honor partnered with Fidesmo to offer wide support for contactless NFC payments with both Mastercard and Visa bank cards.
