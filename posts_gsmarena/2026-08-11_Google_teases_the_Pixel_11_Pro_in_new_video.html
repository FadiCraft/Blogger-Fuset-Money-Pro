Google is unveiling the Pixel 11 family and the Pixel Watch 5 on August 12. Ahead of that, the company has already released a teaser video for the Pixel Watch 5 last week, gently poking fun at the device's weirdly-shaped competitors.

Today Google is back with another teaser video, this time for the Pixel 11 Pro. This one is less about the competition, and more about the fact that these devices are apparently a solid 11 on a scale of 1 to 10. Or something like that. See for yourself:

The Pixel 11, Pixel 11 Pro, Pixel 11 Pro XL, and Pixel 11 Pro Fold are all expected to be powered by the Tensor G6 chipset, which is going to be the first mainstream smartphone chip to be built on a 2nm process. The exclusivity won't last long, though, as both MediaTek and Qualcomm are expected to launch their own 2nm chips in September.
