In case you missed it, Samsung increased the Galaxy S26 prices globally – in the US, they are $100 higher than they were at launch seven months ago. And even back then, most configurations cost more than the equivalent S25 models did last year.

However, it’s not too late to pick up a Galaxy S26, S26+ or S26 Ultra at a good price. Your best bet at the moment is probably Walmart – not only has the retailer not applied the new prices, but it is even selling the phones at a discount compared to the old prices. In the case of the S26 Ultra, that is up to $500 off.

Samsung’s revised price for the Galaxy S26 Ultra is $1,400 for the base 12/256GB model (well, not quite – see below). Walmart will sell it to you for under $1,000. If you haven’t researched it yet, here’s how the S26 Ultra compares against the S25 Ultra and the iPhone 17 Pro Max.
