Yesterday we heard that Oppo is already working on the F35 family, which would be launching in India in the next few weeks. Today a new rumor from the same source claims that the series is actually getting official before the end of this month.

While it's still unclear how many members the F35 family will have, new details about the F35 Pro have emerged. Apparently, it will be a rebranded A7 Pro Max, which just launched in China today.

Oppo A7 Pro Max

That means it will have a whopping 10,000 mAh battery, the largest ever seen in an Oppo phone. If it's a straight-up rebrand job, then expect the F35 Pro to also sport a 6.78-inch 1272x2772 AMOLED screen with a 120Hz refresh rate, a 50MP main camera, a 2MP monochrome camera, and a 50MP selfie camera.

It will be powered by the Snapdragon 4 Gen 5 SoC. It will allegedly be offered in India for INR 40,000 ($420) to INR 45,000 ($473).
