Motorola has the Edge 70, the Edge 70 Max, the Edge 70 Pro and Pro+, the Edge 70 Fusion and Fusion+, and the Ultra, but there's no Neo, at least thus far.

Тhat is about to change according to a forum post about the Android 17 Beta for the Motorola Edge 70 Neo by a senior forum member, the phone is likely coming soon. The post reads as a call for participants for a firmware beta test about an existing phone, which points to one of two scenarios - either the person leaked an unannounced phone, or he made a mistake and meant another model.

It's unlikely to be a mistake, however, as the participation form for the beta is also in the Edge 70 Neo's name.

The Neo model is usually the compact in the Motorola Edge family. The Edge 60 Neo is a 6.36-inch model, like the Edge 50 Neo before it.
