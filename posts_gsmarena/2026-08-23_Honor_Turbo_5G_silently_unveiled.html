Following a recent listing on AliExpress, Honor Turbo 5G is now official. The official listing on Honor Eurasia confirms the previously leaked specs which include the Snapdragon 6 Gen 4 chipset, a 6.79-inch AMOLED (FHD+120Hz), an 8,560 mAh battery and IP69 ingress protection.

The phone ships with MagicOS 10 based on Android 16 and also features a 50MP main cam and a 5MP ultrawide lens. It is available in black, green and orange colors and starts at RUB 33,990 ($411) in Russia.
