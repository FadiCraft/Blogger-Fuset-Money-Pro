Today the prolific leaker who goes by Digital Chat Station on Weibo has shared some new information about Honor's upcoming device ranges. The Magic9 Pro, expected to be unveiled next month, will have two 200MP cameras with some mystery Arri capabilities.

The Honor Win2 series will come with a flat screen with "2K" resolution and a 10,000 mAh battery. The built-in cooling fan that debuted on the original Win is staying.

The Honor Power3 will come with a 7-inch screen and the industry's largest battery, apparently, so this is probably that mystery device that's been in testing with a 12,000 mAh battery.

Finally, there's the Honor 700 series. This will allegedly have a 200MP camera, a new design, a mid-size screen, and a large battery. There's still plenty of time before the launch, though, so we'll undoubtedly hear more in the coming weeks and months.
