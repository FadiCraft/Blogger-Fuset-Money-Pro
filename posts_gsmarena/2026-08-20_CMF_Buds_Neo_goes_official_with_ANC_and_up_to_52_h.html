As promised, Nothing's CMF sub-brand has unveiled its latest pair of wireless earbuds in India. The CMF Buds Neo offer a distinctive design, active noise cancellation (ANC), and a claimed 52 hours of total playback time.

The CMF Buds Neo feature an in-ear design with 12.4mm dynamic drivers. Meanwhile, the charging case sports a square design with rounded corners, a circular dial, and a lanyard hole.

For audio, the earbuds offer active noise cancellation (ANC) of up to 35dB, along with environmental noise cancellation (ENC) for calls. They also feature four microphones in total.

The Buds Neo offer a custom EQ and static spatial audio, along with Bluetooth 6.0 connectivity and support for AAC and SBC codecs. Pairing is handled by Google Fast Pair and Microsoft Swift Pair, while dual-device connectivity is also supported.

In terms of battery life, the Buds Neo are claimed to offer up to 8 hours of playback on a single charge, or up to 30 hours with the charging case when ANC is enabled. With ANC turned off, these figures increase to 11 hours and 52 hours, respectively.

The earbuds carry an IP55 rating for dust and water resistance, while the charging case is rated IPX2.

The CMF Buds Neo will be available in Black, Dark Blue, and White color options. They will go on sale in India via Flipkart and select retail partners starting August 26.

CMF’s latest wireless earbuds are priced at INR 1,999.
