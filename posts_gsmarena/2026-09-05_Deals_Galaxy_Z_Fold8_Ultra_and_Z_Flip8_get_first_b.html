The Samsung Galaxy Z8 series of foldables launched about a month ago and we’re already seeing the first big discounts for the Z Fold8 Ultra and the Z Flip8. They are still not cheap – flagship foldables never are – but the price drop is tangible.

The Samsung Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra is $300 off. This is a great discount and it makes the Ultra $100 cheaper than the wide Galaxy Z Fold8. Ideally, the choice between these two would come down to form factor preference – the multitasking-friendly Ultra vs. the media-friendly Z Fold8 – but price is a factor that is hard to ignore. The Ultra also has superior cameras, a 200MP 1/1.3” main, a 10MP 3x/67mm telephoto and a 50MP ultra-wide, compared to Z Fold8’s 50MP 1/1.56” main and 50MP ultra-wide (no tele).

The Samsung Galaxy S26+, for example, is $100 more if you are looking at the 256GB variants. And this isn’t even the lowest price we’ve seen recently – a couple of weeks ago it was $50 lower. Note that the Plus model has 4 extra gigs of RAM compared to the FE, so it’s not just about the storage.

The Galaxy S26+ is easily the better phone overall. It has the better 6.7” screen (1440p+ LTPO vs. 1080p+ LTPS), the better chipset (Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 for Galaxy vs. Exynos 2500) and better connectivity (including USB-C 3.2 vs. USB-C 2.0). The cameras and battery are similar or identical. If it wasn’t for the $200 gift card, we’d tell you to not even consider the S26 FE. With the gift card, however, both phones are worth thinking about.
