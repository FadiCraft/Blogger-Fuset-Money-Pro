There have been plenty of leaks and rumors surrounding Samsung’s upcoming flagship, the Galaxy S27 Ultra, which is expected to launch early next year. A new rumor has now revealed the phone’s alleged dimensions.

While leaked CAD renders of the Galaxy S27 Ultra suggested that the phone would measure 7.97mm in thickness, a new rumor from tipster Ice Universe has now corroborated the figure.

Alleged Galaxy S27 Ultra render

According to the tipster, Samsung’s upcoming Ultra flagship will measure 7.9mm in thickness, matching the Galaxy S26 Ultra.

The tipster also claims that the Galaxy S27 Ultra will weigh 218 grams, making it slightly heavier than its predecessor, which weighs 214 grams.

The increased weight could be due to a higher capacity battery. As per recent rumors, the S27 Ultra could pack a 5,700mAh battery. We hope the rumor turns out to be true.
