Oppo is launching the Reno17 and Reno17 Pro in China on November 16, and ahead of that today the company has revealed that the Reno17 will have four 50MP cameras: a main one, a periscope telephoto camera, an ultrawide, and the selfie camera.

While the Chinese Reno16 already had the other three 50MP cameras, its main one was 200MP, for what it’s worth. That said, the global Reno16, released this July, had a similar setup to what the Chinese Reno17 will boast.

It's still not clear what will happen with the Reno17 Pro, whether it will keep the 200MP main camera of its predecessor or not. According to recent leaks, the Reno17 will have a 6.5-inch screen, the Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 SoC, and an 8,000 mAh battery, while the Reno17 Pro will have a 6.3-inch screen, the Dimensity 8550 at the helm, and a 7,200 mAh battery.
