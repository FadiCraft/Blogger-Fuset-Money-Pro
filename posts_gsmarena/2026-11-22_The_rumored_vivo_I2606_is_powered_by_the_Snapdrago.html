A few days ago, we saw a BIS certification listing for the vivo I2606. Since we already know the iQOO 16 family is coming to the country, likely at an unfortunately high price, we just put two and two together.


vivo I2606 on GeekBench and BIS

Well, now we have a GeekBench score from the I2606, proving that it is running the Snapdragon 8s Gen 4 (SM8735), along with 12GB of RAM and Android 17. This development is interesting, since if the I2606 is, in fact, the iQOO 16R, we are looking at a pretty major chipset downgrade coming from the iQOO 15R and its Snapdragon 8 Gen 5. If this is the case, then the rest of the specs will probably also include a flat high refresh rate OLED display measuring around 6.59 inches, LPDDR5X RAM, UFS 4.1 storage, a 50MP main camera, a battery around the 9000mAh mark and 100W, or so, charging.

There is, however, the other possibility that the I2606 is in fact the iQOO 16s. We have no idea what an “s” might denote, but an industry source has speculated that the iQOO 16s might just be the first compact device from the iQOO brand, with a display diagonal somewhere in the 6.3-inch range.
