Back in July, we heard from Digital Chat Station on Weibo that the 20th anniversary iPhone Pro Max (which we'll just call iPhone 20 Pro Max for simplicity) will have a 7-inch screen, slightly larger than the iPhone 17 Pro Max's 6.9-inch panel.

Today the same source is back confirming the same claim, while also adding that the iPhone 20 Pro will apparently also get a larger screen than the iPhone 17 Pro's 6.3-inch display. The iPhone 20 Pro will have a 6.4" panel.

iPhone 17 Pro

DCS further states that both the iPhone 20 Pro and the iPhone 20 Pro Max will have the same aspect ratio as the iPhone 17 Pro and iPhone 17 Pro Max, but the upcoming devices may have a new quad-curved front design that will seem bezel-less.

If you're wondering why Apple might skip from 18 straight to 20, it's because next year is the 20th anniversary of the first iPhone being released, and we're pretty sure the company will want to celebrate the occasion in some way.
