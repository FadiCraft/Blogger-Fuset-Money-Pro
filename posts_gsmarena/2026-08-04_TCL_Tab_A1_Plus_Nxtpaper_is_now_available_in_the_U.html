TCL unveiled the Tab A1 Plus and its Nxtpaper variant in early March at MWC, and now the Nxtpaper version is finally available in the US. You can grab it from TCL's online store for $299.99 for a limited time during the company’s Back to School sale, or from Amazon. The RRP is $399.99.

The tablet comes with a 12.2-inch 2400x1600 LCD screen with a 120Hz refresh rate, 3:2 aspect ratio, and 500-nit peak brightness, TCL's Nxtpaper tech with its matte display look, the Snapdragon 4 Gen 2 SoC, 6GB of RAM, 256GB of expandable storage, an 8MP front camera, an 8MP main camera, and a 10,000 mAh battery with support for 33W fast charging.

The device has four speakers, two mics, and it comes in Aerolite Grey. It measures 275.6 x 189.34 x 6.7 mm and weighs 556g. It comes with a stylus and a flip case in the box. If you're interested in it, check out our hands-on with the TCL Tab A1 Plus Nxtpaper from MWC.
