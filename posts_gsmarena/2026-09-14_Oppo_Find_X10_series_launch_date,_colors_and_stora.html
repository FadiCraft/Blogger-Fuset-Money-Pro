After teasing the lineup and their key features last week, Oppo has now confirmed when the Find X10 series will launch in China. The company has also revealed the phones’ designs, color options, and storage configurations.

The Oppo Find X10, Find X10 Pro Max, and Find X10 E will launch in China on September 22, just one day after vivo is set to unveil its X500 series in the country. Oppo has also revealed the design, color options, and the RAM/storage configurations of all three devices on their official online store in China.

The Oppo Find X10 Pro Max will feature a square-shaped rear camera module housing three 200MP sensors. Meanwhile, the LED flash and other sensors will be housed in a vertical, pill-shaped module positioned to the right of the main camera island.

As for color options, it will be available in Moon White, Light Titanium, and Warm Orange.

The Find X10 Pro Max will be offered in 12GB/256GB, 12GB/512GB, 16GB/512GB, and 16GB/1TB configurations. It is also confirmed to feature a display with ultra-thin 1.15mm bezels.

Meanwhile, the standard Find X10 will also feature a redesigned rear camera module, although its design differs from the Pro Max.

Official images show a large rectangular camera plateau housing two separate modules. One is square-shaped and houses three camera sensors, while the other is pill-shaped and contains the LED flash.

The standard model will be available in Clear Orange, Ice Blue, and Light Titanium colorways.

It will be sold in the same RAM and storage configurations as the Pro Max variant.

Lastly, the Oppo Find X10 E’s design is reminiscent of the current Find X9.

It features a square-shaped rear camera module housing three sensors and the LED flash. The Find X10 E will be available in Ice Blue and Light Titanium color options.

It will be sold in 12GB/256GB and 16GB/512GB configurations.

Oppo will also unveil the Watch S2 smartwatch and the Enco X4 TWS earbuds at the event. The company is likely to share more details about the phones in the coming days.
