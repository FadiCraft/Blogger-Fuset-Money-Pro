Yesterday we showed you what the Samsung Galaxy Buds On will look like, thanks to some leaked illustrations. Today the same source is back with new ones, only these are depicting a different pair of earbuds. Specifically, earbuds with an ear hook design.

These are reportedly internally codenamed Buds Canal 5, and like all similar products that are already on the market, they are said to be targeting workout and running use. They have flat hooks and an ear tip design that could be either in-ear or semi-in-ear.

Although it's not pictured here, these will most likely come with a charging case. It's unclear when these will be announced, but they might make their debut alongside the Galaxy Buds On and the Galaxy S26 FE in early September.

It will be interesting to see Samsung finally entering two new earbud market segments at the same time, after having only released traditional in-ear and semi-in-ear earbuds so far.
