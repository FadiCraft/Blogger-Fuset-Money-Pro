Samsung is unveiling the Galaxy S26 FE next week, and the company is also expected to announce the Galaxy Tab S12+ and Galaxy Tab S12 Ultra very soon, perhaps on the same day or, if not, in early September.

Ahead of their release, a reliable source has today revealed the pricing for the two tablets. In the US, the Galaxy Tab S12+ will start at $1,119.99 with 256GB of storage. The 512GB version will set you back $1,399.99. The 5G-capable model with 256GB of storage will be priced at $1,349.99.

Samsung Galaxy Tab S11 Ultra

Moving on to the Galaxy Tab S12 Ultra, this one will be $1,399.99 with 256GB, $1,599.99 with 512GB, and $1,999 with 1TB of storage. Note that the Tab S12+ only comes with 12GB of RAM, while the Ultra has 12GB for all variants except the 1TB one, which has 16GB of RAM. The Ultra prices seem to all be for the Wi-Fi-only model.

If these prices are accurate, then both tablets will be $200 more expensive in their base configurations than their respective predecessors, the Galaxy Tab S10+ and the Galaxy Tab S11 Ultra, were when each of them came out.
