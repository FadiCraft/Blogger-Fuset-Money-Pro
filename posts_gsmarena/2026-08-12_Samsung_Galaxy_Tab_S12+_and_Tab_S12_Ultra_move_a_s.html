Last month, Samsung confirmed that the Galaxy Tab S12 series would be unveiled in H2 2026. Samsung didn't reveal the launch date of the Galaxy Tab S12 series, but the lineup has moved a step closer to launch as it got certified by Bluetooth SIG.

The Galaxy Tab S12 series includes two tablets - the Galaxy Tab S12+ and the Galaxy Tab S12 Ultra. The Bluetooth SIG database lists five models of the Galaxy Tab S12+ and three models of the Galaxy Tab S12 Ultra, along with their model numbers, and both tablets are expected to come in cellular and non-cellular versions.

Samsung Galaxy Tab S12+ and Galaxy Tab S12 Ultra get Bluetooth SIG certified

The Bluetooth SIG database doesn't reveal the specs of either tablet; however, previously leaked CAD renders of the Galaxy Tab S12 Ultra revealed what the tablet will look like.


Samsung Galaxy Tab S12 Ultra's leaked render | Source: OnLeaks and Smartphone Checker

The Galaxy Tab S11 series - consisting of the Galaxy Tab S11 and Galaxy Tab S11 Ultra - was unveiled last September, so we expect the Galaxy Tab S12 lineup to go official around the same time.
