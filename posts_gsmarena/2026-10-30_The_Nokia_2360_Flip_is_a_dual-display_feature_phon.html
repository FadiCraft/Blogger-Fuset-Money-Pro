HMD has unveiled the Nokia 2360 Flip, its latest feature phone with a clamshell design and the Family Assist feature. Designed with school students and senior citizens in mind, the phone features dual displays and a dedicated SOS button.

The Nokia 2360 Flip's main highlight is its Family Assist feature, which allows family members to remotely manage the device. They can add contacts, adjust volume levels, set reminders, and access other settings remotely.

It also features a dedicated SOS button on the rear panel. Pressing it calls a pre-set emergency contact and sends an alert message to up to five contacts.

However, the Nokia 2360 Flip only supports 2G connectivity, which will limit its usability in regions where 2G networks have been discontinued.

In terms of specs, the Nokia 2360 Flip features a 2.8-inch main display and a 1.77-inch external screen. Other highlights include a removable 1,450mAh battery, a loudspeaker, a QVGA camera, 4MB of internal storage, FM Radio, and a 3.5mm headphone jack.

The Nokia 2360 Flip will be available in Burgundy Red, Charcoal, and Teal color options. HMD has yet to reveal its pricing and availability details.
