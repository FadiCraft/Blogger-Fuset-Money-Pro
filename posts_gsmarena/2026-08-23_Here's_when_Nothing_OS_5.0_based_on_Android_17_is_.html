Ever since Google released Android 17, you may have been wondering when Nothing will release its own interpretation of the new OS. Well, we are getting closer. The company has just announced that Nothing OS 5.0, which will be based on Android 17, is "coming soon".

But wait, there's more. The company says it will announce something related to the new Nothing OS 5.0 release on August 25 at 11:00 AM BST, which is 12:00 PM in Central Europe and 3:30 PM in India.

There are no other details for now, but we can assume that the company will announce the Nothing OS 5.0 features on that occasion, and possibly even a beta program. It would be quite extraordinary if we saw a stable release without a beta, so we wouldn't get our hopes up for such a possibility. Anyway, we're going to find out for sure in just a few days.
