Honor has confirmed that the successors to the Win and Win RT will be announced next week in China. The Win 2 series launch will also be accompanied by the Win Tablet and the Win Earbuds.

The Honor Win 2 and Win 2 RT will be unveiled in China on October 21, the company confirmed via a post on Weibo.

Honor has also offered a glimpse of the rear panel of the Win 2 series, which is expected to include the Win 2 and Win 2 RT.

The teaser image reveals a dual rear camera setup, an active cooling fan, the Win logo, and an LED light housed within a large camera island. An exhaust vent is also visible at the bottom of the module.

The company has also teased the rear design of the Win Tablet, which is shown with a single rear camera and a cooling fan. The tablet is confirmed to feature an OLED display and will be equipped with the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 chipset.

Honor will also unveil the Win Earbuds at the event alongside the other products.

As per recent leaks, the Honor Win 2 will sport a 6.89-inch OLED display, the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6 chipset, and a 10,000mAh+ battery.

Meanwhile, the Win 2 RT is rumored to come with the older Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 chipset.
