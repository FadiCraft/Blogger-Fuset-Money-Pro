The OnePlus Pad Go 2 was launched in December of last year and now the company is allegedly preparing to unveil a new member of this family. According to a tipster on X, OnePlus is currently putting the finishing touches on the OnePlus Pad Go 2r.

This device will apparently be launching in India on December 9. Unfortunately, there are absolutely no details about its specs or pricing yet. So all we really have to go on is the name.

OnePlus Pad Go 2

And that name implies that this might be a slightly changed version of the OnePlus Pad Go 2 - maybe a lower-end model, maybe a higher-end one, it's impossible to tell at the moment. We'll let you know when we find out more about it.

For reference, note that the Pad Go 2 has a 12.1-inch LCD screen with 1980x2800 resolution and a 120Hz refresh rate, the MediaTek Dimensity 7300 Ultra chipset, 8GB of RAM, 128/256GB of storage, an 8MP rear camera, an 8MP front camera, and a 10,050 mAh battery with support for 33W wired charging.
