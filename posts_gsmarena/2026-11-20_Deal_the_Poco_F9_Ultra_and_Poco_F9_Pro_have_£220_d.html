Are “flagship killers” still a thing? They definitely should be, considering that some flagships have an MSRP of £1,400 or higher now. That’s how much Xiaomi is asking for a 12/256GB Xiaomi 18 Pro Max and it’s not alone – this is also the price of the Oppo Find X10 Pro Max. Those are awesome phones indeed, but they are prohibitively expensive for many.

The Poco F9 Ultra costs half as much – Amazon UK’s Black Friday sale has the base 12/256GB variant at just under £700 and even the 16/512GB upgrade costs only £70 more. Now, we’re not suggesting that the F9 Ultra is in the same class as the Xiaomi 18 Pro Max. We’re just saying that it’s a very capable phone at a very reasonable price.

The F9 Ultra is equipped with a previous-gen Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5, a roomy 6.9” 185Hz OLED display (12-bit colors, Dolby Vision) and a powerful 8,050mAh battery (with 100W wired and 50W wireless charging). So, there’s no Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6 like on the Xiaomi, but the Elite Gen 5 is still massively powerful.


Poco F9 Ultra

The cameras include a 200MP 1/1.56” main, a 50MP 5x/114mm telephoto, a 50MP 102° ultra-wide and a 32MP selfie camera. The ultra-wide didn’t impress, but the main and telephoto were stand-outs – check out our written review for more details or watch the video review below.

The Poco F9 Pro is a bit cheaper than its Ultra sibling – especially if you need 512GB of storage as the upgrade from the 256GB model is only £30. This one is slightly smaller with a 6.59” display (still a 185Hz, 12-bit panel with Dolby Vision) and a 6,330mAh battery (100W wired, 50W wireless charging).

This phone uses the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 V series, which is a binned version of the Elite Gen 5 inside the Ultra. You wouldn’t be able to tell the difference from CPU tests, but the GPU has 33% less compute hardware compared to the full chip. It’s still a more powerful GPU than what you will find inside most £600 phones, though.

The other thing is that the 8MP 112° ultra-wide camera is pretty basic and the 50MP 2.5x/60mm felt underwhelming next to the very capable 200MP main. Check out our review for more details.


Poco F9 Pro

If you can spare the extra cash, the Poco F9 Ultra is the better phone. Both F9 models punch above their price, however, and will last you several years (both are promised 4 OS updates total, but they started with Android 16).

PS. The Poco F9 Ultra won the Best Value category in the GSMArena End-of-Year awards.
