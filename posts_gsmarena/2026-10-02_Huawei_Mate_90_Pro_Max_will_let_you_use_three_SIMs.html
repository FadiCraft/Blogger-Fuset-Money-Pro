Ever wished you could use three SIMs simultaneously on your phone? You can do just that with Huawei’s latest flagship phone, the Mate 90 Pro Max. Huawei launched the phone in China alongside the rest of the Mate 90 series, which includes the Mate 90, Mate 90 Pro, and Mate 90 RS.

An X user has highlighted the feature, which is also confirmed on Huawei’s official specifications page for the Mate 90 Pro Max.

The Huawei Mate 90 Pro Max supports two physical SIM cards and two eSIMs, but allows users to have two physical SIMs and one eSIM active simultaneously. However, Huawei notes that two of the three active connections must be from the same Chinese carrier.

The smartphone is powered by the Kirin 9050 Pro chipset, features a 6.9-inch display, a triple-rear camera setup, and a 6,800mAh battery.
