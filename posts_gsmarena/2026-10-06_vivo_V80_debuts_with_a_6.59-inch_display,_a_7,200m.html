vivo’s latest V-series smartphone is finally here. The vivo V80 has launched in India with a 7,200mAh battery, a compact 6.59 inch display, and a Zeiss-backed triple-rear camera setup.

The vivo V80 is powered by the Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 chipset and sports a 6.59-inch AMOLED display that offers up to 144Hz refresh rate, Full-HD+ resolution, and up to 5,000 nits of peak brightness.

For photography, the vivo V80 features a Zeiss triple-rear camera setup that includes a 50MP Sony LYT 700V main sensor with OIS and autofocus. It supports 4K cinematic and 4K 60fps video recording.

There’s also a 50MP Sony IMX882 telephoto camera with OIS and 3x optical zoom support, alongside an 8MP ultrawide camera. At the front, the phone gets a 50MP wide camera with autofocus.

The handset packs a 7,200mAh battery with support for 90W wired charging. It’s one of the first phones to run Android 17-based OriginOS 7 out-of-the-box.

The vivo V80 also gets an in-display fingerprint scanner, an IP69 dust and water resistance rating, dual-band Wi-Fi, Bluetooth 5.4, GPS, NFC, and also gets eSIM support.

vivo is offering the latest V series smartphone in Horizon Blue, Stellar Black, and Sunrise Anthem color options in India. The phone uses an aluminum frame and a glass back. It measures 7.79mm thick and weighs 210g
