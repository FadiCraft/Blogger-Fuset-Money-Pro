This week's most popular article isn't about phones, it's about Instagram. It seems like the latest Instagram update got people's attention and introduced a feature that people have been waiting for for years - the ability to edit stories after posting them. You can change text, music, stickers, and drawings. Of course, there's a catch. You can edit stories only 15 minutes after posting them.

More details about the upcoming anniversary iPhone surfaced, suggesting that Apple is readying four models in the lineup, two of which will feature quad-curved glass. The new iPhone 20 Pro models are also tipped to feature new camera sensors and a better ISP.

Xiaomi 18 Pro series finally hit the global stage, and although it was well received by users and critics alike, the price tags in Europe are a bit steep.

We also dropped GSMArena's Awards 2026 winners this week, naming the Oppo Find X10 Pro Max the Phone of the Year 2026. The vivo X300 Ultra took the Best Camera, while the Xiaomi 18 Pro took the Best Compact award. Go check them out.

As the launch approaches, there's no shortage of rumors about the upcoming Samsung Galaxy S27 lineup each week. This one, we got to see the potential camera island design of the S27 Pro and S27 Ultra models. In other Samsung-related news, some insiders suggest that the Korean tech giant will beat Apple to market with a bezel-less phone, meaning we will likely get to see the phone before the traditional September iPhone launch next year.

We had another phone launch this week besides the Xiaomi 18 Pro series, and that's the Oppo Reno17 and Reno17 Pro. But while the Xiaomis got their global release, the Oppos debuted in China, so the global rollout will likely have to wait for next year.

Last but not least, we got to see something new on the smartphone scene, and that's Huawei's new sub-brand named Osta. The first handset under the Osta brand is the 1 Pro and features an unusually wide design that looks like a mini tablet of sorts. Then again, the device seems like a rebrand of the Huawei Pura X View, which launched back in September.
