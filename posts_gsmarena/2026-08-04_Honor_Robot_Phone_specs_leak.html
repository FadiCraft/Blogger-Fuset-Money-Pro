Honor's long-awaited Robot Phone is finally going to be fully unveiled on August 12, and the company has already confirmed details about its cameras in late July.

Today a new leak from Digital Chat Station on Weibo reveals the device's full specs, and the brand itself has published the teaser images you can see below. The Robot Phone will allegedly have a 6.3-inch flat LTPO OLED screen with 1216x2640 resolution, a whopping 6,800-nit peak brightness, and an anti-reflective coating.


Honor Robot Phone teaser images

It will be powered by the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 SoC, and will have a 7,060 mAh battery with support for 120W wired charging. There will also be three self-developed Honor chips inside, the C1, the E2, and the H1. The C1 is a radio frequency enhancement chip, the E2 is an energy management chip, and the H1 is an imaging chip.

On the rear will be a 200MP f/1.6 main gimbal camera using a 1/1.28" type sensor, and a 200MP f/2.6 periscope telephoto camera with 2.7x optical zoom, using a 1/1.4" type sensor. The Robot Phone will be offered with 12GB of RAM and 512GB of storage or 16GB of RAM and 1TB of storage.
