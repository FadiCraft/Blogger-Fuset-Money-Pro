Oppo unveiled the Find X10, Find X10 E, and Find X10 Pro Max in China yesterday. While the devices are not yet official for global markets, Oppo has already started taking blind pre-orders for them in the UK.

The company is basically offering you the opportunity to save either £150 on the Find X10 or £200 on the Find X10 Pro Max. On top of that, you'll also get your choice between a free Hasselblad teleconverter kit, a free Oppo Watch X3, or a free Oppo Pad 5. All you need to do is put down a £49 deposit and wait for the devices to launch in the UK.

There's not even an officially confirmed date for that yet, so you may be waiting a long time. But if you're willing to take that risk, you are getting a lot in return. Oppo is even guaranteeing that if the Black Friday prices are cheaper than you got them, it will refund you the difference.

Oppo's official UK store also reveals that the Find X10 will be available in Glacier Blue, Mist Grey, and Glow Coral with 12GB of RAM and 256/512GB of storage. The Find X10 Pro Max will be available in Titanium Grey and Luna White with 12GB of RAM and 512GB of storage only.
