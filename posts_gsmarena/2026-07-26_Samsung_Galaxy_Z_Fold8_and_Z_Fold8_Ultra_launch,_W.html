Samsung unveiled its 2026 foldable lineup and the book-style family now features two distinct models - the shorter and wider (4:3) Galaxy Z Fold8, aimed at media consumption and everyday usability. The Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra retains the taller, productivity-focused design with upgraded cameras and a larger battery. Both are powered by the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5, but the Ultra is positioned as Samsung's premium foldable for power users. Pre-orders are now open, with global availability starting August 7. Pricing starts at €1,999/$1,899.99 for the Galaxy Z Fold8 and €2,200/$2,099.99 for the Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra.

The Galaxy Watch Ultra2 finally arrived with a thinner design despite packing a much larger 800mAh battery, while retaining its single 47mm size and titanium construction. Alongside it, Samsung introduced the Galaxy Watch9, available in 40mm and 44mm sizes with modest battery upgrades, brighter displays, the new Snapdragon Wear Elite chipset, and expanded AI-powered health tracking features. Both smartwatches are available for pre-order now, with general sales beginning August 7. The Classic model will take a year off.
