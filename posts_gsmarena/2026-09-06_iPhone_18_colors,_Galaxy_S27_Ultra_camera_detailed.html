The iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max could get a completely refreshed color palette this year. A new leak claims Apple will offer the pair in Dark Cherry, Sky Blue, and Black, with Dark Cherry expected to replace Cosmic Orange as the headline finish. The wine-like red/purple shade has appeared repeatedly in previous leaks, while Sky Blue should be a much subtler, almost silvery option.

Perhaps more interestingly, the leak claims Silver won’t make the cut, despite earlier reports and leaked dummy units pointing to a silver version. Black, meanwhile, would make its return after being conspicuously absent from the iPhone 17 Pro lineup. There’s still disagreement among leakers over whether Black or Silver will ultimately be the third option, so we’ll have to wait for Apple’s September event for confirmation.

The Galaxy S27's zoom camera could be a 4x unit with a half-inch (1/1.9") sensor - a middle-ground between the current 3x + 5x, and one with a technically larger sensor than both. It's similar to the one on the iPhone 17 Pro models, though technically larger than that as well - 1/1.9"-1/2.0" > 1/2.55", at least size-wise (though in practice it's much closer).

Poco unveiled its new F9 Pro and F9 Ultra, and both are unapologetically spec-heavy. The pair gets 185Hz AMOLED displays, 200MP main cameras, 100W wired and 50W wireless charging, IP68 protection, and Bose-tuned stereo speakers. The F9 Pro uses the slightly cut-down Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 V, while the Ultra gets the full Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 and adds a dedicated rear subwoofer for a 2.1-channel speaker setup.

The Ultra is the real monster, pairing its chipset with a huge 8,050mAh battery, a 6.9-inch display and a camera system comprising the 200MP main, 50MP ultrawide and 50MP 5x periscope. The F9 Pro is smaller at 6.59 inches and 6,330mAh, and swaps those secondary cameras for a 50MP 2.5x telephoto and 8MP ultrawide. There's also some visual flair: the Ultra has a notification light around its rear speaker, while the Pro's Aurora Green finish can actually glow in the dark.

Pricing is considerably less "Poco" than it used to be. The F9 Pro starts at £799, while the F9 Ultra starts at £899, although early-bird pricing brings those down to £639 and £709, respectively.
