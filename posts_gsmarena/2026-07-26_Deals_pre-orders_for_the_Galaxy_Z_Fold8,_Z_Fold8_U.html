While the Z Flip7 has disappeared from store shelves, the Galaxy Z Fold7 is still around. Compared to the Z Fold8 Ultra, you miss out on the battery upgrade – the new model has a 5,000mAh battery, up from 4,400mAh on this one. And it has faster wired (45W vs. 25W) and wireless charging (20W vs. 15W). The dimensions are essentially the same (the new one is a tad thinner when opened, but it’s still 8.9mm when closed). They weigh the same too.

The new model has a fancy new Flex Titanium structure, which promises to be more durable, plus an anti-reflective coating for the inner display. Only the ultra-wide camera got upgraded, it now has a 50MP sensor (up from 12MP). And there’s the new Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 for Galaxy chipset, of course (up from the previous 8 Elite for Galaxy).

Samsung barely bothered to improve the cameras on the Z8 foldables. The Z Fold8 Ultra has a 200MP main (and so does the Z Fold7) and a 50MP ultra-wide, but the 10MP 3x telephoto module will not win any competitions. The Z Fold8 only has a 50MP main and 50MP ultra-wide and the Z Flip8 has it the worst with a 50MP main and 12MP ultra-wide (neither of these two has a tele lens).

You can also have a look at the Xiaomi 17T Pro – its camera is not as capable as that of the vivo, but this one is cheaper while packing the same Dimensity 9500 chipset. The Xiaomi 17T hasn’t gotten its update yet, but it’s worth a look too. It is smaller with a 6.59” display and a 6,500mAh battery (67W wired-only charging).
