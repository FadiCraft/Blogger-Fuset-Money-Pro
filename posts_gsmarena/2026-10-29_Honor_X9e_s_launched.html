Honor’s latest addition to its X-series is now official with the Honor X9e s. The device is already available across the Middle East with a starting price of SAR 1,499 ($400) for the single 8/256GB trim. It may look familiar, and there’s good reason why: the X9e s is a rebadged Honor Turbo, which came out back in August.

Honor X9e s

The X9e s comes in the same Dawn Orange, Midnight Black and Glacier White colors. It’s built around a 6.79 AMOLED screen with FHD+ resolution, a 120Hz refresh rate and 1,800 nits peak brightness. You also get Qualcomm’s Snapdragon 6 Gen 4 chip with 8GB RAM and 256GB storage.

The back houses a 50MP main cam and a 5MP ultrawide lens. The software side is covered by MagicOS 10 based on Android 16, while the battery comes in at 8560 mAh and supports 80W charging.
