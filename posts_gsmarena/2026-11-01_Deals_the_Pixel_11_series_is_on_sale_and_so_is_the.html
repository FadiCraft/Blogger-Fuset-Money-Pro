Now that the foldable fever is dying down following the iPhone Duo launch, let’s focus on slab phones this week. Surprisingly, Pixels are on sale – Google phones rarely get discounts worth mentioning, but this week is an exception.

The Google Pixel 11 Pro starts at $800 with a solid 27% price cut. The upgrade from 12GB of RAM and 256GB storage to 16/512GB costs $120 – note that this year, the 256GB Pro and Pro XL models have less RAM than the higher storage tiers. This wasn’t the case with previous generations. That aside, the new model brings decent upgrades over its predecessor – better battery life with faster wireless charging, an improved (but still flawed) chipset and 4K60 HDR video recording.

The Google Pixel 11 Pro Fold is on sale – we don’t see that too often. With a 13% discount on the base model, it’s $250 cheaper than the base Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra. The chipset situation is again heavily in favor of the Galaxy. The Samsung phone has a better main camera (200MP 1/1.3 vs. 48MP 1/1.56”), but its telephoto lens is shorter (10MP 3x/69mm vs. 10.8MP 5x/112mm).
