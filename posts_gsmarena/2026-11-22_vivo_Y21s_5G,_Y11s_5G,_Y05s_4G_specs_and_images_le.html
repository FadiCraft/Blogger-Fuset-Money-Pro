Vivo will apparently be flushing out its budget lineup in India soon with at least three new devices. These are not particularly exciting in any meaningful way, but might just offer a good value proposition. There are some clear similarities between the three. All of them are built around a 6.74-inch, 1600 × 720, 120Hz LCD and have a 6,500 mAh battery.

The vivo Y21s 5G is the highest-end of the trio. It is rocking a MediaTek Dimensity 6300 chipset with either 4GB or 6GB of RAM. Storage options include 64GB and 128GB. Interestingly enough, storage is expandable via a microSD slot. The camera setup is kind of underwhelming, with a 50MP main rear camera and a 0.08MP complementary sensor of some sort, likely a depth module, plus a 5MP on the front. The phone can charge at up to an advertised 44W. There is a side fingerprint reader, and the phone has an IP65 ingress protection rating.


vivo Y21s 5G

The vivo Y11s 5G is kind of a toned-down version of the Y21s. You get the same LCD, the same MediaTek Dimensity 6300 chipset, the 6500 mAh battery, side-mounted fingerprint reader and microSD slot. The camera setup is downgraded a bit with a 13MP main camera, plus the depth sensor and the 5MP selfie. Charging speed is down to 15W. The Y11s 5G is only available in one memory version: 4GB + 128GB.


vivo Y11s 5G

Just in case these model numbers sound familiar, it’s not just you. Indeed, for some reason, vivo is kind of reusing model names. Those older models were 4G, though, while these are 5G, so there is at least a relatively easy distinction. Still, vivo could have mixed things up a bit.


vivo Y05s

Finally, there is the Y05s 4G. This one does have a unique model name. Rumors say that this one didn’t even bother with a second camera sensor on the back. You just get the one 8MP shooter and what is apparently a totally fake second camera bump for aesthetics. 5MP selfie once again, a side-mounted fingerprint reader and an IP65 ingress protection rating. You get a UNISOC T7225 chipset with this one and a single 4GB + 64GB memory variant, plus expandable storage.
