Realme today unveiled the Realme 16x as the newest member of the Realme 16 series.

One of the headline features of the Realme 16x is its 7,000 mAh battery with 45W charging support. It also supports all-scenario bypass charging and 6.5W wired reverse charging.

Another headline feature of the Realme 16x is its 144Hz LCD, measuring 6.81" diagonally. It has a 1,570x720-pixel resolution, 1,200 nits peak brightness, and is protected by Panda-1681 glass.

The Realme 16x is powered by the Dimensity 6300 SoC, paired with up to 6GB of LPDDR4X RAM. The smartphone has up to 256GB of onboard storage and runs Android 16-based Realme UI 7.0 out of the box. Realme has promised two years of Android OS upgrades and three years of security updates for the 16x.

For photography, the Realme 16x has two usable cameras - an 8MP selfie camera on the front and a 50MP primary camera on the rear. The camera island on the rear also features a Pulse Light for notifications.

Other highlights of the Realme 16x include a side-mounted fingerprint scanner, MIL-STD-810H certification, 5,300 mm2 VC cooling, and an IP65 rating. The smartphone can also be operated with wet hands or when wearing gloves.


Realme 16x's color options

The Realme 16x is offered in Glory White and Endurance Brown colors with three memory options - 4GB/128GB, 6GB/128GB, and 6GB/256GB, priced at INR25,999, INR27,999, and INR30,999, respectively. It will go on sale in India starting August 13 through Realme's Indian website, Flipkart, and mainline stores.
