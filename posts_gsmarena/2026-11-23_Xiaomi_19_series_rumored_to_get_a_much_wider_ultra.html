Xiaomi took the Xiaomi 18 Pro and 18 Pro Max global last week, but the rumor mill has already moved on to the Xiaomi 19 series. Today, Digital Chat Station on Weibo is claiming that the phones coming in 2027 should get a "true" ultrawide camera. According to him, the 50MP OmniVision OV51C sensor will be used, paired with a roughly 14mm lens and a field of view of around 114°.

This would be a very welcome development for Xiaomi, since its ultrawides aren't exactly known for being very wide. Case in point: the Xiaomi 18 Pro has a 50MP 1/2.87" ultrawide with a 17mm lens and a 102° field of view. DCS added that he's also seen a new periscope and main camera that are probably for the next generation, but didn’t go into any more details.

Xiaomi 18 Pro

When asked why the new ultrawide doesn't use a 200MP sensor, DCS replied that it's already pretty good, since at least it's actually ultrawide. He also said the vivo X500 Ultra, with its Lytia 910 ultrawide, will easily beat it.

As for whether Xiaomi might bring back its Ultra model after the 18 Ultra was reportedly canceled, the tipster says there's no trace of a 19 Ultra in development for now.
