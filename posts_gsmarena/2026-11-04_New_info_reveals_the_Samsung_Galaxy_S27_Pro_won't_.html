There's been a lot of talk lately about the chipset powering the Samsung Galaxy S27 Ultra, with a recent rumor claiming the smartphone would come with the Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6, at least in Europe. Now, the entire S27 lineup has been spotted in the NBD database, with the S27 Ultra listed with the Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6.

The Galaxy S27 and Galaxy S27+ are listed with Samsung's Exynos 2700 SoC, while the Galaxy S27 Pro is listed with the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6 chip instead of Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6. Note that these are European models, and we'll need to wait for information from multiple sources to confirm which chipsets will power the Galaxy S27 lineup in other regions.

Samsung Galaxy S27 Pro's leaked CAD render (Source: OnLeaks and Android Headlines)
