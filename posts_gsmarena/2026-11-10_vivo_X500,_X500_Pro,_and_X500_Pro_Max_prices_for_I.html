vivo launched the X500, X500 Pro, and X500 Pro Max in China in September and then they went global last month, but they're still not officially available in India. That will change soon, with one rumor claiming the launch will happen over there towards the end of this month.

While we wait, a tipster on X has purportedly revealed the devices' "tentative" launch prices for the Indian market. The X500 will allegedly go for INR 119,999 (approximately $1,280) with 12GB of RAM and 256GB of storage. The X500 Pro with the same configuration is said to cost INR 149,999 ($1,600), while the X500 Pro Max will go up to INR 159,999 ($1,706).

The X500 Pro Max with 512GB of storage will be priced at INR 179,999 ($1,920), while the X500 Pro Max "master set" will go for a whopping INR 229,999 ($2,453). The X500 will be available in black and red, while the Pro and Pro Max will come in orange and black. The tipster in question also says there will be exclusive offline discounts to make the prices slightly more palatable. Anyway, what do you think of these prices? Too high?
