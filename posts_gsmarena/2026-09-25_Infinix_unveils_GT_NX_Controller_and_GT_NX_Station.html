Infinix has now launched the GT NX Controller and the GT NX Station. The former is designed to be able to replicate "the nuanced precision" of touchscreen operation, featuring "the world's first Pixel-Level FPS Precision Touchpad" in a mobile gaming controller, delivering "mouse-like accuracy for mobile FPS titles".

The GT NX Controller has an "esports-grade" three-chip low-latency architecture with "millisecond-level" Direct Connect. There are two chips in the left handle and one in the right, which ensure that "every input stays responsive and consistent".

The controller has 22 hours of battery life and supports three-level haptic intensity adjustment in XArena, which Infinix describes as "handheld-console-grade". The ergonomic anti-slip design delivers "enhanced comfort" during extended gaming sessions. The NX Controller can be used with phone clamping, desktop clamping, and portable single-hand play.

The Infinix GT NX Station is a 9-in-1 expansion dock with built-in TEC cooling, which is of course designed to avoid thermal throttling triggered by overheating. It supports Universal Extend or Mirror on any compatible screen.

The station has Dynamic RGB lighting, and packs an HDMI port, RJ45 Ethernet, 2 USB-A ports, two USB-C ports (one with PD input), a USB-C host cable, a microSD card slot, and a 3.5mm audio jack.
