The Oppo Find X10 and Find X10 Pro Max, launched in India last week, go on sale today via Oppo's Indian website, Amazon.in, Flipkart, and mainline retail outlets.

The Oppo Find X10 Pro Max comes in Luna White and Titanium Grey in a single 12GB/512GB configuration, priced at INR199,999.


Oppo Find X10 Pro Max's color options

The Oppo Find X10, on the other hand, has three color options - Glacier Blue, Mist Grey, and Glow Coral. It comes in two memory configurations - 12GB/256GB and 12GB/512GB, priced at INR139,999 and INR169,999, respectively.


Oppo Find X10's color options

The Oppo Hasselblad Teleconverter Kit is sold separately for INR24,999.

The Oppo Find X10 is powered by the Dimensity 9600M SoC, while the Find X10 Pro Max has the Dimensity 9600 Pro chip at the helm. Another major difference is that the Find X10 Pro Max features three 200MP cameras. However, both smartphones pack 8,000 mAh batteries in India.
