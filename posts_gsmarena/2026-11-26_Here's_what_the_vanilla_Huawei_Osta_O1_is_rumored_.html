Huawei launched its Osta sub-brand last week with the Osta O1 Pro, which is essentially a rebadged Pura X View. That won't be the only phone in the lineup for long, though. Today Digital Chat Station on Weibo has shared some details about the vanilla Osta O1.

According to DCS, the Osta O1 will have a 7-inch "1.5K" screen that's curved equally on all four sides, with a Kirin 9020-series chipset at the helm. He describes it as a slim, big-screen handset and says you can think of it as something like the Mate Air. The phone has apparently already been registered and is now waiting to be released.

Huawei Mate 90 Air

If that sounds familiar, it's because Huawei recently announced the Mate 90 Air with a 7-inch 1320x2760 OLED screen, the Kirin 9020 chipset, and a 6.6mm thin body. So it looks like Osta could be doing the rebranding thing again, this time with a Mate.

The main difference we know of so far is the quad-curved screen, and an earlier leak also mentioned a large circular camera island on the back. When a commenter asked why Osta would need such a phone now that the Mate 90 Air is out, DCS simply replied that the brand is expanding.
