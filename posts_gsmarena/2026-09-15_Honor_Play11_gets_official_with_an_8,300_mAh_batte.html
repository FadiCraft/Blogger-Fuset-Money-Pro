Honor's increasingly vast Play11 family has added a new member today, namely the 'vanilla' Honor Play11. This device comes with a 6.872-inch LCD screen with 720x1592 resolution, a 120Hz refresh rate, and 1,020-nit peak brightness.

On the rear there's a 50MP camera with f/1.8 aperture, while on the front there’s a 5MP selfie shooter. The handset is powered by the Snapdragon 4 Gen 4 SoC, paired with 6/8GB of RAM and 128/256GB of storage.


Honor Play11 official images

Keeping the lights on is an 8,300 mAh battery with support for 45W fast wired charging and 7.5W reverse wired charging. The Honor Play11 runs Android 16 with MagicOS 10 on top.

It's IP68, IP69, and IP69K rated for dust and water resistance and was tested to survive drops from up to 2.5m. It has a side-mounted fingerprint scanner embedded in the power button. The Play11 is offered in Desert Gold, Midnight Black, Meteor Silver, and Mist Purple.

With 6GB of RAM and 128GB of storage, its normal price is listed as CNY 1,499 ($223). With 8GB of RAM and 128GB of storage, the normal price is CNY 1,699 ($253). The top version with 8GB of RAM and 256GB of storage is priced at CNY 1,999 ($297). You can currently buy all of them for CNY 100 ($14) off.
