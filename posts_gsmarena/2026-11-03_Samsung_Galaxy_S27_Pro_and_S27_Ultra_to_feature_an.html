Samsung launched the Galaxy S26 Ultra earlier this year with a Privacy Display feature, and next year's Galaxy S27 Pro and Galaxy S27 Ultra are rumored to have it, too. A new report by SamMobile corroborates those rumors and claims these two S27 series smartphones will feature an upgraded version of Privacy Display.

The source also says that the Galaxy S27 Pro and Galaxy S27 Ultra will have enhanced display brightness, making their screens easier to read under direct sunlight.

Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra with Privacy Display

Samsung was previously said to launch the Galaxy S27 and Galaxy S27+ with Privacy Display as well, but multiple reports say that won't be the case.
