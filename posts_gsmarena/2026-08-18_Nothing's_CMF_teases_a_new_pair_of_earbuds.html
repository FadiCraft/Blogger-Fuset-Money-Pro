Nothing's sub-brand CMF may not be launching a new smartphone this year, but it is still busy working on other products. And it now looks like the next one we're getting will be a new pair of earbuds. At least that's what the company's new teaser, seen below, strongly hints at.

That definitely looks like a stem-style earbud to us, with an overall white/silver color scheme and an orange tip.

The CMF Buds Pro 2 were launched in 2024, while the Buds 2 and Buds 2 Plus came out last year, so it's definitely not out of the question that these would be the CMF Buds 3, or Buds 3 Pro, or Buds 3 Plus, or something like that.

We're sure CMF will keep teasing them in the coming days and weeks and will eventually reveal exactly what they are, so stay tuned for that.
