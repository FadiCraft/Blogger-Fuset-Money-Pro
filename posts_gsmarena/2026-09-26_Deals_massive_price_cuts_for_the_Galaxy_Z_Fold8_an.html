The Samsung Galaxy Z Flip8 is a third off. With both Google and now Apple focusing on horizontal foldables, there aren’t many flips even in Europe (the Chinese makers are pretty lukewarm on flips too). Compared to its predecessor, the 2026 Flip brings a new chipset (Exynos 2600 vs. 2500), but not much else. It has a Si/C battery, but it has the same capacity as before, 4,300mAh, and is rated at the same 25W charging. Official numbers aside, in our review we found that the new model lasts longer on a charge and then charges faster. Samsung didn’t do much to fix the thermal throttling, though.
