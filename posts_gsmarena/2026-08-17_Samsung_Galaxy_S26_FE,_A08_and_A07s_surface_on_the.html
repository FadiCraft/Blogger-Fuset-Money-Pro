After clearing the FCC, the Samsung Galaxy S26 FE is now past another major hurdle – it is now listed on the Google Play Console. Two entry-level models joined it, the Galaxy A08 and A07s.

The Galaxy S26 FE specs leaked in detail last week. The console confirms the chipset as S5E9955, aka the Exynos 2500. The phone (SM-S741U) is configured with 8GB of RAM and a 1,080 x 2,340px display as expected.


Samsung Galaxy S26 FE image and specs from the Google Play Console

The GPC offered one image of the phone, but you can see more of the Galaxy S26 FE in these leaked renders, which also show several color options. There’s still no word on when Samsung will make the S26 FE official.

Next up is the Samsung Galaxy A08 (SM-A085F). This model uses the MediaTek MT6789V/CD, aka the Helio G99. This LTE-only chip is already used in the Galaxy A07 4G and it looks like it will be featured on the upcoming Galaxy A18 4G as well. Curiously, the GPC claims that the A08 will have up to 8GB of RAM – this matches the A07, sure, but that model was released before the RAMpocalypse. How much does 8GB of LPDDR4X RAM cost these days, anyway?


Samsung Galaxy A08 image and specs from the Google Play Console

Then there’s the Galaxy A07s (SM-A077M), which features the same Helio G99 chip, though with only 4GB of RAM. Both the A08 and A07s will feature 720 x 1,600px displays. This is the norm for the A0 family. If you want more, you may have to look at that A18 model.


Samsung Galaxy A07s image and specs from the Google Play Console

The Galaxy A07 4G was unveiled in late August last year, so its successor might arrive soon. The Galaxy S25 FE was a September release, however, so FE fans might have to settle in for a wait.
