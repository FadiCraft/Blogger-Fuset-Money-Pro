Huawei will be holding its next product launch event on September 2 in Munich, Germany. The keynote features the “Chase the wild” tagline, and we’re expecting to see the latest crop of wearables from the brand.

Huawei recently unveiled the Watch GT 7 series in China so it seems like the perfect time to bring the Watch GT 7 and GT 7 Pro to the global stage. Huawei has historically also introduced new wireless earbuds alongside its smartwatches, so we’ll have to wait and see if that will be the case this time.
