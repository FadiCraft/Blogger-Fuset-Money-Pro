According to a new report, Oppo is planning to launch the Find X10 and Find X10 Pro Max in global markets "by late October". The devices are first expected to be unveiled in China in September. Over in India, the official announcement is allegedly going to take place before Diwali, which is in early November.

Last year, the Find X9 and Find X9 Pro landed in India in mid-November, after having been unveiled in China and some European markets in October.

Oppo Find X9 Pro

According to the source of this rumor, the Find X10 Pro Max will launch at around INR 1,55,000 (approximately $1,620 at the current exchange rate). The vanilla Find X10 will be priced at around INR 90,000 ($940).

The Find X10 Pro Max is shaping up to be a camera monster, with three 200MP shooters on its back - the main one (using a new Samsung ISOCELL HPC sensor with DeepPix technology), a 200MP ultrawide, and a 200MP periscope telephoto camera. It will be powered by the MediaTek Dimensity 9600 Pro chipset, with an 8,000 mAh battery supporting 100W wired charging.
