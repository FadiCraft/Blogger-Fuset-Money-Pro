A few hours ago a leak talked about the battery capacities of the upcoming Poco F9 Pro and Poco F9 Ultra, and now we have a mega leak with renders and specs for both devices.

As expected, the Poco F9 Pro is a rebranded Redmi K100 Pro with a smaller battery, and the Poco F9 Ultra is a rebranded Redmi K100 Pro Max with a smaller battery. Otherwise, the specs are identical to their Chinese counterparts. The F9 Pro has a 6,330 mAh battery and the F9 Ultra has an 8,050 mAh battery (compared to 8,580 mAh and 9,070 mAh on the Redmi K100 Pro and Pro Max, respectively).


Poco F9 Pro leaked images

The Poco F9 Pro has a 6.59-inch 1156x2510 AMOLED touchscreen with a 185Hz refresh rate and 4,500-nit peak brightness, the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 SoC at the helm, 12/16GB of RAM, 256/512GB of storage, a 200MP main camera with OIS, a 50MP telephoto camera with 2.5x optical zoom, an 8MP ultrawide, and a 32MP selfie camera.

The Poco F9 Ultra, on the other hand, has a 6.9-inch 1200x2608 AMOLED touchscreen with the same refresh rate and peak brightness, the same chipset and RAM/storage options, the same main camera, a 50MP periscope telephoto camera with 5x optical zoom, and a 50MP ultrawide.


Poco F9 Ultra leaked images

Both phones have Gorilla Glass 7i on their screens, metal frames, and a fiberglass-reinforced plastic back. Both devices have Bose speakers, and the Ultra even gets an LED ring for notifications. The Poco F9 Pro and F9 Ultra are going to be IP69 certified for dust and water resistance. Both will charge at 100W.

The Poco F9 Pro will allegedly cost "at least" €799, while the Poco F9 Ultra will apparently go for "at least" €999. We've put "at least" in quotes because this is how the source phrased the pricing information, which makes us think it's more of an educated guess than an actual leak.
