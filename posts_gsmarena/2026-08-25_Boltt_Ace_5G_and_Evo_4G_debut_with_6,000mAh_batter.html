India-based smartwatch brand Fire-Boltt has officially entered the smartphone market with the launch of the Boltt Ace 5G and Boltt Evo 4G budget phones in India. The two phones share several specs, run stock Android 16, and are available in multiple colour options.

The Boltt Ace 5G features a 6.79-inch LCD display with a 120Hz refresh rate and up to 600 nits of peak brightness.

Under the hood, it is powered by a Unisoc T8200 chipset, paired with up to 6GB of RAM and 128GB of internal storage.

For photography, the phone features a 64MP primary rear camera and an 8MP selfie camera.

The handset packs a 6,000mAh battery with support for 18W fast charging. It runs stock Android 16 out of the box and supports NFC, Bluetooth 5.0, Wi-Fi, and GPS.

The Boltt Ace 5G is available in Arctic White, Burgundy Bliss, Forest Green, Lavender Bloom, Midnight Black, and Sky Blue color options.

The Boltt Evo 4G, meanwhile, is a 4G smartphone that shares the Ace 5G’s display and battery specs and also runs Android 16.

However, it comes with a less powerful chipset and a more basic camera setup. The phone is powered by a Unisoc T7250 chipset, paired with up to 6GB of RAM and 128GB of storage.

For photography, the Boltt Evo features a 50MP primary rear camera paired with a 2MP depth sensor, while an 8MP camera handles selfies.

Unlike the Ace 5G, the Evo 4G does not support NFC. Other connectivity options remain the same, including Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, and GPS.

The Boltt Evo 4G is available in Arctic White, Berry Red, Lavender Bloom, Midnight Black, and Sky Blue colorways.

As for pricing, the Boltt Ace 5G starts at INR 13,999 for the 4GB/64GB variant. The 6GB/128GB and 8GB/128GB options are priced at INR 14,999 and INR 15,999, respectively.

The Boltt Evo 4G starts at INR 10,999 for the 4GB/64GB variant, while the 6GB/128GB model costs INR 11,999.

Both smartphones will go on sale starting September 1 via Flipkart.
