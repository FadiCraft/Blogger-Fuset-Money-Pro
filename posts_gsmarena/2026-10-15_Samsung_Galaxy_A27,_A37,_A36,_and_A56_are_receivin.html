After rolling out the Android 17-based One UI 9 stable update for the Galaxy S23 series, Galaxy Z Fold5, and Galaxy Z Flip5, Samsung has now released stable One UI 9 for the Galaxy A27, Galaxy A37, Galaxy A36, and Galaxy A56.

The stable One UI 9 update for the Galaxy A27 comes with firmware version A276KKSU3BZIF, while the Galaxy A37 update has firmware version A376NKSU3BZI6.

Samsung Galaxy A27

The update for both smartphones is currently rolling out in South Korea, but it should expand to other regions soon.

If you live in South Korea and haven't received the One UI 9 stable update on your Galaxy A27/A37, you can check for it manually by heading to your phone's Settings > Software update menu.

We are waiting for more details about the rollout of stable One UI 9 for the Galaxy A36 and Galaxy A56. In the meantime, you can watch the video below to learn about One UI 9.
