Motorola finally made the Signature 27 fully official this week, revealing a 7,000mAh silicon-carbon battery with 90W wired and 50W wireless charging. The flagship is powered by the Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6 and features a 6.78-inch 120Hz tandem OLED display with up to 12,000 nits of peak brightness, alongside up to 16GB of RAM and 512GB of storage.

The camera setup includes a 50MP main camera with Sony's Lytia 910 sensor and LOFIC technology, a 200MP 3x periscope telephoto, and a 50MP ultrawide. There's also a dedicated EVS action sensor designed to help capture fast-moving subjects with less blur. Other highlights include Bang & Olufsen audio, IP68/IP69 protection, and Android 17 with seven promised major OS updates. The Signature 27 launches in Europe on November 16 for a pricey €1,899, although some markets will get it for €1,399.

Xiaomi confirmed that the Xiaomi 18 Pro and 18 Pro Max will make their global debut later this month, following their initial launch in China in September. The company hasn't announced an exact date yet, but recent listings from Italian retailer Epto have revealed potential European pricing, starting at €1,398.90 for the 18 Pro with 12GB of RAM and 256GB of storage, and €1,783.90 for the 18 Pro Max with 12GB of RAM and 512GB of storage.
