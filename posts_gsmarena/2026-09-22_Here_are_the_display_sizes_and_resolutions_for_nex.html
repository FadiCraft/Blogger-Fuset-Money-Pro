Apple just released the iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max on Friday, and today we already have a new rumor about their successors. These are tentatively being called iPhone 20 Pro and iPhone 20 Pro Max, since next year is the 20th anniversary of the original iPhone - which is why Apple is expected to skip the number 19.

Apple iPhone 18 Pro

Apple iPhone 18 Pro Max

So, according to Digital Chat Station on Weibo, the iPhone 20 Pro will have a 6.41-inch screen with 1236x2686 resolution, while the iPhone 20 Pro Max will have a 6.96-inch display with 1340x2912 resolution. Both of them should also have tiny bezels on all four sides, and a minimal punch-hole cutout for the selfie camera.

For comparison, note that the iPhone 18 Pro has a 6.3-inch screen with 1206x2622 resolution, while the iPhone 18 Pro Max has a 6.9-inch screen with 1320x2868 resolution. So both upcoming Pro iPhones will slightly increase their panel sizes and resolutions, but not by a lot.
