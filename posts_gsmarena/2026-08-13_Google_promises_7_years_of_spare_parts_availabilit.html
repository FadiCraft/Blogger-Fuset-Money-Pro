Google officially unveiled the Pixel 11 series at its Made by Google event. Google has now shared details about the availability of spare parts for the new smartphones.

At the Pixel 11 series launch event in India, Google reportedly confirmed that it will provide 7 years of spare parts support for the new smartphones.

Google says spares will be available in every market where the Pixel 11 series is sold, making it easier for users to replace components such as the battery or display even years after buying the phone.

One of the first components to degrade over time is the battery, which can lose a significant portion of its original capacity after a few years of regular use. Having access to a replacement battery is therefore important.
