Honor has been pushing smartphone battery capacities higher and higher, and earlier this month we heard that its 14,000 to 15,000 mAh battery has entered trial production. Now, the same tipster, Digital Chat Station, says the company has a new X-series phone coming before the Chinese New Year, which is on February 6.

The device will reportedly be a mid-ranger with a battery capacity of around 13,500 mAh and a 6.96-inch or 7-inch screen, so its main selling point will obviously be long battery life. For context, the Honor Power2, which launched in January, has a 10,080 mAh battery.

Speaking of which, DCS says Honor is also speeding up the development of the Power3, which will have a new 7-inch screen, a huge battery, and a more powerful chipset. He didn't share the exact capacity this time, but back in July he said it would be at least 10,000 mAh.

The same post mentions two wide devices as well. Honor's wide foldable, which is rumored to be the Magic V7, will most likely launch after the Chinese New Year in early February, a bit later than the January timeline rumored back in October. Meanwhile, the wide phone with a regular, non-folding screen is tentatively planned for the first half of 2027.
