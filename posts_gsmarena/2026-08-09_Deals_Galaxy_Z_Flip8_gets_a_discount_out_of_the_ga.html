The Motorola Razr Fold goes up against the Z Fold8 Ultra. It’s thicker (10.1mm vs. 8.9mm) and heavier (243g vs. 215g) than the Galaxy, though it does have a larger battery (6,000mAh vs. 5,000mAh). And it shows – the Razr scored an Active Use Score of 16:03h, compared to 13:06h for the Galaxy. The Moto foldable is powered by the non-Elite Snapdragon 8 Gen 5.

While its main camera has 50MP resolution to Samsung’s 200MP, the sensor sizes are comparable (1/1.28” vs. 1/1.3”). And the 3x telephoto has a 50MP sensor, while Samsung is still using a 10MP sensor. Both phones can expect 7 years of OS updates, but the Galaxy launched with Android 17, while the Motorola comes with Android 16 out of the box.

By the way, the Razr 70 and 70+ above come with a free Moto Watch Fit and Moto Buds Loop. The same freebie pack is available for the Motorola Edge 70 Fusion. While the phone itself has no discount at the moment, its €350 price tag is already pretty reasonable.

That buys a good quality 6.78” OLED display (1272p+ resolution, 10-bit colors with HDR10+, 144Hz refresh rate), a Snapdragon 7s Gen 3 chipset and a 5,200mAh battery with fast 68W wired-only charging. It has stereo speakers (with Dolby Atmos), a 50MP main camera (1/1.56” sensor, OIS) and a 13MP ultra-wide. It’s nothing spectacular, but it’s a solid mid-ranger and the free watch and buds allow you to refresh your whole kit.
