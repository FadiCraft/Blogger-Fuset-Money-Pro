The Xiaomi Redmi Watch 6 Active and the Redmi Watch 6 Lite have leaked yet again – this time they broke cover as they surfaced on a price tracking site in Hungary.

The Redmi Watch 6 Lite has a rectangular 1.96” OLED display (410 x 502px) and a 470mAh battery that should last up to 18 days. The watch measures 39.4 x 44.5 x 9.9mm without the strap. The previous model, the Redmi Watch 5 Lite, measured 11.4mm thick and had the same battery capacity.

The body is rated for 5 ATM of water resistance and uses a proprietary charging connector. The watch has a heart rate sensor (with SpO2 and stress tracking), accelerometer, compass and light sensor. Bluetooth calling is supported and it sounds like there is an on-board GPS receiver.


Xiaomi Redmi Watch 6 Lite

The Xiaomi Redmi Watch 6 Lite is priced at just under HUF 25,000 on MarketWorld.hu, which works out to just under €70. The already official (and more capable) Redmi Watch 6 is HUF 38,000 without NFC and HUF 45,000 with NFC on the official Xiaomi online store for Hungary.

Meanwhile, neither of the two stores that list the Xiaomi Redmi Watch 6 Active have any specs for it. However, a previous leak did spill some of the beans.


Xiaomi Redmi Watch 6 Active in black

The Active has a 1.85” OLED display (with a lower 390 x 450px resolution) and the same 470mAh battery. It will have heart rate (with SpO2) measuring and over 140 sports modes.


Xiaomi Redmi Watch 6 Active in gray

This is a sequel to the Redmi Watch 5 Active and costs less than its Lite sibling at HUF 17,000 at MStore.hu. This converts to a bit over €45, which aligns with the previous leak’s price target of €50.
