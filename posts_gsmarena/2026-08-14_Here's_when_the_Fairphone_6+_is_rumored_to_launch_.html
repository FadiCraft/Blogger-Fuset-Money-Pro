According to a new rumor, the Fairphone 6+ will become official on August 18. It will be priced at around €685, based on a listing in Denmark. Of course, the price may slightly vary in other European markets because of different VAT rates. In fact, it may not be that bad in the Eurozone - a leak from France said the RRP would be €649.

That's €50 more than the Fairphone 6's launch price, and €100 more than it costs today. According to recent leaks, the Fairphone 6+ will have a 6.31-inch OLED screen with a 120Hz refresh rate, the Snapdragon 7s Gen 4 SoC at the helm, 12GB of LPDDR5 RAM, 256GB of expandable UFS 3.1 storage, a 50MP main camera with OIS, a 13MP ultrawide, a 32MP selfie camera, and a 4,415 mAh battery with support for 30W wired charging.

The Fairphone 6+ will allegedly be offered in a new Cobalt Blue color, which was leaked a few days ago (you can see it in the image above). Compared to the original Fairphone 6, which launched in June of last year, the 6+ has 50% more RAM, a newer chipset, and a new color. That's about it.
