Some iPad models have gotten plenty powerful in the last few years with the introduction of the in-house Apple M silicon, so users are now demanding a more powerful OS that can take advantage of the iPad's hardware capabilities. That doesn't mean Apple isn't improving iPadOS, but it's still not macOS-level.

Well, according to one of Mark Gurman's inside sources, Apple is already running macOS on some iPads internally. But don't hold your breath. The source clarifies that it's only for internal testing and Apple doesn't have plans to port macOS onto iPads anytime soon.

Instead, Apple is running macOS on iPads so that it can improve the iPadOS experience and also provide the same level of flexibility as macOS does.

The foldable, giant-sized iPad is still in the works, however, despite delays. It's going to be a 19-inch or 20-inch iPad with a foldable display without an external one like the iPhone Duo. Then again, the rising hardware cost and some yield issues may kill the so-called Project J312.
