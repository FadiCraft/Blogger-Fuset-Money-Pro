Samsung is working on a pair of open-ear clip-on wireless earbuds which are expected to launch as the Galaxy Buds On, and we now have our first official-looking renders complete with additional details thanks to a new report from SammyGuru.

Samsung Galaxy Buds On (images: SammyGuru)

Just as the previous renders show, Galaxy Buds On feature a two-part design with one side featuring the speaker and the other sitting behind the ear. The two sides are connected with a silicone loop, and we can also spot pogo-pin connectors, which will charge the buds when they are placed inside their case.


Galaxy Buds On renders inside the Galaxy Wearable app (images: SammyGuru)

Galaxy Buds On are shown in black, though we also get confirmation of a white version. In addition, we get some clarification on their supported features, which include automatic volume control that adjusts the volume based on your surroundings, as well as a volume boost feature.


Galaxy Buds On inside the Galaxy Wearable app (images: SammyGuru)

Samsung has also implemented gesture controls, which include a head nod as well as quick actions for starting voice recording and Galaxy AI. It remains to be seen when Samsung will introduce the Galaxy Buds On. Previous rumors speculated that they would launch alongside the Galaxy S26 FE, but that clearly did not pan out. The buds are now expected to launch in Q4 2026.
