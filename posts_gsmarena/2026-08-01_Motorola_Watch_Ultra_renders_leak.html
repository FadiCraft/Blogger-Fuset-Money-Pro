It seems like Motorola is now getting ready to enter the Ultra smartwatch fray, competing directly with the likes of Apple and Samsung. Today some renders showing what's purported to be the upcoming Motorola Watch Ultra have leaked.

As you can see, it has a circular screen, a large rotating crown at 10 o’clock with the Motorola logo on it, and a button at four o’clock. The printed text on the watch's rear confirms its 50m water resistance, IP68 dust and water resistance, as well as its 46mm stainless steel case, and the presence of GPS and LTE on board.

The leaked images show what might be a leather strap with red stitching, but of course Motorola could be offering the device with different strap options as well.

Unfortunately, it's unclear when this is scheduled to launch, and in which markets. But now that the leak floodgates have opened, we might get more information about the Motorola Watch Ultra in the near future, so stay tuned.
