Back in March, Digital Chat Station reported that the then-upcoming Huawei Enjoy 90 Pro Max would have an 8,500mAh battery. This leak was confirmed a few days later when Huawei made the Enjoy 90 series official. Now the leakster has the details for the next generation of Enjoy models.

The Huawei Enjoy 100 Pro Max will have an 8,800mAh battery (typical capacity, rated capacity will be 8,560mAh), according to DCS. This is a 300mAh upgrade over its predecessor. There’s no word on charging, however (the Enjoy 90 series offered 40W wired-only charging).

The Pro Max will feature an identical-sounding screen – a flat 6.84” 1.5K LTPS panel. It will upgrade to a new chipset, the Kirin 8020, and will support BeiDou satellite messaging, which was missing on the old Pro Max.

Digital Chat Station reveals specs for the Huawei Enjoy 100 series

Meanwhile, the smaller Huawei Enjoy 100 and Enjoy 100 Pro will have smaller 6,100mAh batteries. The Enjoy 90, 90 Plus and 90m Plus all had 6.67” screens and 6,620mAh batteries – we’re not sure what the cause of the smaller battery capacity is. The 90-series models also used Kirin 8000 chips (8000A for the vanilla model, 8000 for the other two) and that will remain true for the Enjoy 100 and 100 Pro. All three new models will ship with the latest HarmonyOS 7 out of the box.

DCS also reported on the color options for the upcoming phones. The Huawei Enjoy 100 Pro Max will be available in Obsidian Black, Snow White, Sand Gold and Azure Ocean. Meanwhile, the smaller Enjoy 100 Pro will come in Midnight Black, Feather White, Peacock Blue and Feature Purple. The Enjoy 100 will be available in Midnight Black, Glacier White, Cloud Blue and Danxia Orange.
