vivo is unveiling the X300 E next week and ahead of that the phone has been listed by China Telecom's website. This has revealed its main specs.

The device has the model number V2612A. It's powered by the Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 chipset and will be offered in three RAM/storage combos: 8/256GB, 12/256GB, and 12/512GB.

It has a 6.59-inch screen, a 50MP main camera as well as an additional 50MP camera and an 8MP camera on the back, and a 50MP selfie snapper. It will be available in Moonlight White, Sunlight Orange, and Midnight Black.

The vivo X300 E measures 157.52 x 74.33 x 7.99 mm and weighs 203g. It runs Android 16 with vivo's OriginOS 6 on top. It has a 7,200 mAh battery.
