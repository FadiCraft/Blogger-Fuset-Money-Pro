Motorola has put up a teaser page on Flipkart for the Motorola Edge 70 Neo in India. The company will officially launch the phone on November 24 - next Tuesday.

The images on the page show the phone in a sage-like hue, with a raised rounded-square camera island on the back and a curved display with a centered punch-hole up front. The camera ring reads "50MP", "Ultra HDR", "OIS", and "Lytia". Motorola is also highlighting AI photo editing done just by asking, with "add butterfly on shoulder" as the example prompt.


Motorola Edge 70 Neo teaser images

That 50MP marking matches a leak from last month, according to which the Edge 70 Neo will come with a 6.78-inch 144Hz AMOLED display, the Snapdragon 7s Gen 3 chipset, a 50MP Sony Lytia 710 main camera paired with a 2MP macro sensor, and a 32MP selfie shooter. The same leak mentioned a 7,000 mAh battery with 45W charging, IP64 dust and water resistance, Android 16, and four colors - Gray Lilac, Mountain View, Milky Blue, and Sage. That's at odds with the renders that surfaced in August, which showed a 200MP inscription on the camera island.

The Edge 70 Neo was first outed by a Motorola forum post that same month. The Neo has traditionally been the compact member of the Edge family, with the Edge 60 Neo sporting a 6.36-inch screen, but with a rumored 6.78-inch panel, that won't be the case this time around. Given its chipset, the newcomer should slot in below the Edge 70 Fusion in Motorola's Indian lineup, but its price is still unknown.
