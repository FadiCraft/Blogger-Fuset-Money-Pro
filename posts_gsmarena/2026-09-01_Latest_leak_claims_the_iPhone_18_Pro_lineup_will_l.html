There have been plenty of rumors surrounding the color options for the upcoming iPhone 18 Pro series, and now there’s another one to add to the list. The latest leak reiterates some previous rumors, but notes that the new iPhones will be available in three color options.

According to tipster Fixed Focus Digital, the iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max will be available in Dark Cherry/Burgundy, Sky Blue, and Black colorways.

The latest leak contradicts recent rumors that claimed the upcoming iPhones would also be offered in a silver colorway.

If this turns out to be accurate, Apple would be replacing all the color options from the iPhone 17 Pro series. The new Dark Cherry/Burgundy shade is said to serve as this year’s hero color, replacing last year’s Cosmic Orange.

The iPhone 18 Pro series is set to be unveiled at Apple’s upcoming event on September 9, alongside the company’s first foldable, the iPhone Ultra.
