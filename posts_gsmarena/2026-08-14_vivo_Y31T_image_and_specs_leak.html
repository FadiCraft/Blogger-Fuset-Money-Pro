vivo launched the Y31 in September of last year, and now it's allegedly working on the Y31T. This will apparently be launching in India soon. The image below purportedly shows its back.

The Y31T is said to come with a 6.75-inch LCD screen with an HD+ resolution and 1,250-nit peak brightness. It will be powered by the Snapdragon 4 Gen 2 chipset, and it will have UFS 2.2 storage.

The device will reportedly be offered in three configurations: 4/128GB, 6/128GB, and 6/256GB. It will have a 7,200 mAh battery with support for 44W fast charging. It will be offered in Crimson Red, Gold Mist, and Seashore Green colors.

On the rear will be a 50MP main camera and a 2MP decorative sensor of some sort, while for selfies there will be an 8MP front camera. The Y31T will be 8.39mm thick and will be IP66 and IP68 certified for dust and water resistance.

The Y31T will compete with the Oppo A6 5G, so expect its pricing to be in the same INR 26,999 (approximately $282) ballpark. Compared to the Y31, it has a slightly larger and brighter screen and a bigger battery.
