vivo launched the V80 Lite a few weeks ago, and now a new rumor tells us the company is, rather unsurprisingly, also working on the V80, the proper successor to the popular V70. This will allegedly launch in India in the first week of October.

It will come with a 7,200 mAh battery, which would be a notable improvement over the V70's 6,500 mAh cell. The device will also apparently feature an AI Creative Camera mode with over 30 AI effects.

This may be similar to the 25 styles that the X300 FE shipped with. There's also a Live Sticker Collage feature, which will let you create a collage using Live Photos and stickers.

The V80 is said to make its debut running Android 17 with vivo's OriginOS 7 on top, which is getting unveiled on September 16.
