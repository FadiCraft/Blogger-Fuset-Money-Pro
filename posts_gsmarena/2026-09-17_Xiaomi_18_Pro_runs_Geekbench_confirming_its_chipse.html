Yesterday Xiaomi finally started to officially tease the upcoming 18 Pro series, revealing that the Xiaomi 18 Pro and Xiaomi 18 Pro Max will be unveiled later this month (an exact date is yet to be announced).

Today a Xiaomi device powered by the upcoming Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6 Pro SoC was spotted in the Geekbench online database with the model number M154FF. That is a very new way for Xiaomi to do model numbers, by the way, as the company has so far been using a system that starts with the year and month. We're not saying the listing is fake, just that it's got a surprising model number.

As you can see, the prototype in question managed a single-core score of 2,871 and a multi-core score of 8,836 in Geekbench 7. As usual, you shouldn't take these numbers seriously, as prototypes can have unfinished software and hardware.

The listing reveals that the chipset's CPU will go up to 5.01GHz. The specific prototype which ran the benchmark had 16GB of RAM, but of course Xiaomi could offer more options. It seems like the Xiaomi 18 Pro series will launch with HyperOS 4 on board, which is based on Android 17. According to previous certifications, both the Xiaomi 18 Pro and the Xiaomi 18 Pro Max will support 100W wired charging.
