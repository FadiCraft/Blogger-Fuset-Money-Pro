Just yesterday, a leaked image of the Redmi K100 Pro surfaced online, showing the phone in a cherry red finish. Now, Xiaomi has officially revealed the K100 Pro Max design and confirmed its launch date.

The Redmi K100 Pro series will launch in China on August 11, the company confirmed in an official Weibo post.

In a separate post, Redmi shared official renders of the K100 Pro Max in a new Cabernet Red colorway, which matches the recently leaked image.

The company also confirmed that the K100 Pro Max will feature a flat 6.9-inch display with ultra-narrow bezels. The handset gets a curved metal frame and sports a design largely similar to its predecessor, the K90 Pro Max.

On the back, the Redmi K100 Pro Max features a triple-camera setup that includes a periscope telephoto unit. It also retains the Bose-powered rear woofer found on its predecessor.

The Redmi K100 Pro Max is already available for pre-reservations in China through Xiaomi's official online store.


Xiaomi President Lu Weibing also revealed in a Weibo post that the Redmi K100 Pro will be priced at around CNY 4,000. He added that the standard Redmi K100 will be unveiled at a later date.
