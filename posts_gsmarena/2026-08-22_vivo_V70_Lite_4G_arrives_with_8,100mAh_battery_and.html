The vivo V70 Lite was announced back in June, and its 4G counterpart has not been listed across several markets in Eurasia. As the name implies, this device features a 4G chipset – specifically Unisoc’s T7300. It is paired with 8GB of LPDDR4X RAM and 128/256GB of UFS 2.2 storage. The phone also brings an 8,100mAh battery with support for 44W wired charging.

V70 Lite 4G is built around a 6.83-inch AMOLED featuring 1,260 x 2,800px resolution and a 120Hz refresh rate. It also features an optical fingerprint scanner and a 32MP front-facing camera. The back houses a 50MP main cam and a 2MP depth sensor. The software side is covered by OriginOS 6 based on Android 16, and the device is also covered by IP69 ingress protection.
