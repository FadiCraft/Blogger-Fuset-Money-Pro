Honor launched the Magic9, Magic9 Super, and Magic9 Pro Max in China a few days ago. If the devices interest you, you may want to know when to expect them to arrive in Europe. Today we have the answer for the UK and France, officially revealed by Honor's website. Obviously, the rest of Europe is likely to get them at the same time.

Over in the UK and France, Honor says the Magic9 series is landing in January. That's quite the wait, no doubt. The company is showing the Magic9 Pro Max in Shadow Black, Moss Green, and Glacier Silver on its official website.

The device will be offered with 12GB of RAM and 256/512GB of storage. While the company is only showing the Pro Max, all of the wording on the website is about the "Magic9 series", so clearly there will be at least one other phone launching in the UK - most likely the 'vanilla' Magic9.

Prices aren't available yet, unsurprisingly since we're still so far from the release. Given how expensive flagship smartphones are becoming these days, if you're interested in the Magic9 series, you may want to start saving up. Anyway, you can register your interest in the Magic9 series on the Honor website if you want to be notified when the devices become available in Europe.
