Honor is set to unveil the Win2 series on October 21, and a tipster on X has now shared what look like its first Geekbench runs. The listings use the model number BRM-AN00 and put the Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6 at the helm, paired with 16GB of RAM in the specific prototype in question. The Win2 should run Android 17 from day one, naturally with Honor’s MagicOS 11 on top.

As you can see from the screenshot above, in Geekbench 6.7.1, the handset managed a 4,141 single-core score and an 11,514 multi-core score. A separate Geekbench 6 GPU run shows a score of 34,695.

The original Honor Win launched with the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 SoC, so it makes perfect sense that its successor would be powered by the Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6. What remains to be seen is whether the Win2 RT will go with the new Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6, or last year's Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5. We'll let you know when we find out more.
