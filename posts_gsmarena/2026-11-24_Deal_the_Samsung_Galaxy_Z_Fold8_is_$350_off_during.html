Our review explores the Z Fold8 in depth. You can also watch the video review below. The phone is thinner (4.5mm and 9.7mm closed/opened vs. 5.2mm/11.3mm) and much lighter (201g vs. 254g) than the iPhone foldable. It has a comparable battery (Si/C 4,800mAh) with 45W wired and 20W wireless charging. It’s powered by the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 and features a dual 50MP camera – 1/1.56” wide and a 120° 1/2.51” ultra-wide.


Screen size comparison: Samsung Galaxy Z Fold8 vs. Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra

In our review, we found the form factor to be highly versatile and the two LTPO displays to be excellent. There are advanced features like DeX over USB-C too and the Snapdragon chip can handle heavy multitasking and the latest games. The chip does start to throttle after a while, though. Also, there’s no dedicated telephoto camera – a flaw that the Z Fold8 shares with the Duo.
