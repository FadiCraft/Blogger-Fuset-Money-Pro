Oppo's Android 17-based ColorOS 17 is scheduled to be unveiled globally today. The latest mobile operating system will be available for Oppo and OnePlus devices.

If you’re interested in watching the launch event live, you can do so via the embedded YouTube player below.

The ColorOS 17 update will bring several UI changes with updated visuals and smoother animation. It is also teased to include glass-like design elements.

Oppo has also noted that the update will bring several new smart AI features. The Find X10 series, which is going global on October 21, will come with the latest ColorOS 17.
