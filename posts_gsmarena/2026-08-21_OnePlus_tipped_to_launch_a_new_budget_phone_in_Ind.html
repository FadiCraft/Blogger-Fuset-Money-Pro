After announcing its exit from North America and Europe, OnePlus reaffirmed its commitment to India and said it would continue launching new smartphones in the country. Following the OnePlus N6x launch last month, a new rumor now claims that the company is preparing to unveil another budget phone soon.

Tipster Yogesh Brar claimed in a post on X that OnePlus is planning to launch a new budget smartphone in India. The handset is reportedly set to be a 4G-only device.

OnePlus N6x

According to Brar, the phone could be unveiled as early as next month and will be exclusive to India. It is also reportedly set to be OnePlus' final smartphone launch of the year.

The tipster did not share any further details about the upcoming device. Based on the information available, the phone could be part of the N series and potentially serve as a lower-end, 4G-only variant of the N6x.

The OnePlus N6x comes with a Dimensity 6360 Apex chipset, a 7,000mAh battery, a 6.8-inch LCD display, and a 13MP rear camera.
