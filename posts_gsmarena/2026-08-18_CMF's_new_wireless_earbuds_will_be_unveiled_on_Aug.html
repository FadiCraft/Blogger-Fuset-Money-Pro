After announcing the CMF Clip Pro earbuds earlier this month and teasing a new product recently, Nothing sub-brand CMF has now confirmed that it’s unveiling the Buds Neo later this week.

In a post on X, CMF by Nothing announced that the Buds Neo will be unveiled on August 20.

A teaser video shared alongside the announcement offers a first look at the charging case, which appears to feature a smart dial similar to those found on other CMF audio products. The video also reveals the earbuds' color options.

Notably, another teaser shared recently revealed that the Buds Neo will feature an in-ear design, with a silver stem and orange-colored ear tip.

The brand is likely to reveal more details about the upcoming earbuds ahead of their launch on Thursday.
