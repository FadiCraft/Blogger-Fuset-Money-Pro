Back in early September of last year, Poco launched the C85 4G. Now, the brand is apparently gearing up to release a successor of sorts. It's called Poco C95 Pro 4G and today a new leak brings us purported renders of the device as well as its most important specs.

So let's dive in. The Poco C95 Pro 4G is allegedly powered by the MediaTek Helio G91 Ultra chipset. It will reportedly have a 6.9-inch 720x1600 LCD screen with a 120Hz refresh rate, a 50MP rear camera, an 8MP front camera, and a 7,500 mAh battery with support for 45W fast wired charging.

It will disappointingly launch running Android 16 with HyperOS 3 on top, so regardless of how many major OS upgrades Poco will promise for it, the first one will be Android 17. The Poco C95 Pro 4G will have a side-mounted fingerprint scanner embedded in the power button.

It will apparently be offered in two RAM/storage configurations: 6/128GB and 8/256GB in dark blue, black, and green. The green model seems to have a fake leather finish on its back. The Poco C95 Pro 4G was recently certified for sale in Singapore by IMDA, so it's definitely coming soon. Whether there will be a non-Pro C95 remains to be seen. Compared to last year’s C85 4G, the C95 Pro 4G has a newer chipset, a bigger battery, and a more recent Android version.
