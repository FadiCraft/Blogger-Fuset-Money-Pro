Motorola launched the Moto G47 in April, and interestingly, the company is now readying not a successor, but a new member of the Moto G47 family - the Moto G47 Power.

Motorola Moto G47

Motorola has published its European Energy Label documentation, and this reveals that the phone comes with a battery rated at 6,790 mAh. That will most likely be advertised by Motorola as a typical capacity of 6,900 or 7,000 mAh.

And it's a big boost compared to the G47's 5,200 mAh battery. The G47 Power will support 33W fast charging, which is also better than the vanilla model's 20W support. The G47 Power will be IP64 rated for dust and splash resistance, just like the G47.

A premature retailer listing in Europe last month revealed that the G47 Power will be offered with 4GB of RAM and 128GB of storage, but other specs remain a mystery for now. For what it’s worth, the G47 has a 6.67-inch LCD screen with 720x1604 resolution, 120Hz refresh rate, and 1,050-nit peak brightness, the Dimensity 6300 SoC at the helm, a 108MP main camera, a 2MP macro camera, and an 8MP selfie snapper.
