Pre-orders for the new iPhone 18 Pro and 18 Pro Max started this Saturday. And since this is Apple we are talking about, there are no pre-order goodies – the opposite, actually, since Apple raised the iPhone 16, 17 and Air prices by $100. But we won’t be looking at the vanilla models today since the vanilla iPhone 18 isn’t expected until next year.

So, here are the iPhone 18 Pro and 18 Pro Max. They feature Apple’s first 2nm chip, the A20 Pro, which runs at close to 5GHz (it peaks at 4.93GHz) and posted massive Geekbench scores. Additionally, the new models promise longer battery life, especially the Pro Max. And they have a variable aperture for the 48MP 1/1.28” main camera (f/1.5-f/4.0).

Since there aren’t many wide foldable alternatives in the US, the Duo’s main rival is the Galaxy Z Fold8. The Samsung phone is thinner (9.7mm vs. 11.3mm) and much lighter (201g vs. 254g). The 7.6” inner displays on both are nearly identical in size and aspect ratio, but Samsung has had several years to optimize One UI for foldable use. Meanwhile, this is the first time iOS tries to transition apps from a cover to a larger internal display. However, Apple offers stylus support and uses an under-display camera, which are features that Samsung abandoned last year.

The two foldables have comparable cameras with a 48MP main camera with sensor shift OIS for the iPhone Duo and a 50MP camera with lens shift OIS for the Z Fold8. They are joined by a 48MP and a 50MP 120° ultra-wide cameras, respectively. Check out a more detailed comparison between the iPhone Duo and Galaxy Z Fold8 here.

Of course, Samsung also has a larger book-style foldable, the Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra. Despite having a larger 8” inner display (nearly 1:1 aspect ratio) and a larger 6.5” cover display (with a tall and skinny aspect ratio), the Ultra is still a lot lighter than the iPhone Duo (215g vs. 254g). It’s thinner too – in fact, at 8.9mm, the Z Fold8 Ultra is only 0.1mm thicker than the iPhone 18 Pro Max (which weighs 249g, by the way).

This one has a 200MP 1/1.3” main camera plus a dedicated 10MP 3x/67mm telephoto lens, which is missing from both the Z Fold8 and the Duo. There’s a 50MP ultra-wide camera too. The cameras are not as good as the S26 Ultra, but they are better than the Z Fold8 cameras.
