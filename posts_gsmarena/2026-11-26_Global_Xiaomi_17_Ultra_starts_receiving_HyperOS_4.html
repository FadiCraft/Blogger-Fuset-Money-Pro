Xiaomi announced the HyperOS 4 update schedule earlier this month, but it seems to have missed the deadline for some of its phones, including the flagship Xiaomi 17 Ultra. Our office unit received the update just this morning, bringing some of the new features that debuted with the Xiaomi 18 Pro series and new aesthetics.

Surprisingly, we got the update without any confirmation. The handset updated itself overnight, which is the first time we have experienced this sort of thing.


HyperOS 4 changelog

In any case, HyperOS 4 brings a new Soft Light Glass design language with UI elements and buttons now having translucent layers with diffused lighting. Animations and transitions feel smoother and faster, while interactions are a tad more responsive.

The changelog is quite informative and describes all of the new features and functionalities. You get new Lock screen customization options, new and better-optimized first-party apps like the default Gallery app, better cross-platform connectivity options, especially when it comes to Apple products, and new AI-powered features with the Notes and Recorder apps.


HyperOS 4 changelog

If you want to learn more about the new under-the-hood improvements, visual changes and new features, check out the software section from our Xiaomi 18 Pro Max review.

Unfortunately, the Xiaomi 17 Ultra doesn't get any of the new camera features that the Xiaomi 18 Pro series brought. The camera app is basically ported from HyperOS 3.
