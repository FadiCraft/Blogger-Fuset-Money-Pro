Still, a nearly €600 discount on the base model is nothing to sneeze at. But is the new wide foldable form factor better than the book-style format? You should also consider some alternatives. The Motorola Razr Fold was €1,330 two weeks ago, now it’s €120 more. That’s still cheaper than the Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra, though. The Moto phone is thicker (10.1mm vs. 8.9mm) and heavier (243g vs. 215g), but it does have a larger battery (6,000mAh vs. 5,000mAh). It’s down on performance (Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 vs. the Elite Gen 5 chipset). However, the camera setup is competitive enough – 50MP 1/1.28” vs. 200MP 1/1.3” main, 50MP 3x/71mm vs. 10MP 3x/69mm telephoto and 50MP 122° vs. 50MP 120° ultra-wide. Both phones are slated for 7 OS updates (but note that the Galaxy launched with Android 17, while Motorola launched its foldable with Android 16).
