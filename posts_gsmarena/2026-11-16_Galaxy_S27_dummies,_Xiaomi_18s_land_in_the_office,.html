Samsung's upcoming Galaxy S27 series appeared in a new leak, courtesy of Sonny Dickson, who shared dummy units of the Galaxy S27 Ultra, S27 Pro, and S27+. The Ultra and Pro feature a radically redesigned, nearly full-width camera island, while the S27+ retains the familiar vertical camera arrangement. The Pro is noticeably smaller than both the Ultra and the Plus. However, tipster Ice Universe questioned the accuracy of the dummy units, claiming the fourth camera shown on the Ultra and Pro doesn't actually exist.
