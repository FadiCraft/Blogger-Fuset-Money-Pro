According to a new report, T-Mobile is going to launch a new fleet of plans under a new T-Mix brand. The T-Mix plan tiers will be Standard, More, and Loaded.

T-Mix Standard will allegedly cost $65 per month for a single line, $105 for two, $130 for three, $155 for four, $180 for five, and $205 for six lines. This includes unlimited talk, text, and data, with 50GB of "premium data".

T-Mix More will set you back $85 per month for a single line, and it brings unlimited premium data and 60GB of hotspot use. Finally, T-Mix Loaded will go for $105 for a single line, and will come with unlimited premium data and 250GB of hotspot use. None of these prices include the autopay discount.

There will apparently also be a new Essentials Saver 3.0 plan for $55 per month for a single line and $100 for two (which is the maximum number of lines you can have on it). This will include 50GB of "premium data".

Unfortunately, it's unclear when T-Mobile plans to launch these new plans, but they are expected to be available very soon, so stay tuned.
