The Redmi K100 Pro is expected to launch alongside the K100 Pro Max in September, and we now have some additional details for the Pro thanks to a Geekbench listing. As previously rumored, the K100 Pro is equipped with Qualcomm’s Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 Elite chip (SM8850) and the specific device from the test also boasts 16GB RAM.

Redmi K100 Pro (M511CD) Geekbench 6 test run

It managed 3,429 single-core points and 10,461 in the multi-core score test. Keep in mind that this is a Geekbench 6 run and not the most recent Geekbench 7 version.

Redmi also posted a pair of teaser images for its participation in the ChinaJoy 2026 event, which kicks off this Friday (August 31). It’s highly likely we’re looking at the silhouette of the K100 Pro.


Redmi K100 Pro teasers

In related news, the K100 Pro was previously spotted in a listing on China’s 3C certification database. That listing confirmed that it will support 100W charging. Previous rumors suggest the phone will feature an 185Hz refresh rate OLED display alongside an 8,500mAh battery and a 200MP main cam.
