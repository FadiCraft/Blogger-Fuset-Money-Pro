Introduction

The Oppo Find X10 series is now official, and there's plenty to get excited about! The standard Find X10 combines a relatively compact design with flagship-grade performance and a high-end camera system, while the larger Find X10 Pro Max goes all out with a top-tier chipset and cutting-edge camera hardware. Today, we're taking a closer look at the smaller of the two - the Oppo Find X10.

The Oppo Find X10 sports a sleek dual-glass design with a clean and classy look, IP66/IP68/IP69 dust and water resistance, and claimed protection against drops. On the front is a 6.59-inch LTPO OLED display with a 1,272p resolution, 10-bit color depth, a 144Hz refresh rate, and support for Dolby Vision and HDR10+.

The phone employs a Dimensity 9600M chip with an all-big-core processor and powerful graphics. It also boots Android 17-based ColorOS 17 out of the box.

One of the most important aspects of the Find X10 is its camera system. There are three imagers on the back: a 200MP primary, a 200MP 2.8x telephoto, and a 50MP ultrawide camera with autofocus. The system supports the optional Hasselblad Teleconverter Kit for 11x optical zoom and up to 20x hybrid zoom.

The selfie camera was given a proper imager and lens as well - it uses a 50MP sensor with an 18mm ultra-wide-angle lens that supports autofocus.

The Find X10 is powered by an 8,000mAh Si/C battery for the global model and a 7,150mAh one in the EU version.

Oppo Find X10 specs at a glance:

  • Display: 6.59" LTPO AMOLED, 1B colors, 144Hz, 3840Hz PWM, Dolby Vision, HDR10+, HDR Vivid, BT.2020, 800 nits (typ), 2000 nits (HBM), 3600 nits (peak), 1272x2772px resolution, 19.61:9 aspect ratio, 462ppi; Ultra HDR image support.

  • OS/Software: Android 17, ColorOS 17.
  • Rear camera: Wide (main): 200 MP, f/1.6, 23mm, 1/1.4", 0.56µm, PDAF, OIS; Telephoto: 200 MP, f/2.6, 65mm, 1/1.56", 0.5µm, PDAF, OIS, 2.8x optical zoom; Ultra wide angle: 50 MP, f/2.0, 15mm, 1/2.75", 0.64µm, PDAF.

The Oppo Find X10 seems to have it all. In fact, if we didn't know about the X10 Pro Max's existence, this one could have easily passed for the top model.

Unboxing the Oppo Find X10

The global version of the Oppo Find X10 ships inside a rather thick white box. It contains the phone, an 8A-rated USB-A-to-C cable, and an 80W SuperVOOC charger.

A color-matched silicone case is also part of the bundle, and the phone comes with a protective film on its display.
