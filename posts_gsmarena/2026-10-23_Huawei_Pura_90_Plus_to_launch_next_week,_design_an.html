Huawei has officially confirmed the Pura 90 Plus and opened pre-orders for the phone in China. The company has also revealed its design, color options, and storage configurations.

The Huawei Pura 90 Plus will go on sale in China on October 28. While the company has yet to reveal the phone’s specifications, it has given us a first look at its design.

Based on images shared on Huawei’s official online store, the Pura 90 Plus appears to feature curved sides, unlike the flat frame found on the other Pura 90 series models. However, it retains the familiar triangular rear camera module.

The Huawei Pura 90 Plus will be available in Dawn Gold, Obsidian Black, Sunset Purple, and Snowy White colors with up to 12GB of RAM and 1TB of internal storage.


A recent rumor claimed that the handset will feature a 1-inch type 50MP main rear camera with variable aperture. It is also said to get a 6.8-inch OLED display with a 1.5K resolution.
