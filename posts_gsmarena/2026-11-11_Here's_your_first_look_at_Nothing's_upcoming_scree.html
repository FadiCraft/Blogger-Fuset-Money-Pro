Nothing CEO Carl Pei recently confirmed that the company would launch its first AI-native product next month, sharing an early teaser of the device. Today, Pei has revealed the full design of the upcoming screenless product.

In a post on X, Carl Pei shared a short video revealing the design of Nothing's upcoming device.

The video shows a device with a brushed metal body, rounded corners and curved edges. Its front features a large physical button with a red dot, possibly for recording, alongside two pill-shaped LED indicators that light up orange and green.

Nothing has yet to reveal any details about the device, including its name.

However, Pei accompanied the video with the caption, “Context doesn't live in a chat box,” hinting that the AI-powered device would be able to remember context when having conversations. Beyond this, the company has only confirmed that it will unveil the screenless product sometime next month.
