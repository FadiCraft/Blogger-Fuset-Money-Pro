Huawei brought the Pura 90 series to China in April this year. Now, a tipster claims that the company is preparing to add a new Pura 90 Plus variant to the lineup.

According to tipster Digital Chat Station, the Pura 90 Plus will feature a triple-rear camera setup with a 50MP 1-inch type RYYB main sensor that offers a variable aperture.

Huawei Pura 90 Pro Max

The phone is also tipped to get a 6.8-inch display with a 1.5K resolution and will be offered in Dawn Gold, Obsidian Black, Snow White, and Sunset Purple color options.

Huawei’s Pura 90 series currently consists of the base Pura 90, a Pura 90 Pro, and the Pura 90 Pro Max. The Pro variants are offered in gradient color options and the Pro Max also gets a 50MP RYYB main rear camera with variable aperture.
