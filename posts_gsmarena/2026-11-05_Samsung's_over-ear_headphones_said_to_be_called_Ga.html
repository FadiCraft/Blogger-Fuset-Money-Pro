Samsung’s rumored high-end over-ear headphones, reportedly called Galaxy Studio, are expected to launch in early 2027 alongside the Galaxy S27 series. A new leak now offers the first details about their design.

A pair of Samsung headphones bearing the model number SMU700 have appeared on the Federal Communications Commission (FCC) certification website. These are said to be the rumored Galaxy Studio headphones.

The listing includes an image showing the placement of the regulatory label, which also offers a partial look at the headphones. According to the partial image shared, the Galaxy Studio headphones appear to feature flat-folding earcups.

Moreover, other related documents also reveal that the headphones will be available in black and beige color options.
