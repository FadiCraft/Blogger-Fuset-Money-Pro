Samsung has put a new twist on its Certified Re-Newed program – the refurbished phones are now also available on Amazon.com in the US. Previously, you could only get them from Samsung itself. This is the store page on Amazon where you can browse all models (well, not all – see below). We’ve also prepared a table where you will find the individual models and their prices.

What makes these different from other refurbished units is that the phones are serviced by Samsung specialists using genuine Samsung parts. This includes replacing the battery. Units need to pass 147 quality checks before getting the Certified Re-Newed label. Once done, the phones are issued a new IMEI and are given a limited 1-year warranty. You can also sign up for Samsung Care+, which offers Unlimited Accidental Damage from Handling (covering drops, spills and mechanical breakdowns).


Samsung’s Certified Re-Newed program

The phones ship in new packaging (made from 100% recyclable material), which includes a charging cable, a SIM ejector and a quick start guide.
