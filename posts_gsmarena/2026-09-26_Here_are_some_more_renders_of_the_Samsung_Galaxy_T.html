Samsung was expected to unveil the Galaxy Tab S12+ and Galaxy Tab S12 Ultra this month, but rumors now suggest it will make these tablets official next month - October 7, to be precise. Leaked renders of both tablets revealed their designs, so we already know what they would look like. Now a new set of renders has surfaced, corroborating earlier design leaks.

The Samsung Galaxy Tab S12 Ultra has a notched display and two rear cameras.


Samsung Galaxy Tab S12 Ultra's leaked renders

The Samsung Galaxy Tab S12+ doesn't have a notched display, but its bezels are thicker than the Tab S12 Ultra's. Moreover, it only sports a single rear camera.


Samsung Galaxy Tab S12+'s leaked renders

Another difference is thickness: the Galaxy Tab S12+ is 5.3mm thick, while the Galaxy Tab S12 Ultra is 5.1mm thin. The Tab S12 Ultra's image also mentions an IP68 rating, which the Tab S12+ doesn't have, even though its image suggests water resistance. Screen sizes also differ: the Tab S12+ packs a 12.6" display, while the Tab S12 Ultra packs a 14.6" display.

Both the Galaxy Tab S12+ and Galaxy Tab S12 Ultra have a metal build and stylus support. One image also shows 5G support for the S12+. While the source hasn't shared a similar image for the S12 Ultra, a couple of renders confirm LTE connectivity for the Ultra model.
