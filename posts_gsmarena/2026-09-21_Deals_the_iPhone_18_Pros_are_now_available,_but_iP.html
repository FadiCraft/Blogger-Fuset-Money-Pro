The iPhone 18 Pro and 18 Pro Max went on sale this week in Germany, along with 64 other countries in the first wave. If you have your eyes set on an iPhone Duo, you will have to wait for over a month more to get it.

As for the new Pro models, here is a reminder of their prices – the small iPhone 18 Pro starts at €1,450, while the larger iPhone 18 Pro Max starts at €1,600. As is tradition, Apple stopped selling the old Pros when the new ones were unveiled – only non-Pro models last more than a year on Apple.com. But that doesn’t stop third-party retailers from selling older Pros as you’ll see below.

The iPhone 17 Pro launched at €1,300 for a 256GB unit last year, now you can have it for less. That said, there are reasons to pay the extra premium for the new 18 Pro models. For example, the new model brings Apple’s first 2nm chipset – the A20 Pro is a beast – and it has a smaller punch hole for the FaceTime camera and Face ID system. There’s also a variable aperture and faster charging (50% in 15 minutes instead of 20 minutes).

Meanwhile, the Samsung Galaxy S26 is €370 off. It’s thinner (7.2mm vs. 8.8mm) and lighter (167g vs. 211g) than the iPhone 18 Pro, while having a small battery advantage – 4,300mAh vs. 4,056mAh (for an eSIM iPhone version). The Samsung Galaxy S26+ and S26 Ultra are both €450 off. We’re leaning towards the Ultra, even though the S26+ is pretty cheap for a 512GB unit. The Ultra is better-equipped to go head to head with the iPhone Pros in a camera shootout – it has a 200MP main (1/1.3”), a 50MP 5x/111mm periscope (and that same 10MP 3x/67mm tele), plus a 50MP ultra-wide. Then there are the Redmi 17 and Redmi Note 17. Both are huge with 6.9” and 6.99” displays, but the Redmi 17 has a 720p+ IPS LCD, while the Note 17 has a 1080p+ OLED. Battery capacity on both is essentially the same, 7,500mAh vs. 7,700mAh with 45W charging. Funnily enough, the Redmi 17 has the better chipset – well, “better” is relative as it is a Snapdragon 4 Gen 5 vs. Snapdragon 4 Gen 4. Still, since they are at the same price right now, we’d recommend the Redmi Note 17, unless you really, really prefer LCD over OLED. By the way, the Note 17 also has that 15% discount for Prime Student subscribers.
