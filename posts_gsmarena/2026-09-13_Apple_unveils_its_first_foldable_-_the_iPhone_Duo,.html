Of course, Apple dominated the most popular stories of week 37 this year. Apple held a historic event on September 9 - the first in new CEO Ternus' reign, and the one to finally unveil the first foldable iPhone.

Interestingly, the iPhone 18 Pro and 18 Pro Max scored more hits than the iPhone Duo. Apple's latest Pro models got a variable aperture main lens, a faster A20 Pro SoC, and a smaller Dynamic Island. The iPhone 18 Pro series is now on sale, starting from $1,200/€1,450 and $1,300/€1,600, respectively, and will begin shipping on September 18.

The iPhone Duo is, undoubtedly, the star of the show. Apple's first foldable looks every bit the cutting-edge piece of tech everyone expected. It's a wide-styled foldable, about the size of a passport when folded. Its inner display is anti-reflective, has a less-visible crease, and is seemingly seamless, thanks to an under-display camera. Oh, and Touch ID is back! The iPhone Duo is shipping in October and costs $2,000/€2,300.
