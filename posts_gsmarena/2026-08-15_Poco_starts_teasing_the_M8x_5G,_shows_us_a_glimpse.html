Poco launched the M8 and M8 Pro in January, the M8s in April, and the M8 Power earlier this month, but now it turns out that there's a fifth member of the family on the way too.

This will be called the M8x, and Poco has started teasing it today via a dedicated microsite on Flipkart. That tells us where you'll be able to buy it once it launches. We also get the teaser image you can see below, showing us a glimpse of the phone's back.

The microsite reveals that this device will support 5G, and that's it for now. More specs should be revealed in the coming days and weeks, and we'll keep you posted.
