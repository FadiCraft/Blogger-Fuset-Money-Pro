The Poco F9 Ultra focuses on two key upgrades over the F8 Ultra. It brings a new 200MP main camera (replacing the 50MP 1/1.31” module of the old model) to help bridge the gap between 1x and the 5x optical zoom offered by the 50MP periscope.


Poco F9 Ultra highlights

The other major change is that the battery now has 8,050mAh capacity, up from 6,500mAh. Charging is the same as before with 100W wired and 50W wireless. However, the phone remains on the same Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 chipset as last year’s model. Check out our review for detailed results on battery life, performance, camera quality and more.


Poco F9 Ultra with SD 8 Elite Gen 5 • Poco F9 Pro with SD 8 Elite Gen 5 V Series

The Poco F9 Pro does upgrade the chipset – it uses the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 V Series, which is an Elite Gen 5 with a cut down GPU. It’s still a solid upgrade over the original Snapdragon 8 Elite that powered the F8 Pro. We have a test unit in for review, so expect results soon.


Poco F9 Pro highlights

Additional upgrades brought by the F9 Pro include the same 200MP camera as the Ultra. Considering that the F9 Pro kept the 50MP 2.5x telephoto camera of the old model, there isn’t really a zoom gap to bridge here – the 50MP 1/1.55” module of the F8 Pro produced good enough 2x shots and the 2.5x lens took over from there.

The battery capacity upgrade on the Pro is minor – 6,330mAh vs. 6,210mAh on the old model – but the 100W wired charging is now paired with 50W wireless. Last year, wireless was exclusive to the Ultra.
