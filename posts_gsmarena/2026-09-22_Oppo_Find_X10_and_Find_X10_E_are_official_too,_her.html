Alongside the Find X10 Pro Max, today Oppo has also unveiled the Find X10 and Find X10 E in China. These are more affordable options than the X10 Pro Max, as you may have expected from their names. Let's take them one by one.

Find X10

The Oppo Find X10 has a 6.59-inch AMOLED screen with 1272x2768 resolution, a 144 Hz refresh rate (in some games, 120Hz system-wide), 288Hz touch sampling, and a 2,000-nit peak brightness. It's powered by the MediaTek Dimensity 9600M chipset, which is a rebranded Dimensity 9500, built on a 3nm process (unlike the Dimensity 9600 Pro in the Find X10 Pro Max which uses a newer 2nm process).


Oppo Find X10 official images

The Find X10 has a 200MP main camera using a 1/1.4" type sensor. It gets two-axis OIS and an f/1.6 aperture. The ultrawide is a 50MP camera with autofocus, a 120-degree field of view, and f/2.0 aperture. There's also a 200MP periscope telephoto camera with two-axis OIS, with 2.8x optical zoom, using a 1/1.56" type sensor. For selfies you get a 50MP camera with autofocus.

Like the Find X10 Pro Max, the Find X10 has an 8,000 mAh battery with support for 80W wired charging and 50W wireless charging. The Find X10 is also IP66, IP68, and IP69 rated for dust and water resistance. It too runs ColorOS 17 based on Android 17, and it has an ultrasonic in-display fingerprint scanner.


Oppo Find X10 color versions

It's available in Ice Blue, Clear Orange, and Light Titanium. Pricing starts at CNY 5,499 ($821) with 12GB of RAM and 256GB of storage, goes up to CNY 5,999 ($896) with 512GB of storage, CNY 6,499 ($971) with 16GB of RAM and 512GB of storage, and climbs all the way to CNY 7,499 ($1,120) if you want 16GB of RAM and 1TB of storage.

Find X10 E

The Find X10 E is the cheapest of the bunch. It features a 6.59-inch AMOLED screen with 1256x2760 resolution, a 120Hz refresh rate, 240Hz touch sampling, and 1,800-nit peak brightness. This one is powered by the MediaTek Dimensity 9500s, which is a lower-tier 3nm SoC than the Dimensity 9600M in the Find X10.


Oppo Find X10 E official images

On the rear you get a 50MP main camera using a 1/1.4" type sensor, with f/1.6 aperture and two-axis OIS, a 50MP f/2.0 ultrawide with 120-degree field of view, and a 50MP telephoto camera with 3x optical zoom and two-axis OIS using a 1/1.95-inch type sensor. Selfies are handled by a 32MP camera with f/2.4 aperture.

The Find X10 E has a 7,025 mAh battery with support for 80W wired and 50W wireless charging. It's IP66, IP68, and IP69 rated for dust and water resistance like the other two members of the family, and it also runs ColorOS 17 based on Android 17.


Oppo Find X10 E color options

It will be available in Ice Blue and Light Titanium for CNY 4,999 ($747) with 12GB of RAM and 256GB of storage, and CNY 5,499 ($821) with 16GB of RAM and 512GB of storage.
