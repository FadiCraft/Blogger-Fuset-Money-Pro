For comparison, the base iPhone 18 Pro Max (256GB) is $700 cheaper and if portability is a concern, there is the smaller iPhone 18 Pro for $100 less than that.

Now, let’s look at the competition. The Samsung Galaxy Z Fold8 is the only other wide foldable in the US market. With a $200 discount and a lower starting price, it costs $300 less than the iPhone. The Galaxy is thinner (9.7mm vs. 11.3mm) and much lighter (201g vs. 254g) while offering a similar cover display (5.5” vs. 5.4”) and inner display (both 7.6” with similar aspect ratios).

They have nearly identical battery capacities too (4,800mAh vs. 4,883mAh), so endurance will depend on chipset efficiency – it’s a Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 for the Galaxy and an Apple A20 Pro for the Duo. Both phones lack dedicated telephoto cameras, offering only dual 50MP and dual 48MP modules, respectively.

Samsung also has a larger book-style foldable, the Samsung Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra. This one packs a 200MP 1/1.3” sensor in the main camera and does have a telephoto lens, even if it’s only a 10MP 3x/69mm module. Google still can’t manage a good discount for its foldable, so we turn to the Motorola Razr Fold as an alternative. This phone is not as thin or light (it measures 10.1mm and weighs 243g), but it has a large 6,000mAh battery with fast 80W wired and 50W wireless charging (vs. 5,000mAh 45W/20W for the Z Fold8 Ultra). It uses the non-Elite Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 chipset and promises 7 OS updates, the same as the Galaxy. The phone has a 50MP main with a 1/1.28” sensor, a higher resolution 50MP 3x/71mm telephoto and a 50MP ultra-wide camera.
