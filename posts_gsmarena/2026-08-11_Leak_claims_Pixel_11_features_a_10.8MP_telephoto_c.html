The Google Pixel 11 series is going official tomorrow at the Made by Google event. While most details about the lineup have already leaked, a new rumor has revealed details about the base Pixel 11's telephoto camera.

According to tipster @evowizz, the standard Pixel 11 will feature a 10.8MP periscope telephoto camera. It is likely the same sensor used in the Pixel 10 and is expected to support 5x optical zoom.

Previously leaked marketing materials suggest the handset will offer up to 30x digital zoom. The tipster also claims the Pixel 11 will be equipped with 12GB of RAM, which aligns with earlier reports.

The leak further reiterates that the standard Pixel 11 will not feature the rumored HiLight RGB notification LED. The feature is instead expected to remain exclusive to the Pixel 11 Pro, Pixel 11 Pro XL, and Pixel 11 Pro Fold.

Recent rumors suggest that the Pixel 11 will offer a 6.3-inch OLED display, a 5,000mAh battery, and the Tensor G6 chipset. It is expected to be available in Frost, Hibiscus, Obsidian, and Pistachio colorways.
