vivo launched the T5 4G in May, and last week it teased the T5 5G. Today, vivo's Indian branch announced that it will unveil the T5 5G on September 2 at 12 PM local time in India.

Additionally, vivo revealed the T5 5G's key specs, including the Dimensity 7500 Turbo SoC, a 1.5K 144Hz 3D curved AMOLED display, and a 7,050 mAh battery.

The image of the T5 5G shared by vivo on its Indian website shows a design almost identical to the Indian iQOO Z11 that went on sale today, suggesting the vivo T5 5G will be a rebranded iQOO Z11.
