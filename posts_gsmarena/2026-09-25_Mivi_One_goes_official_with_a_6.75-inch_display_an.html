Mivi, the Indian electronics brand known for its audio products, has officially entered the smartphone market with the launch of the Mivi One 5G.

The Mivi One is a budget 5G phone that comes with a 6.75-inch LCD display with 90Hz refresh rate and HD+ resolution.

It is powered by the Snapdragon 4 Gen 2 chipset and is available with up to 8GB of LPDDR4X RAM and 128GB of UFS 2.2 storage. The phone does also get a microSD card slot for storage expansion.

The Mivi One packs a 6,000mAh battery and features a 50MP rear camera along with an LED flash and an auxiliary lens. It gets an 8MP front camera for selfies and video calls.

Other features include a side-mounted fingerprint scanner, a 3.5mm headphone jack, an IP64 rating, Bluetooth 5.1, dual-band Wi-Fi, and a USB Type-C port. The handset runs stock Android 16 out-of-the-box.

The Mivi One 5G is available in Blue, Burgundy Green, Navy Blue, and Pink color options.

It will be available for purchase in India via Flipkart starting October 8 with a base price of INR 14,999 for the 4GB/128GB option.

Meanwhile, the 6GB/128GB variant will be priced at INR 15,999 and the 8GB/128GB model has a price tag of INR 16,999.
