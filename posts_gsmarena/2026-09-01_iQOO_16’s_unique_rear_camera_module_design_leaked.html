If recent rumors are accurate, the iQOO 16 could launch sometime this month. While we haven’t heard anything official from iQOO, new details about the phone’s rear camera module have surfaced.

Tipster Digital Chat Station claims that the iQOO 16 will feature a redesigned, square-shaped rear camera module with “large rounded corners.” DCS has also shared an image that allegedly shows the new camera module.

According to the tipster, the module will house three cameras arranged in a connected layout. It is also expected to feature a textured finish, along with detailing around the frame.

Additionally, the iQOO 16 is said to feature a newly developed manufacturing process for its rear panel, giving it a distinctive pattern.

Recent rumors suggest that the upcoming iQOO flagship will be powered by an Extreme version of the soon to be announced Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6. The handset is also said to offer a 50MP primary rear camera and an 8,500mAh battery.
