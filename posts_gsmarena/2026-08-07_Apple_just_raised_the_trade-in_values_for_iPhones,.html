Apple has increased the trade-in values of most iPhones, iPads, Macs, and Watches in the US. The list also includes some Android smartphones from Samsung, Google, and OnePlus.

The Watch Ultra 2, Mac Pro, iPhone 16 Pro Max, and the iPad Pro are the Apple devices that currently have the highest trade-in values. However, note that the trade-in values of some products remain unchanged, and the values mentioned by Apple on its website aren't flat discount rates; they are estimated trade-in values, so you might get less than what's mentioned as the values will "vary based on the condition, year, and configuration of your eligible trade-in device." Besides, Apple states that not all devices are eligible for credit.
