Lava has added a new device to its portfolio in India today, the Virat V1 Pro 5G. It comes with a 6.75-inch 720x1600 screen with a 120Hz refresh rate, a 50MP main camera, and a 6,000 mAh battery.


Lava Virat V1 Pro 5G marketing images

It's powered by the Unisoc T8200 SoC, paired with 6GB of RAM and 128GB of expandable storage via a Hybrid SIM slot. The phone runs "clean Android 16". It's IP64 rated for dust and splash resistance.


Lava Virat V1 Pro 5G official images

It measures 168.29 x 77.64 x 8.68 mm and weighs 207g. The price is currently INR 15,999 ($167), down from an RRP of INR 21,999 ($229), and you can purchase the phone from Flipkart in Sikkim Midnight and Spiti Frost colors.
