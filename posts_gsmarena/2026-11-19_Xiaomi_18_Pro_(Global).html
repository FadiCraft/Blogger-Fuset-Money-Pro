Introduction

The Xiaomi 18 Pro is here, and Xiaomi seems determined to prove that a compact flagship doesn't have to make serious compromises. With a 6.4-inch display, the phone is hardly tiny, but it's still refreshingly manageable compared to the ever-growing crop of large-screen flagships. And despite its relatively modest footprint, the 18 Pro packs some impressive hardware, including two 200MP cameras, Qualcomm's latest flagship silicon and a secondary OLED display on the back.

Xiaomi 18 Pro Max vs. 18 Pro

The rear display is not a novelty for Xiaomi, of course. The Xiaomi 17 Pro brought back the concept last year, and its successor retains it with a few refinements and expanded functionality. At 2.7 inches, the secondary screen is slightly smaller than the Pro Max's 2.9-inch panel, but it looks every bit as impressive. It can serve as a viewfinder for selfies with the main cameras, display notifications and widgets, and even run mini-apps. Meanwhile, the main display has an interesting trick of its own - Xiaomi's new Smart Privacy Display technology, which limits viewing angles to keep prying eyes away from sensitive on-screen content.

Photography is arguably where the biggest generational upgrades are to be found. The 18 Pro replaces its predecessor's 50MP main and telephoto cameras with two 200MP units. The main shooter retains the large 1/1.28-inch sensor format, while the periscope gets a considerably larger sensor and a brighter lens. Interestingly, Xiaomi has also shortened the native telephoto focal length from 115mm to 75mm, trading some optical reach for what should be better versatility, particularly in low light and close-up photography. The 50MP ultrawide completes the setup.

Unlike the Pro Max, which gets the more powerful Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6, the smaller Pro uses the standard Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6. It also packs a substantial 6,000mAh silicon-carbon battery with 90W wired and 50W wireless charging. All of this comes in a body that's only marginally larger than last year's model, although it's noticeably heavier.

Xiaomi 18 Pro has impressively slim bezels

Another interesting development is that Xiaomi is finally taking its compact Pro flagship beyond China, unlike the Xiaomi 17 Pro, which never received a proper international release. Between the ambitious camera hardware, the unusual dual-screen setup, and the latest silicon, the Xiaomi 18 Pro certainly looks promising on paper. Whether all of that translates into a genuinely better compact flagship, and whether the extra bells and whistles justify the price, is what we're here to find out.

Unboxing

The Xiaomi 18 Pro ships in a fairly unassuming white two-piece cardboard box.

Our European retail package doesn't include a charger, but you do get Xiaomi's proprietary orange-tipped USB-A-to-USB-C cable. It's rated for 6A and features an additional contact pin, presumably to support Xiaomi's proprietary fast charging protocols.

Xiaomi offers a compatible 90W HyperCharge adapter as an optional purchase if you are after the highest charging speed possible.

On the plus side, Xiaomi did include a semi-soft, clear TPU case in our box, which is always a nice little bonus.

We also received a separate matte-black Xiaomi case with a pleasant soft-touch finish and magnets that make the phone compatible with magnetic accessories, including magnetic wireless chargers.
