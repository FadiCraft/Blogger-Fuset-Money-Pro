There have been conflicting reports surrounding Apple’s twentieth-anniversary iPhone, aka the iPhone 20 Pro, lately. Earlier this week, the all-glass device was said to be canceled due to alleged low yield rates but a second report from Bloomberg quickly refuted those claims and said that the device is still on track to launch next year.

Tipster Digital Chat Station is now chiming in with details surrounding the iPhone 20 Pro and 20 Pro Max. He claims that the two devices known internally as the V73 and V74 feature a larger screen than the current crop of iPhone Pro models but with the same 9:21 screen aspect ratio.

The tipster did not confirm the exact screen diagonals but previous reports suggest the iPhone 20 Pro will bring a 6.4-inch display while the 20 Pro Max is rumored to feature a 6.96-inch diagonal.

DCS goes on to explain that the iPhone 20 Pro models will feature a flat screen but with a “glass cover” on top, which, through optical refraction, will create the previously rumored borderless effect. Apple is also expected to bring improved cooling alongside solid-state buttons to next year's iPhone Pro lineup.
