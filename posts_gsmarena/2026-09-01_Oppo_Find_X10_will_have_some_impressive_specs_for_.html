Oppo is expected to unveil the Find X10 series in China in September, and then, according to a recent rumor, the Find X10 and Find X10 Pro Max will make it to global markets by late October.

While the specs of the Find X10 Pro Max have been talked about quite a few times already, less attention has been paid to the Find X10. But this is going to be an impressive device in its own right, according to a new rumor from Digital Chat Station.

Oppo Find X9

The Find X10 will be the most powerful vanilla flagship smartphone from any Chinese brand this year, apparently. It will be the only one with an 8,000 mAh battery, the only one with two 200MP cameras (the main one and the periscope telephoto), and the only one with a screen supporting the BT.2020 color space.

Previously, Oppo was said to be testing both a 200MP periscope telephoto camera and a 64MP one, but now it seems like the company has decided to go with the former. It will use a 1/1.56" type sensor, while the Find X10's 200MP main camera will use a 1/1.4" type sensor. The device should have a 6.59-inch screen with "1.5K" resolution and incredibly thin bezels.

For comparison's sake, note that the Find X9 has a 50MP main camera using a 1/1.4" type sensor, a 50MP periscope telephoto camera using a 1/1.95" type sensor, and the same screen size and resolution. But the Find X10's panel will be upgraded.
