Google is unveiling the Pixel 11 family at a special event tomorrow, on August 12. Ahead of that, a last-minute leak brings us some new renders depicting the Pixel 11, Pixel 11 Pro XL, and Pixel 11 Pro Fold.

The Pixel 11 is shown here in the Obsidian colorway, which, as you can see, is a dark shade of gray.

Moving on, we have the Pixel 11 Pro XL, also shown in Obsidian. This phone's camera bar protrudes way more from its back than the Pixel 11's.

Finally, the Pixel 11 Pro Fold in Obsidian. This phone is only expected to launch in Obsidian and Pine, while the Pixel 11 should be available in Obsidian, Olive, Canyon, and Fog.
