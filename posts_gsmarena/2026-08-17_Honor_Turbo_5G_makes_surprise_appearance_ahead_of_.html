Back in May, Honor unveiled its Win Turbo phone with a massive 10,000mAh battery and a listing on AliExpress has revealed a new Turbo member. The Honor Turbo 5G is a familiar-looking device, sharing the same design as the X80 Pro Max but with different specs.


Honor Turbo 5G

Turbo 5G is equipped with a 6.79-inch AMOLED with 1,200 x 2,640px resolution and a 120Hz refresh rate. It also features an 8,560 mAh battery with 80W wired charging and IP69K ingress protection. The phone boots MagicOS 10 based on Android 16.

There’s a Snapdragon 6 Gen 4 under the hood, which is paired with 8GB RAM and 256GB storage. In terms of cameras, Turbo 5G packs a 50MP main shooter on the back, which is paired with a 5MP ultrawide lens. You also get a 16MP selfie camera and an optical fingerprint scanner.


Honor Turbo 5G color options

Honor Turbo 5G is listed in black, green and orange colors. The listing confirms the device will go on sale from August 17.
