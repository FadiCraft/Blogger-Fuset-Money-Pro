Honor unveiled MagicOS 11 based on Android 17 last month, and today the company has finally revealed when the stable update will reach existing devices. Don't expect anything too clear, though.

As you can see below, the company's arm in the UK has just dumped a huge list of supported devices into a post on X, saying it would update all of them to MagicOS 11 between this month and March next year.

Wondering when your HONOR device will be blessed with MagicOS 11 and all those swanky new features?

Well wonder no more! 👇 https://t.co/l7AOj3suOt

— HONOR UK (@UKHonor) October 15, 2026

On one hand, it's good to see that the update will reach so many phones. On the other hand, it's pretty weird not to have any clearer indication of which handset will receive the update when - at least in which month, since the rollout is after all spread over half a year.

Anyway, it's the best we have for now. At least we're clear that the update is on its way, and the full list of supported devices is now in the open.
