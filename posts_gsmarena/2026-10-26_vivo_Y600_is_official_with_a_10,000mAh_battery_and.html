The vivo Y600 is now available for pre-order in China, as promised, with open sales set to begin on October 30. The phone packs a massive 10,000mAh battery and has also earned a Guinness World Record for battery endurance.

As per the Guinness World Records test, the phone lasted for 32 hours and 37 minutes during a continuous livestream test. The 10,000mAh battery supports 44W wired fast charging and reverse wired charging.

The handset sports a 6.83-inch AMOLED display with a 120Hz refresh rate and is powered by the Dimensity 7300e chipset. It also gets a 50MP primary rear sensor and an 8MP selfie camera.

The vivo Y600 offers an under-display fingerprint scanner, NFC support, and an IP68 rating as well.

Looking at the specs and the design, it’s pretty much confirmed that the Y600 is a rebranded vivo S2 FE/V80 Lite for the Chinese market.

Pricing starts at CNY 1,999 ($280) for the 6GB/128GB variant, with the 8GB/128GB option priced at CNY 2,099 ($295). It is also available in 6GB/256GB and 8GB/256GB models, which are priced at CNY 2,199 ($310) and CNY 2,499 ($350), respectively.

The vivo Y600 is being offered in Diamond Gold, Obsidian Black, and Sapphire Blue color options.
