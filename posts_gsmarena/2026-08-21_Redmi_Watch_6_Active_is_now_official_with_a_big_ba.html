The Redmi Watch 6 Active last leaked a few days ago, and today it's finally become official. It's already listed in Xiaomi's Chinese online store, and it's up for grabs for CNY 349 (approximately $51). You can get it in Gunmetal, Pink Orange, and Silver colorways.


Redmi Watch 6 Active colors

It sports a 1.85-inch AMOLED screen with 390x450 resolution and 1,200-nit peak brightness, a 9.9mm thick plastic case, and a 470 mAh battery. It supports Bluetooth 5.3 and BLE, and it's IP68 rated for dust and water resistance. It has an accelerometer and a heart rate monitor that also supports blood oxygen tracking.

It weighs 26g without the strap. You can pair it to devices running Android 8.0 and above, and iOS 14.0 and above. Battery life is advertised at six days with the Always-on display working, twelve days without it, and even up to 18 days in power-saving mode. There are over 140 sports modes on board. The watch tracks the usual stuff, including sleep, stress, and calories burned. It also features breathing and relaxation exercises. You can make calls on it via Bluetooth too.

The Redmi Watch 6 Active is definitely coming to Europe at some point in the near future, judging by past leaks.
