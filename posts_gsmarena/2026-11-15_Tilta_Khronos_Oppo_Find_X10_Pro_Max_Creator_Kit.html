Today, we've got another video creator kit for unboxing. It's the Tilta-made Khronos Oppo Find X10 Pro Max Creator Kit, and it's nearly identical to the one we tried with the Find X9 Ultra.

If you want a quicker version, here's our video unboxing over on our TikTok.

The entire kit comes in a quality hard case and includes the following accessories: the modular phone case, a lanyard, a 67mm filter adapter, a 67mm 1-5 stop ND filter, a filter adapter for the optional 230mm teleconverter lens, the focus PD handle, a 22W MagSafe cooler, a mini LED light panel, a 38mm Arca-Swiss quick-release adapter, a cold shoe quick-release adapter, an adjustable handle, a quick-release selfie stick tripod, and a USB-C cable.

All of these accessories transform the Oppo Find X10 Pro Max into a rather extreme, full-fledged video kit. The Focus PD handle pairs to the phone via Bluetooth and can control its camera interface. It packs a focus and zoom dial, a record button, a USB-C port, and its own 5,000mAh battery from which it sips power and charges the phone.

There are several electrical contact pins and mounting points so you can attach the available accessories in different ways.

The video light is a very useful video accessory, which we didn't get with the Find X9 Ultra Khronos Creator Kit.

The Khronos Oppo Find X10 Pro Max Creator Kit ships in Space Gray for an introductory price of $512 (the regular price is $570) and is on pre-order now. The accessory is slated to start shipping in two to four weeks.
