Oppo has just made the Reno17 and Reno17 Pro official in China, following plenty of leaks and teasers. They succeed the Chinese Reno16 and Reno16 Pro, but with a twist - last year, the vanilla model was the compact one, with a 6.32-inch screen, while the Pro had a 6.78-inch display. This time around, it's the Pro that's small, while the Reno17 gets a 6.56-inch panel.

Both are IP69K rated, have metal frames and optical under-display fingerprint readers, and run ColorOS 17 out of the box. If their spec sheets seem eerily familiar, that's because they're very close to the Reno16 and Reno16 Pro that Oppo launched outside of China in June.

Oppo Reno17 Pro

The Reno17 Pro is almost a carbon copy of the international Reno16 Pro. It has a flat 6.32-inch 120Hz screen with 1216x2640 resolution and 1,800 nits of peak brightness, the Dimensity 8550 Super chipset, and the same 151.2x72.4mm footprint. It comes with 8GB or 12GB of RAM and 256GB or 512GB of storage.


Oppo Reno17 Pro color variants

The camera setup is identical too: a 200MP 1/1.56-inch main sensor with f/1.8 aperture and OIS, a 50MP ultrawide with autofocus, a 50MP periscope telephoto with 3.5x optical zoom and OIS, and a 50MP selfie shooter with autofocus. The biggest change is the battery, which has grown to 7,250 mAh, from 6,700 mAh. It still charges at 80W. As a result, the Reno17 Pro is a bit thicker at 8.32mm and weighs 192g, or 194g for the cat-themed color, which is 8.5mm thick.

The vanilla Reno17 also uses the same Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 chip as its international predecessor, as well as an identical camera setup. That consists of a 50MP main shooter with a Sony sensor, f/1.8 aperture, and OIS, a 50MP ultrawide with autofocus and a 116-degree field of view, a 50MP periscope telephoto with 3.5x optical zoom and OIS, and a 50MP selfie camera with autofocus. However, the Reno17's body is bigger, measuring 157.64x75.27x8.28mm and weighing 206g. The cat version comes in at 8.47mm and 208g.

Oppo Reno17

This makes room for a flat 6.56-inch 120Hz screen with 1208x2624 resolution and 1,800 nits of peak brightness, as well as an 8,000 mAh battery with 80W charging. According to Oppo, that's the largest battery it has ever put in a Reno. The phone comes with 8GB or 12GB of LPDDR5X RAM, and 256GB, 512GB, or 1TB of UFS 3.1 storage. Both new phones come in three colors - Cat and Cocoa Brown, plus Ice Moon White for the Reno17 and First Snow White for the Pro.


Oppo Reno17 color options

The Reno17 starts at CNY 3,599 (about $507) for 8/256GB. It costs CNY 3,799 ($535) for 12/256GB, CNY 3,999 ($563) for 12/512GB, and CNY 4,899 ($690) for the 12GB/1TB version. The Reno17 Pro starts at CNY 4,199 ($591) for 8/256GB, and goes up to CNY 4,599 ($648) for 12/256GB and CNY 4,999 ($704) for 12/512GB. With China's national subsidy, prices drop by CNY 500, so the vanilla model can be had for as little as CNY 3,099 ($436).
