Introduction

The global versions of the Redmi Note 17 models are coming out earlier this year, with a clear shift in focus from power to battery autonomy. Today we are going to explore the most beefed-up one, the Redmi Note 17 Pro Max.

But first, you might wonder: what happened to the Redmi Note 16? The answer is pure marketing strategy - when Xiaomi skipped the '16' generation for its flagship line to align with the iPhone 17, the Redmi Note lineup skipped from 15 directly to 17 to maintain the internal series alignment as well.

The Redmi Note 17 Pro Max is the top-tier model of the new family, offering the most premium set of features - the coolest design, the fastest chip, the best cameras, and the largest battery.

The Note 17 Pro Max keeps the screen of the Redmi Note 15 Pro and Pro+ - a 6.83-inch AMOLED with 1,280p resolution, 120Hz refresh rate, 68B colors, HDR10+ and Dolby Vision streaming support. Its peak brightness is now up to 3,500nits.

The Max model employs the Snapdragon 6 Gen 5 (4nm) chipset, which feels like a performance step down compared to previous Pro+ models as Xiaomi focuses on durability and battery life. Memory configurations feature LPDDR5X RAM, with UFS 4.0 storage on the higher-capacity tiers and UFS 2.1 on the base model.

The camera department trades peak megapixel counts for efficiency, featuring a 50MP primary camera with OIS alongside an 8MP ultrawide secondary unit. Up front, you get a 32MP fixed-focus selfie camera.

The highlight feature of the Max model is its 10,000mAh Si/C battery with 100W wired and 27W reverse wired charging. The Max is advertised as being able to last three days on a single charge. We are, however, reviewing the European version, which has a reduced 9210 mAh capacity.

The design of the Redmi Note 17 Pro Max did not only improve in looks, but also in durability. It is now IP66, IP68, IP69, and IP69K rated for dust and water resistance, plus TÜV-certified to survive in 2m deep water for 72 hours. Furthermore, the Max is also certified for 3m drop resistance.

Xiaomi Redmi Note 17 Pro Max specs at a glance:

  • Body: 163.5x78.3x8.6mm, 230g; glass front, plastic frame, plastic back; IP68/IP69K dust tight and water resistant (high pressure water jets; immersible up to 1.5m for 30 min), Manufacturer-rated water-resistance (up to 2m for 24 hours), Drop resistant up to 2.5m.
  • Display: 6.83" AMOLED, 68B colors, 120Hz, 3840Hz PWM, HDR10+, Dolby Vision, 1800 nits (HBM), 3500 nits (peak), 1280x2772px resolution, 19.49:9 aspect ratio, 447ppi.
  • Chipset: Qualcomm SM6850 Snapdragon 6 Gen 5 (4 nm).
  • Memory: Up to 512GB 12GB RAM; UFS 2.1 - 128GB only, UFS 4.0.
  • OS/Software: Android 16, HyperOS 3.
  • Rear camera: Wide (main): 50 MP, f/1.8, 26mm, 1/2.76", 0.64µm, PDAF, OIS; Ultra wide angle: 8 MP, f/2.2, 15mm, 120˚, 1/4.0", 1.12µm.
  • Front camera: 32 MP, (wide).
  • Video capture: Rear camera: 4K@30fps, 1080p@30/60/120fps, gyro-EIS, OIS; Front camera: Yes.
  • Battery: 9210mAh (EU); 10000mAh (Global); 100W wired charging, PD3.0, 22.5W reverse wired.
  • Connectivity: 5G; eSIM; Wi-Fi 6; BT 5.4; NFC; Infrared port.
  • Misc: Fingerprint reader (under display, optical); stereo speakers (with Dolby Atmos).

The Redmi Note 17 Pro Max shapes up to be a solid device with a focused set of features. However, if you were holding out for a major performance jump over the Redmi Note 15 series, you won't find it here. With rising component and memory costs squeezing the entire mobile industry, manufacturers are shifting priorities. And Xiaomi's strategy with this model clearly trades processing power for battery autonomy and long-term durability.

Unboxing the Redmi Note 17 Pro Max
