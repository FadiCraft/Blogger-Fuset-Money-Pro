The Poco X8 series currently comprises the X8 Pro and X8 Pro Max, with a base X8 model also rumored to be in the works. Now, a new leak claims that Poco is preparing to launch an X8 Power variant as well.

According to a tipster, the Poco X8 Power could debut soon with a massive 10,000mAh battery. The tipster has also shared the phone's full specifications, suggesting that it could be a rebranded Redmi Note 17 Pro Max, which is set to launch next week.

Poco X8 Pro Max

The Poco X8 Power is tipped to feature a 6.83-inch AMOLED display with a 120Hz refresh rate and 1.5K resolution. The panel is said to be protected by Gorilla Glass Victus 2. The phone is also tipped to carry an IP69 rating and measure 8.68mm in thickness.

Under the hood, the handset will be powered by the Snapdragon 6 Gen 5 chipset, paired with LPDDR5X RAM and UFS 4.0 storage, as per the tipster. It is also said to receive four years of OS updates and six years of security updates.

For photography, the Poco X8 Power is tipped to feature a 50MP primary rear camera paired with an 8MP ultrawide camera. Selfies are reportedly handled by a 32MP front-facing camera.
