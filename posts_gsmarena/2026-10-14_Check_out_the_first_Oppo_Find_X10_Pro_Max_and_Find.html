Our reviews of the global Oppo Find X10 and Find X10 Pro Max are still ongoing. We already shared an unboxing, but now we can share some of our camera samples with you guys.

But before we get to samples, we have a quick side-by-side between the Find X10 Pro Max and the Pixel 11 Pro, over on TikTok.

As usual, we're saving our full and detailed analysis for our review. But you're welcome to draw your own conclusions based on the samples.

What we can say is that shooting on the Find X10 series feels virtually as refined as on the Find X9 Ultra. The camera interface is snappy, moving between lenses is instantaneous, and taking photos is zippy.

We're not in love with the fact that Oppo removed the dedicated camera button on the side, which was present in the Find X9 Pro series.
