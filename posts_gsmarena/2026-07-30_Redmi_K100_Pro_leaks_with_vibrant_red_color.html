The Redmi K100 Pro continues to leak ahead of its launch. A new poster making rounds on Weibo gives us our best look at the device yet. The overall design looks similar to the Redmi K90 Pro Max with a triple camera setup inside an island configuration. We can also spot what appears to be a subwoofer, which could again feature Bose branding and sound tuning.

The more interesting bit is the color choice. The dark red is quite similar to the rumored Cherry-red color that’s expected to be the hero hue for the iPhone 18 Pro and 18 Pro Max. Funnily enough, Xiaomi will beat Apple to the punch as the Redmi K100 Pro is expected to launch on August 11.

Just yesterday, the K100 Pro emerged in a Geekbench test run where we got confirmation it will launch with the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 chip alongside up to 16GB RAM. Previous rumors also suggest a sizeable 8,500mAh battery with 100W charging, a 185Hz refresh rate screen and a 200MP main camera.
