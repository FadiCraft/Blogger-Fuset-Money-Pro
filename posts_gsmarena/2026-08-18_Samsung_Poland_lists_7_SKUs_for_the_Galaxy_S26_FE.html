The Samsung Galaxy S26 FE is on a roll – yesterday, the phone appeared on the Google Play Console and in a hands-on video. Now, the FE model has been listed in an official Samsung document.

The PDF in question comes from Samsung Poland and concerns the terms and conditions for Samsung Care+, the company’s extra insurance plan. The document doesn’t reveal much, but it does list 7 model numbers for the Galaxy S26 FE.

An official Samsung Care+ PDF reveals seven model numbers for the Galaxy S26 FE

So far, we’ve seen leaked renders that show three color options. Assuming there are two storage versions – 8/128GB and 8/256GB based on the leaked specs – that makes six SKUs. So where does the seventh come from? That’s not entirely clear. The Galaxy S26 Ultra, for example, has 18 model numbers listed, which makes sense: 6 colors x 3 storage options = 18.

Maybe one of these SKUs is a single-SIM, the other dual-SIM (we mean that in terms of physical cards, the phone will almost certainly have an eSIM option). Or maybe the extra SKU is an Enterprise Edition – the Galaxy S25 FE Enterprise Edition was available only in Jetblack and only with 128GB storage, making for a single SKU.


Samsung Galaxy S26 FE

Anyway, Roland Quandt, who first spotted the PDF, also shared these two images of the Samsung Galaxy S26 FE that show two of the three different colors. Let’s call them mint and black. The third one, navy blue, can be seen in the hands-on video we mentioned above.

We don’t have an official release date for the Galaxy S26 FE. However, since it passed through the FCC and is already listed on the Google Play Console, there is little stopping Samsung from launching the phone.
