We've heard about the Realme 17T and Realme 17 Pro before, and we've even seen their designs. While Realme hasn't confirmed these smartphones yet, a tipster claims the Realme 17 series will also include an Ultra model.

The Realme 17 Ultra is tipped to be the first Ultra smartphone in the company's number series, and it's coming to India. When? We don't know yet. However, if the tipster is right, it will launch in India in Ultra Silver and Ultra Red, with two memory configurations: 12GB/256GB and 12GB/512GB.

The Realme 17 Ultra has model code RMX5341. More details about it should surface in the coming weeks.
