Last month, reliable tipster Digital Chat Station claimed the vivo X500 Ultra's primary camera could use Sony's LYTIA L910 50MP 1/1.28" sensor with a 35mm lens. Today, DCS said the X500 Ultra will feature two LYTIA L910 sensors.

The second camera on the vivo X500 Ultra using the L910 sensor will be the ultrawide camera. DCS said the X500 Ultra prototype using the L910 sensor for the primary and ultrawide cameras achieved the highest CIS (CMOS Image Sensor) test results.

Additionally, DCS claimed the vivo X500 Ultra's periscope unit will use the HPU sensor, making it the industry's only smartphone with LOFIC and DeepPix tech.

vivo X300 Ultra

DCS also reiterated that the vivo X500 Ultra will be powered by the Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6 SoC.

Other rumored specs of the vivo X500 Ultra include a 6.85" 144Hz 2K LTPO AMOLED display, an in-display ultrasonic fingerprint scanner, and a battery with a capacity between 7,000 mAh and 8,000 mAh.

The X500 Ultra will arrive next year in March or April.
