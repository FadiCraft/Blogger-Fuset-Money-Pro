iQOO will introduce the Z11 in India on August 20, with a design and specs different from the Chinese and Malaysian Z11. iQOO has already revealed a few features of the Indian Z11, and it has now shared more details about the smartphone.

iQOO has revealed that the Indian Z11 will sport a 32MP selfie camera on the front, while the dual-camera setup on the rear will comprise a 50MP primary (Sony IMX882, OIS, 26mm) and an 8MP (15mm) ultrawide-angle camera.

Additionally, iQOO announced that the Indian Z11 will get three years of Android OS upgrades and five years of security updates, without revealing which Android version the smartphone will run out of the box. Chances are it will be Android 16 with OriginOS 6 on top.

The iQOO Z11's Indian model will also be IP68 and IP69-rated and have MIL-STD-810H certification. iQOO also said that the AI Creation and AI Captions features will have unlimited AI credits so "students can use these features without worrying about restrictive usage limits."

iQOO will also be introducing the iQOO Buds TWS earphones in India on August 20. The earphones will offer up to 50 hours of battery life, with a single charge enabling up to seven hours of calling or up to 12 hours of audio listening. A 10-minute charge will offer up to four hours of playback.

Circling back to the Indian Z11, iQOO previously announced that the smartphone will come with the Dimensity 7500 Turbo SoC, a 6.83" 1.5K resolution 144Hz curved AMOLED display, and a 7,050 mAh battery. The smartphone will be priced under INR40,000 in India.
