The Xiaomi 18 Pro and Xiaomi 18 Pro Max debuted in China in September, with Xiaomi subsequently confirming plans for a global launch. The company has now begun teasing their international debut, offering a tentative launch timeline.

In a social media post, the company confirmed that the Xiaomi 18 Pro series will make its global debut later this month.

Notably, the phones were recently spotted on Italian retailer Epto’s online store, revealing their specifications, colour options, and European pricing.

According to the listings, the Xiaomi 18 Pro will start at €1,398.90 for the 12GB/256GB variant. The Xiaomi 18 Pro Max, meanwhile, will reportedly be available in a single 12GB/512GB configuration, priced at €1,783.90.

While most of the specs remain unchanged, the listings suggest that the global variants will feature smaller batteries than their Chinese counterparts. The Xiaomi 18 Pro is listed with a 6,000mAh battery, down from 7,000mAh in China. Similarly, the Xiaomi 18 Pro Max is tipped to pack a 7,050mAh cell, compared to the 8,500mAh battery in the Chinese variant.

Both phones are also listed in three colour options, including Black, White, and Velvet Red.
