vivo’s next-generation X500 flagship series is set to launch in China later this month. Ahead of the launch, the company has revealed several key features of the upcoming devices and has now offered a first look at the vivo X500 Pro Max’s design.

In a post on Weibo, vivo Product Vice President Huang Tao shared a first look at the rear design of the vivo X500 Pro Max. The handset is shown in silver and orange colorways.

The image reveals a design that closely resembles the vivo X300 Ultra. The phone is seen with a dual-tone rear panel and a large circular camera module with three sensors and Zeiss branding. Meanwhile, the LED flash is positioned along the top-left edge of the rear panel.

The vivo X500 Pro Max will also support a 400mm teleconverter, which is also shown in the teaser image. vivo also showcased the video capabilities of the phone in a video shared on Weibo.

vivo will unveil the X500, X500 Pro, and the X500 Pro Max in China on September 21.
