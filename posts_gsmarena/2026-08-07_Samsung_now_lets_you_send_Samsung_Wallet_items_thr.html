Samsung's latest update to the Quick Share app for its smartphones and tablets is now rolling out and it comes with a new feature. You can now share Samsung Wallet items through Quick Share. This includes tickets and boarding passes.

So you can much more easily send someone a ticket to an event or a boarding pass for a flight by simply using Quick Share. The latest Quick Share update also unsurprisingly brings some bug fixes.

Keep in mind that the rollout seems to be staged, so it may take a while until it reaches all supported devices. To manually check for the update, go to the Galaxy Store app and tap on the three-dot menu, then select Updates > Update all.
