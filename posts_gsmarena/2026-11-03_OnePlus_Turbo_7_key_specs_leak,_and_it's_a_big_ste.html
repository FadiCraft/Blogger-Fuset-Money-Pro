OnePlus launched the Turbo 6 in China back in January, and now a tipster on Weibo has shared the first details about its successor. According to the source, the Turbo 7 will be powered by the Snapdragon 8 Gen 6, which is still being refined, and OnePlus is allegedly vying to be among the first to launch a phone with it.

The Turbo 7 is also said to continue OnePlus' push to bring a 200MP main camera and a 185Hz refresh rate (165Hz system-wide) to more of its phones. It will reportedly have a glass body but no periscope telephoto camera, and the tipster says the handset will launch by the end of the year.

OnePlus Turbo 6

That's the same main camera resolution and screen refresh rate as on the OnePlus 16, as well as the Ace 7 and Ace 7 Pro, which were announced in China with cooling fans and 185Hz displays last week. The Turbo 6, on the other hand, has the Snapdragon 8s Gen 4 chipset, a 165Hz screen, and a 50MP main camera, so on paper this would be quite the upgrade.

The Snapdragon 8 Gen 6 hasn't been announced yet, but the upcoming chipset will either be called the Snapdragon 8 Gen 6 or the Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 Pro, according to a previous leak. In late October, Digital Chat Station said that a OnePlus Turbo Pro is on the way around the end of the year with that chipset, a 6.78-inch 1.5K 185Hz screen, a 200MP main camera, and a battery of at least 9,000 mAh. So it's quite possible that both of these leaks are about the same phone, keep that in mind.
