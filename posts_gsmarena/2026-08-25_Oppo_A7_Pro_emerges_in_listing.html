Earlier this month, Oppo announced its A7 Pro Max in China, and we now have our first look at its non-Max sibling, which emerged in a certification listing in Brazil. The A7 Pro appeared with the CPH2931 model number and features an interesting back design with a horizontal camera visor housing two lenses and an LED flash.

Oppo A7 Pro (via Technoblog)

The same device previously received certification in Thailand and Malaysia. The new certification listing comes from Brazil’s National Telecommunications Agency (Anatel) and reveals the A7 Pro will feature an 8,000 mAh battery instead of the larger 10,000 mAh cell found on the Pro Max.


Oppo A7 Pro (via Technoblog)

The new listing also reveals that A7 Pro units sold in Brazil will ship with a 45W charging brick.
