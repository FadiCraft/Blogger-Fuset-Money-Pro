The Samsung Galaxy S26 FE has just been certified by the FCC, and now we have a new set of leaked renders that portray it in three colors. According to the source of this leak, these will be all the hues in which the Galaxy S26 FE will be offered.

Unsurprisingly, the Galaxy S26 FE goes with the same updated camera design as the Galaxy S26 and S26+. There's now an oval-shaped island onto which the three cameras are mounted.


New Samsung Galaxy S26 FE leaked renders

The Galaxy S26 FE is rumored to be unveiled in early September. It will be powered by the Exynos 2500 chipset, and should run Android 17 with One UI 9 on top.

The phone is apparently going to be more expensive than its predecessor, the Galaxy S25 FE. It may have the exact same cameras as that device, along with the same 45W wired charging support.
