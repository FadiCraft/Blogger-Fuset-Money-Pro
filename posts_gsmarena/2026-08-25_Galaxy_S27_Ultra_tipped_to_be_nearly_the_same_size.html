Yesterday, a leaked CAD render revealed a redesigned rear camera layout on Samsung’s upcoming Galaxy S27 Ultra. Now, the same source has shared more details about the phone’s design.

According to tipster Ice Universe, the Galaxy S27 Ultra will retain the overall design of the Galaxy S26 Ultra, with the main change being the rear camera layout.

The upcoming flagship is also tipped to have almost identical dimensions to its predecessor and will retain the S Pen slot on the bottom-left side. The speaker grille will also remain on the bottom-left, the tipster claims.

However, the Galaxy S27 Pro, Galaxy S27 and Galaxy S27+ are said to have the speaker grille on the right side.


Meanwhile, yesterday’s leak also suggested that Samsung could launch the Galaxy S27 Ultra in two different sizes. However, Ice Universe has refuted the claim, saying the smaller model shown in yesterday’s leak is actually the rumored Galaxy S27 Pro. The tipster claims that the S27 Pro will feature the same new camera layout as the S27 Ultra, but will not come with the S Pen.

The Galaxy S27 Ultra is rumored to come with a triple-rear camera setup. Meanwhile, both the Ultra and Pro models are said to offer the Privacy display feature from the Galaxy S26 Ultra.
