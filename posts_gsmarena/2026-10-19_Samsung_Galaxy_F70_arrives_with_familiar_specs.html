Samsung’s latest F-series smartphone is now official in India with the launch of the Galaxy F70. This is the third entry in the F70 series, following the Galaxy F70e from February and the Galaxy F70 Pro, which launched in August. The new Galaxy F70 is the midpoint in the F-series and features plenty of similarities with last year’s Galaxy A17.

Galaxy F70 is built around a 6.7-inch Super AMOLED display with FHD+ resolution and a 90Hz refresh rate. The panel is protected by Corning Gorilla Glass Victus and features a waterdrop notch for its 13MP front-facing camera. Around the back, we have a 50MP main cam with OIS alongside a 5MP ultrawide lens and a 2MP macro shooter.

Samsung India did not confirm which version of One UI and Android is running on the phone out of the box. The phone’s Flipkart page suggests it boots Android 15 which would be strange in late 2026. We’ve also seen indications that the phone ships with One UI 8.5 based on Android 16. We’ll have to wait for official confirmation from Samsung to be sure. Elsewhere, Galaxy F70 features a 5,000 mAh battery with 25W charging and IP54 ingress protection.

Galaxy F70 is available in black and violet colors. It comes in two RAM/storage trims, with a 4/128GB version going for INR 19,999 ($210) and a 6/128GB model priced at INR 22,999($242). The device is listed on Flipkart and will be available from October 20.
