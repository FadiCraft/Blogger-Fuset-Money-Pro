The Samsung Galaxy S26 FE is coming soon, having been already certified by the FCC and starred in multiple leaks recently. It may be unveiled in early September alongside the new Galaxy Tab S12 tablets.

Ahead of that, a user on X has uploaded what he claims is a hands-on video of the Galaxy S26 FE. Take a look:

First irl look at the Samsung Galaxy S26 FE! pic.twitter.com/grrlhJ68Di

— Bradley (@VerdeSelvans) August 17, 2026

The video shows the phone running Android 17 with One UI 9 on top, unsurprisingly. The design is in line with what Samsung's been doing to its camera bumps this year, making the individual cameras part of an oval-shaped island.

The video confirms a 6.7" AMOLED screen with a 120Hz refresh rate, and a 4,900 mAh battery with support for 45W fast charging for the device. None of those are changed from the Galaxy S25 FE, by the way. What is new is the Exynos 2500 SoC, and a new sensor for the 12MP ultrawide camera, apparently.
