Oppo is set to unveil its Find X10 series smartphones next week in China with three members. And ahead of the big launch, display units of the Find X10 E, Find X10 and Find X10 Pro Max were spotted in what appears to be an official vivo store in China.

Oppo Find X10 E

The Find X10 E brings a familiar look to last year’s Find X9 and will be the more affordable entry in the Find X10 lineup. It’s expected to bring a triple camera setup with 50MP sensors and a 7,000 mAh battery. The phone will be available in Ice Blue and Light Titanium colors with two RAM/storage trims – 12/256GB and 16/512GB.

The standard Find X10 also brings a similar design to its predecessor and it is confirmed to feature dual Hasselblad-tuned 200MP cameras (main and telephoto) while the Find X10 Pro Max will also pack a 200MP ultrawide. The Pro Max will also be the first phone to launch with MediaTek’s new Dimensity 9600 Pro chip. Both are expected to offer 8,000mAh silicon-carbon batteries with 80W wired and 50W wireless charging.


Find X10 and Find X10 Pro Max (left) and Find X10 Pro Max (right)

All three Find X10 series phones are expected to launch with ColorOS 17 based on Android 17. It remains to be seen if the Find X10 series will debut outside of China.
