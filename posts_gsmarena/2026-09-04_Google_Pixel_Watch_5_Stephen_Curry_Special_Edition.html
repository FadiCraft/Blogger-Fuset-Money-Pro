Google unveiled the Pixel Watch 5 last month alongside the Pixel 11 series smartphones. The smartwatch comes with and without LTE connectivity in 41mm and 45mm sizes, with the smaller model offering four color options and the larger model three. Google also unveiled the Stephen Curry Special Edition, which is now available for purchase.

The Google Pixel Watch 5 Stephen Curry Special Edition is offered in 45mm size with LTE connectivity. It shares the specs and features with other models, but comes in a Satin Pyrite Aluminum Case and a Rye Performance Loop Band with a water-repellent coating to wick away moisture. The Stephen Curry Special Edition costs $579.99.

For comparison, the standard 45mm Pixel Watch 5 with LTE connectivity costs $529.99, while the 45mm Performance Loop Band costs $59.99. If you buy them both separately, you pay $589.98 in total. But if you purchase the Pixel Watch 5 Stephen Curry Special Edition, you save $9.99.
