vivo unveiled the X300 E in July, and the X500E is expected to arrive as its successor in the next few months, with Bluetooth SIG certification bringing it closer to launch.

The vivo X500E has been listed on the Bluetooth SIG website with model designation V2656, but the certifying authority hasn't revealed any other details about the smartphone.


vivo X500E Bluetooth certified

However, rumors claim the vivo X500E will come with the Dimensity 9500 SoC, a 6.59" display, a 50MP primary camera, a 64MP periscope telephoto camera, and a 7,500 mAh battery.
