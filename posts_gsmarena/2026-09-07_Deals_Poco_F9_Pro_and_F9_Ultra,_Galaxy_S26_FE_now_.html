The Poco F9 series launched this week as did the Galaxy S26 FE. Let’s have a look at the newcomers as well as some alternatives.

The Poco F9 Pro brings some serious upgrades over the F8 Pro – however, it also comes at a higher price. Scroll down a bit and you’ll see that the old Pro is €260 cheaper. The new Pro brings a faster chipset (Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 V Series vs. Snapdragon 8 Elite), it has a 200MP main camera (replacing a 50MP module) and its slightly larger 6,330mAh battery supports both wired (100W) and wireless charging (50W), while the old Pro only did wired.

Given their prices, it may be prudent to compare the Poco F9 Pro with the F8 Ultra as well. The old Ultra has a better GPU (the V Series cuts off a third of the GPU hardware), a bigger screen (6.9” vs. 6.59”) and a bigger battery (6,500mAh vs. 6,330mAh). And while it lacks a 200MP main camera, it does have the better 5x telephoto module.

Then there is the Xiaomi 17T. This one has a powerful mid-range chip, the Dimensity 8500 Ultra, paired with a 6.59” display similar to the F9 Pro. And while there’s no 200MP camera on here either, the 17T duo features a 50MP 5x/115mm periscope. The 6,500mAh battery supports only 67W wired charging.

Without the Prime discount, a 256GB Galaxy S26+ is actually cheaper than a 128GB S26 FE. Note that the Plus model has 4 extra gigs of RAM compared to the FE, so it’s not just about the storage.

The Galaxy S26+ is easily the better phone overall. It has the better 6.7” screen (1440p+ LTPO vs. 1080p+ LTPS), the better chipset (Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 for Galaxy vs. Exynos 2500) and better connectivity (including USB-C 3.2 vs. USB-C 2.0). The cameras and battery are similar or identical. The S26 FE needs a price cut, quickly.
