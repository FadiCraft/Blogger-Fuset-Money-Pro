The Infinix Hot 70 Pro, unveiled in July, debuted in India today.

The Infinix Hot 70 Pro comes in Mirage Green, Dive Blue, Silk Glow Purple, and Thermo Orange in India, with 6GB/128GB and 8GB/128GB memory options priced at INR24,999 and INR27,999, respectively. All models will be available starting September 12 through Infinix's Indian website, Flipkart, and retail stores.

The Infinix Hot 70 Pro is powered by the Dimensity 7100 SoC and runs Android 16-based XOS 16 out of the box. Infinix has promised three years of Android OS upgrades and five years of security updates for the Hot 70 Pro.

The Hot 70 Pro features a 6.76" LCD on the front with a 2,344x1,080-pixel resolution, a 144Hz refresh rate, and 910 nits HBM brightness. The display also has a centered punch-hole for the 8MP selfie camera.

On the back, you get a 50MP primary camera (Sony IMX882), along with an auxiliary unit and what Infinix calls an Active Matrix Cube.

The Hot 70 Pro also features a dedicated AI button on its right side for different actions. Under the hood is a 6,000 mAh battery with 45W wired charging, 10W reverse wired charging, and bypass charging support.

Other highlights of the Infinix Hot 70 Pro include an IP68 rating, NFC, a side-mounted fingerprint scanner, and a dedicated microSD card slot for storage expansion.
