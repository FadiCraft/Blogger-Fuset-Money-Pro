Another family of devices that rarely gets mentioned is Amazon tablets. The Fire tablets are alluringly cheap, but their hardware is as basic as it gets and they come with Amazon’s app store (though installing Google Play is easy enough). This week, Amazon unveiled the Alexa Tablets, which are much more traditional Android tablets, complete with full access to the Google Play Store.

The Amazon Alexa Tablet 12 Pro is easily the most powerful slate that the company has built yet. It actually has some premium features, the most interesting of which is the Nanomatte glass – Amazon worked with Corning to offer glare-resistant nano-etching. This is a €60 upgrade over the base model and you can push that further with 16GB of RAM and 256GB upgrade. The base model has an 8/128GB configuration. The Pro tablet is powered by the Dimensity 8400, which is massively more powerful than what the best Amazon Fire tablet has. Its USB-C port is wired for 5Gbps transfer speeds (USB 3.2 Gen 1) and it even has video out via DisplayPort.

Note that Amazon also released a keyboard cover and a stylus for the Alexa Tablet 12 Pro. You can get them in a bundle for €725 (this is for the 8/128GB standard glass model). The Nanomatte version of the bundle starts at €785 for the 8/128GB model and the fully kitted out 16/256GB Nanomatte bundle is €895.
