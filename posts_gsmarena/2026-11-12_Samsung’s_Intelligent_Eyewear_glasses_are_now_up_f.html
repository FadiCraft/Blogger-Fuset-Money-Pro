Following their announcement back in July, Samsung has now opened pre-orders for its Intelligent Eyewear smart glasses. They come in 30 distinct designs co-developed with Warby Parker and Gentle Monster.

Pricing starts at $395, with the glasses scheduled to be available in just two markets – South Korea and the United States. General availability starts on December 10. The glasses will be available via select Samsung retail stores as well as via Warby Parker retail and online channels in the U.S. and Gentle Monster's online store.


Samsung Intelligent Eyewear x Gentle Monster

Samsung’s Intelligent Eyewear run on Google’s Android XR platform. All pairs are equipped with cameras, microphones and speakers, with access to Google Gemini. The cameras allow the AI assistant to see your surroundings and provide additional context, turn-by-turn navigation, real-time translations, note-taking and more.


Samsung Intelligent Eyewear x Warby Parker

The glasses rely on Qualcomm's Snapdragon AR1 Gen 1 chipset and an unspecified-capacity battery, which Samsung claims will last you up to 9 hours on a charge.
