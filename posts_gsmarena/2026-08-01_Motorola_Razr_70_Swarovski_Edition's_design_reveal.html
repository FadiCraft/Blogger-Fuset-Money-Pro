Last August, Motorola launched a Swarovski Edition of the Razr 60, called the Razr 2025 in North America. We now learn the brand will give the Swarovski treatment to this year's Razr 70/Razr 2026 as well, as renders of the Motorola Razr 70 Swarovski Edition have surfaced online.

Last year's Razr 60 Swarovski Edition came in Pantone Ice Melt color, but this year's Swarovski Edition Razr will come in a black shade.

You can see that the rear panel of the Motorola Razr 70 Swarovski Edition is textured and arranged in a quilted pattern. It features small crystal-like elements arranged in the same pattern, with a few of them used to decorate the area near the cover display. Even the phone's hinge has a crystal on it.


Motorola Razr 70 Swarovski Edition

Aside from these differences, the Motorola Razr 70 Swarovski Edition is identical to the standard model.

One of the images shared by the source reveals the Razr 70 Swarovski Edition will come bundled with a USB-C cable, a 33W power adapter, and a protective case.


Motorola Razr 70 Swarovski Edition

There's no word from Motorola about the Razr 70 Swarovski Edition's launch, but we may see it go official this month, as the Razr 60 Swarovski Edition was unveiled in the same month last year.
