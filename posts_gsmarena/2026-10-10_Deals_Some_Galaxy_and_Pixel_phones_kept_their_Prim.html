Amazon’s Prime Big Deal Days only lasted two days and there were some really good deals to be had for Prime subscribers. But that is over, so today we will look at no-Prime deals and, as it turns out, many phones kept their Prime Day prices. The good news is that these deals don’t require a Prime subscription. The bad news is that some phones did go back up in price.

These are the exact same prices that we saw during Prime Big Deal Days, except now you don’t even need Prime. It’s worth thinking about whether you should get a Z Fold8 Ultra or an S26 Ultra, but maybe wait until you see the S26 Ultra price first.

What is this – do our eyes deceive us? The Samsung Galaxy S26 FE finally got its first discount and it’s a good one. The FE was an easy skip at its MSRP, but now the sub-$600 starting price gives you something to think about.

If you don’t need the larger 6.7” display, you are probably better off paying a bit extra for the Galaxy S26 – it gets you a current flagship chip (Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 vs. Exynos 2500) and its 6.3” display is an LTPO panel. The S26+ is better than the S26 FE, but then its price tag is $185 higher. You can always try looking for leftover Galaxy S25 FE units too, as the 2025 model offers roughly the same user experience. And if you care about DeX, you should know that Samsung removed the wired option from the S26 FE as it cut the DisplayPort video output from the USB-C port (wireless DeX is still supported, though).
