Introduction and specs

After several years of keeping its Pro-series flagships exclusive to China, Xiaomi is finally bringing the 18 Pro Max to global markets. The move puts it in more direct competition with flagship offerings from Oppo, vivo and Honor.

Last year's Xiaomi 17 Pro Max was a notable step down from the 17 Ultra, but the 18 Pro Max gets much closer to Ultra territory. It now features two 200MP rear cameras, including a 3.2x periscope telephoto, while the secondary screen on the back makes a return.

The 6.9-inch main display retains its predecessor's resolution, but Xiaomi promises higher brightness and introduces hardware-level privacy features similar to those on the Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra. The idea is to limit visibility from off-center angles, making it harder for people nearby to read what's on the screen.

The rear display isn't a new concept for Xiaomi - the Mi 11 Ultra pioneered it back in 2021. But the concept was really taken to the next level last year with the Xiaomi 17 Pro Max where we first saw the considerably larger 2.9-inch take up the entire camera island.

Of course, the device also ships with the latest and greatest from Qualcomm - the Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6, paired with at least 12GB of RAM and 256GB of internal storage. And HyperOS 4 introduces a few new functions compared with last year's software.

Xiaomi 18 Pro Max vs. 18 Pro

One substantial difference between the regional versions is battery capacity. The global model gets a 7,050mAh battery, compared with 8,500mAh for the Chinese model. Both support 100W wired charging and up to 50W wireless charging, although the latter requires a compatible Xiaomi wireless charger.

On paper, the Xiaomi 18 Pro Max looks like a serious flagship contender, but how well does it all come together in practice, especially against the competition? Let's find out, starting with what's in the box.

Unboxing the Xiaomi 18 Pro Max

The European retail box contains the usual paperwork and a USB-A-to-USB-C cable, but no power adapter. You'll need a compatible charger, sold separately, to reach the phone's maximum 100W wired charging speed.

The box does include a clear silicone case, though.
