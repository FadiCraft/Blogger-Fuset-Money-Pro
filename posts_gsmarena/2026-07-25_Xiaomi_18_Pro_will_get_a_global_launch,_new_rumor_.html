Last year the Xiaomi 17 Pro and Xiaomi 17 Pro Max were launched in China and never made it outside of the company’s home country. The situation is changing this year, however, according to a new report.

The Xiaomi 18 Pro at least will allegedly be released in markets like Europe, Türkiye, Russia, Taiwan, and Japan. A few days ago a different report claimed that the Xiaomi 18 Pro would also launch in India alongside the Xiaomi 18 Pro Max.

Xiaomi 17 Pro

Today's rumor says the Xiaomi 18 Pro won't be coming to India, so the situation is still pretty confusing. Today's report also doesn't mention the Xiaomi 18 Pro Max at all, so we're not sure what will happen with that one.

Anyway, according to past rumors, the Xiaomi 18 Pro will be powered by the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6 Pro SoC, and will have two 200MP SmartSens camera sensors with 1/1.28-inch type size on the main camera. There will also be upgraded speakers, a larger vibration motor, and a battery with a capacity of at least 7,000 mAh, supporting 100W wired as well as wireless charging.
