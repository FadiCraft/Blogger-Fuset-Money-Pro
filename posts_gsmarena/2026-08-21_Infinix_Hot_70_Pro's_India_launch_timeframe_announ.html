The Infinix Hot 70 Pro, unveiled last month, will debut in India soon. Infinix hasn't revealed the Hot 70 Pro's India launch date yet, but said it will arrive in India in the first week of September.

Infinix also confirmed that the Hot 70 Pro will be sold through Flipkart in India and will come in four colors - Mirage Green, Dive Blue, Silk Glow Purple, and Thermo Orange.

The Infinix Hot 70 Pro's Thermo Orange model features a back panel that changes color with temperature, while Mirage Green has a finish that "transforms under nighttime conditions." The texture on Silk Glow Purple "shifts with motion," whereas the Dive Blue model comes with a "sleek matte finish."
