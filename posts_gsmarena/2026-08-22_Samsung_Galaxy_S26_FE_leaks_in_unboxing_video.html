Samsung is unveiling the Galaxy S26 FE on August 27, and ahead of that we've already seen the device in a hands-on video as well as plenty of leaked renders. How about a quick unboxing video? Would you like to see that, even if the handset isn't official yet?

Well, if you do, here you go. As you'd expect from a Samsung, you only get the phone, some documentation, a SIM ejector tool, and a USB-C cable in the box. No case, no charger.

The Galaxy S26 FE is expected to have a 6.7-inch Super AMOLED screen with FHD+ resolution and a 120Hz refresh rate, the Exynos 2500 chipset at the helm, 8GB of RAM, 128/256GB of storage, a 50MP main camera, a 12MP ultrawide, an 8MP telephoto camera, a 12MP selfie camera, and a 4,900 mAh battery with support for 45W wired charging.

The device will be IP68 rated for dust and water resistance, and will be 7.4mm thick apparently. Samsung should offer seven years of OS upgrades for it. Leaked color names include Blueberry (which the one in the video above probably is), Graphite, and Pistachio.
