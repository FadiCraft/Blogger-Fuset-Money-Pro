Samsung launched the Galaxy Buds4 Pro back in February alongside the Galaxy S26 series smartphones, and now the company is coming out with a new special edition made in collaboration with pop star Nina Chuba. This version will be limited to just 2,000 units.

The price is the same €249, so you don't have to pay more than the recommended retail price for the regular model. The special edition is already up for pre-order at Amazon Germany, and it will be released on November 10.

If you're wondering what's different, the Nina Chuba Galaxy Buds4 Pro come in white and have a case with a "Nina Chuba-inspired design". There's also a braided bracelet in the box, with five unique charms which hang from it. This, Samsung says, makes the limited edition "a collector's item for fans".

Each of the charms represents a song by Nina Chuba: "Maldives", "If This Is Love", "Go to Hell", "Rage Girl", and "Unsafe". "Maldives" is represented by a mermaid charm, while "Go to Hell" gets a charm with a fire symbol.

The Galaxy Buds4 Pro Nina Chuba Limited Edition have the exact same specs as the normal version, so if you're interested in them, don't miss our review of the Buds4 Pro.
