A Samsung executive recently confirmed the existence of the Galaxy S26 FE, with a company representative hinting the phone will launch this year. While we await more details about the Galaxy S26 FE's launch from Samsung, its camera specs have surfaced online, revealing the camera system won't be entirely different from the Galaxy S25 FE's.

This information comes from the folks at Android Authority, which they say is based on evidence they have "reviewed from a trusted source." According to their source, the Samsung Galaxy S26 FE, codenamed "r14," will use a 50MP Samsung ISOCELL S5KGN3 sensor for the primary camera, while the 8MP OmniVision OV08A1 sensor will be used for the 3x telephoto unit. Last year's S25 FE used these same sensors for its primary and telephoto cameras.

Samsung Galaxy S25 FE

The Galaxy S26 FE will also feature a 12MP GalaxyCore GC12A2 sensor and a 12MP Sony IMX825 sensor; however, it's unclear which one will be used for the ultrawide camera and which one will be employed for the selfie camera. Regardless, we know that the S26 FE's ultrawide and selfie cameras will have 12MP resolution, like the S25 FE.

That said, it's not just the cameras on the S26 FE that are almost identical to the S25 FE's. The charging speed is also likely to remain the same, as the S26 FE was recently certified with 45W wired charging support.
