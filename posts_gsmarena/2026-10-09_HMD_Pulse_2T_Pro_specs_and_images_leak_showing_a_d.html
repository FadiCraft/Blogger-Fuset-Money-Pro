At the beginning of September, the HMD Pulse 2 Pro got prematurely listed by a Swiss retailer, and that revealed its specs. The phone is still not official, but in the meantime we have a new leak from a reliable source of HMD-related information, and this one is all about the HMD Pulse 2T Pro - note the "T" in the name.

This device will allegedly sport a 6.78-inch OLED screen with FHD+ resolution and a 120Hz refresh rate, and it will be powered by the MediaTek Helio G100 SoC, paired with 8GB of RAM and 256GB of UFS 2.2 storage.


HMD Pulse 2T Pro leaked images

It's said to have a 50MP main camera with OIS, an 8MP secondary rear camera (probably an ultrawide), a 50MP selfie camera, and a 6,000 mAh battery with support for 33W wired charging. The Pulse 2T Pro will run Android 17 when it comes out, and it will be IP64 certified for dust and splash resistance.

It has an in-display fingerprint scanner, dual speakers, and a dot matrix display on the back. It will reportedly be offered in three colorways: Temper Blue, Titanium, and Orbit Green.

Compared to the Pulse 2 Pro, the Pulse 2T Pro has a slightly larger screen (and it's OLED vs. LCD), a different chipset (Helio G100 vs. Unisoc T615), more RAM and storage, a much newer Android version, a 20% bigger battery, and that screen on the rear.
