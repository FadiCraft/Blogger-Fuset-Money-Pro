vivo launched the X Fold6 back in late June, and it was the first model in the series to switch from Qualcomm to MediaTek, with the Dimensity 9500 Super at the helm instead of the Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 that powered the X Fold5. Now, its successor is apparently already in the works, and it might not stick with MediaTek after all.

According to Digital Chat Station on Weibo, vivo is A/B testing next year's flagship big foldable with 2nm chips from both companies - the Dimensity 9600 Pro on one side, and the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6 or Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6 on the other. The final pick will reportedly come down to which chipmaker offers the better price.

vivo X Fold6

Interestingly, DCS says in the comments that the Dimensity 9600 Pro, which already powers the X500 Pro and X500 Pro Max, is currently the more expensive option. That could give Qualcomm a real shot at a comeback, even though it raised its Snapdragon prices in September.

The foldable will also reportedly keep a conventional screen aspect ratio, unlike the wide Galaxy Z Fold8 and Xiaomi 18 Fold. In fact, Digital Chat Station claims Xiaomi will only make wide foldables from now on, even though plenty of people still prefer the conventional format.
