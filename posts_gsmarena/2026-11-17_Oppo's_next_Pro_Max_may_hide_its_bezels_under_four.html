Last week, leftover hardware from the reportedly canceled Oppo Find X10 Ultra was said to be heading to a later phone, tipped to be the Find X11 Pro Max. Today, Digital Chat Station on Weibo has shared what he calls a "super early preview" of a next-generation flagship display that will be used by Oppo.

According to the tipster, the design would pair a flat panel and very tight edge packaging with cover glass that curves on all four sides. The visual bezel is said to be 0.3mm, give or take, which would make the screen look almost bezel-less, and he says the technology is on par with Apple's (presumably for the upcoming 20th anniversary iPhone).

In the comments, DCS added that the solution would be expensive and would only show up on the pricier models in a flagship series, while OnePlus would stick with traditional flat screens. Asked whether the brand would still have a photography flagship, he simply replied "PM" - pointing to a Pro Max. That would line up with last week's rumor that the Find X11 Pro Max is in line to inherit the canceled Ultra's 6.89-inch "2K" BOE display, 200MP main camera, and 100MP square selfie camera.

For some context, the current Find X10 Pro Max officially has 1.15mm bezels, while the smaller Find X10 goes down to 0.99mm. 0.3mm would thus obviously be a big jump from either of those. And with all of these recent rumors it seems like the Find X11 Pro Max is turning into quite a beast - or a Find X10 Ultra by another name? We're definitely still many months away from its launch, though, so there's plenty of time for things to change. We'll keep you posted.
