Apple has released the first iOS 27.3 developer beta to testers, bringing several new features and changes. The company also rolled out the first developer betas of iPadOS 27.3, macOS 27.3, watchOS 27.3, tvOS 27.3, and visionOS 27.3.

The iOS 27.3 beta 1 introduces a redesigned Health app alongside several Audio Intelligence features such as Siri Recap and Live Rewind on the Apple Watch.

With the latest beta, the company is also bringing Apple Intelligence features to China, as confirmed last week. However, not all features are available to testers yet, with Siri AI notably missing.

The update also includes the Reachability accessibility feature for the iPhone Duo, along with vertical tabs on Safari and an adaptive Dark Mode. Meanwhile, StandBy mode now gets functionality similar to the iPhone Duo across all supported iPhone models.

Apple also announced a new Siri AI Developer Preview which is now available to all developers and allows them to integrate their apps with Siri AI.
