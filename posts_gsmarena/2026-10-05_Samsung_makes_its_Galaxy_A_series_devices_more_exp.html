Last week, Samsung hiked the prices of its high-end devices in the US (along with Europe and India), and today the company has raised the prices of its low-end and mid-range phones in the US as well.

Samsung Galaxy A57

We're talking about the Galaxy A17, A27, A37, and A57. The A17 is now $270, up from $200 when it launched. The A27 now goes for $380, up from $350 when it was released. The A37 now costs $490, up from $450 when it arrived.
