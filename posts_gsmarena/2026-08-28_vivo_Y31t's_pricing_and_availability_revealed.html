vivo quietly listed the Y31t on its Indian website last week without revealing its pricing or availability. But we now know how much the vivo Y31t costs, as it’s now on sale in the country.

The vivo Y31t comes in Crimson Red, Seashore Green, and Golden Mist colors, with three memory configurations - 4GB/128GB, 6GB/128GB, and 6GB/256GB, priced at INR25,999, INR29,999, and INR34,999, respectively. You can purchase these in India from vivo's official website, Flipkart, and partner retail stores.


vivo Y31t's color options

The vivo Y31t is powered by the Snapdragon 4 Gen 2 SoC and runs Android 16-based OriginOS 6 out of the box. It's built around a 6.75" 120Hz LCD with 1,250 nits HBM brightness and wet-hand touch support.

The Y31t features three cameras: a 50MP primary camera (f/2.0, 90° FOV), a 2MP bokeh camera (f/2.4), and an 8MP selfie camera (f/2.05). Fueling the entire package is a 7,200 mAh battery with 44W charging support.

Other highlights of the vivo Y31t include dual stereo speakers, a side-mounted fingerprint scanner, and IP68 and IP69 ratings.
