Earlier this month, the iPhone Air 2 and the standard iPhone 18 showed up as dummy units on Weibo. Now, another set of alleged dummies has surfaced on X, giving us a closer look at the design of both phones.

The biggest change is once again the iPhone Air 2, which has two cameras side by side in its pill-shaped camera plateau, instead of the single one found on the original iPhone Air. It's a similar arrangement to the one on the iPhone Duo. Meanwhile, the iPhone 18 looks almost identical to the iPhone 17, with the same vertical camera module on the back.


iPhone Air 2 (left) and iPhone 18 (right) dummy units

According to past leaks, the second camera on the Air 2 will be a 48MP ultrawide, joining the 48MP main shooter. The handset is also rumored to come with a 6.55-inch OLED screen with a 120Hz refresh rate and a smaller Dynamic Island, an A20-series chipset, and a larger battery than its predecessor, all in a body that's said to be just 5.2mm thin.

Both phones are expected to launch next spring, and a recent report claims display production for the two is lined up with a March launch.
