Patience pays off – some flagships were holding a fairly high price last week, but now they are down to a much better number.

That is the case for the Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra – a 256GB unit was $1,105 last week, now it’s $1,000. We’ve seen it as low as $950 not too long ago, but the $50 difference isn’t huge. You can also get double the storage for $150 more.

But never mind the new Ultra, what about the Samsung Galaxy S25 Ultra? That is currently a bit cheaper than the S26+. You’ll be getting the older chipset (Snapdragon 8 Elite vs. Elite Gen 5), but pretty much everything else is better, including the 200MP 1/1.3 main camera (vs. 50MP 1/1.56”), the two telephoto modules – 10MP 3x/67mm and 50MP 5x/111mm (vs. just the 10MP 3x/67mm) – and the 50MP 120° ultra-wide (vs. 12MP 120°).

The S25 Ultra is slightly larger (77.6mm wide vs. 75.8mm) and heavier (218g vs. 190g). Unless that is a deal-breaker, we’d say that an old Ultra is better than a new Plus.

As another alternative to the S26+, there’s the Samsung Galaxy S26 FE – specifically the 8/256GB model because the base 128GB version has a pitiful discount. And yes, you do lose 4 gigs of RAM. The Exynos 2500 loses to the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 in terms of performance and efficiency.

The screens may be the same size at 6.7”, but the S26+ has a 1440p+ LTPO panel, while the FE has a 1080p+ LTPS. The Galaxy S26 FE is a solid improvement over the S25 FE, however, it doesn’t compare well against the pricier S-series models.
