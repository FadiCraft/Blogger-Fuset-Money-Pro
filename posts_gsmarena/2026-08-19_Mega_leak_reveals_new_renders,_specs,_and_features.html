Samsung's upcoming Galaxy S26 FE has already been the subject of several leaks, including official-looking renders and even a hands-on video. Now, a major leak has surfaced, revealing almost everything about the upcoming smartphone.

Android Headlines has shared 26 new marketing images of the Galaxy S26 FE, revealing the phone's key specs, features, and color options.

According to the leaked images, the Galaxy S26 FE will be available in Blueberry, Graphite, and Pistachio color options.

The handset will feature a flat display protected by Gorilla Glass Victus+, along with an aluminium frame.


For cameras, the Galaxy S26 FE will reportedly feature a vertically aligned triple rear setup comprising a 50MP main sensor, 12MP ultrawide camera, and 8MP telephoto unit. At the front, a 12MP camera will handle selfies.

The marketing images also reveal that the Galaxy S26 FE will be powered by the Exynos 2500 chipset and pack a 4,900mAh battery with support for 45W wired charging.

The phone will feature a 6.7-inch AMOLED display with Full-HD+ resolution and a 120Hz refresh rate.


The handset will reportedly carry an IP68 rating and measure 7.4mm in thickness. Samsung is also said to offer seven years of Android OS and security updates.

The Galaxy S26 FE will also reportedly come with several software features, including Horizontal Lock, My FanCam, Audio Eraser, and more.


If Samsung follows the same launch timeline as the Galaxy S25 FE, then it’s likely that the Galaxy S26 FE could be unveiled as early as next month.
