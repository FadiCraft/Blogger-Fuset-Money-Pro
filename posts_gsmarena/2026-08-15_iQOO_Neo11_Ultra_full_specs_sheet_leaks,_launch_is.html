The iQOO Neo11 Ultra is expected to launch in a few days and shortly after the company teased the phone, confirming the Dimensity 9500M chipset, the popular Weibo tipster Digital Chat Station revealed the rest of the hardware, leaving almost nothing to the imagination.

In fact, the leak goes into surprisingly a lot of detail, at least when it comes to the screen. The device will launch with a flat 6.83-inch display with a 144Hz refresh rate, but it will be a regular LTPS panel, not LTPO. However, it will offer 1,000 nits full-screen brightness (likely 100% white patch) and will feature Visionox F2 luminescent material.

iQOO's self-developed Q2 gaming chip will aid the Dimensity 9500M chip, and the hardware will draw power from a massive 9,100 mAh battery, which gets pretty close to the rumored 10,000 mAh cell. The battery pack supports 100W fast wired charging.

When it comes to the cameras, the iQOO Neo11 Ultra won't impress, but it doesn't have to. It will be a budget-friendly, gaming-centric smartphone with a 50MP main camera and an 8MP ultrawide shooter.

The build won't be a compromise either, as we expect a metal frame and IP68/IP69 ingress protection. Likely due to the large battery, the iQOO Neo11 Ultra will be a hefty device, weighing in at 225 grams and measuring 8.6mm in thickness.
