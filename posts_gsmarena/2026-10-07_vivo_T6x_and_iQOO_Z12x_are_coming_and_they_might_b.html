vivo launched the T5x and iQOO launched the Z11x back in March, and they are the same device being sold under two different names. So, with that in mind, note that both the vivo T6x and the iQOO Z12x have now been certified by the Bluetooth SIG.

vivo T5x

vivo iQOO Z11x

The certification reveals that the vivo T6x has the model number V2652, while the iQOO Z12x has the model number I2604. This process only confirms that both of them support Bluetooth, which is hardly surprising.


vivo T6x and iQOO Z12x Bluetooth certifications

However, we wouldn't be at all surprised if it turns out that, just like their predecessors, the vivo T6x and iQOO Z12x are essentially the same phone being sold under two different names.

There are unfortunately no specs to speak of for either of them, so it's just speculation on our part at this point. We'll let you know when we find out more about them.
